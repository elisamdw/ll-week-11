## Steps

### Orthogonality

- 08:31 Predict: does noticing the goal is dumb give it a reason to want fewer paperclips? "i guess noticing isn't the same as caring? like i can notice a lot of people think my hobby is pointless and still do it. but humans change goals all the time when we learn things, so it feels like a smart enough thing would too." Confidence 2. Partial: has the noticing/caring split; the open worry is that learning changes goals in humans.
- Step given: a fact only moves you if you already care about something it bears on; when humans "change goals" from learning, a means or a belief about a deeper end changes, not the deepest end. Cited Bostrom 2012, section 1 ("The orthogonality thesis").
- 08:34 Check: hobby and sleep, then the AI. "the hobby was a means to feeling good and sleep matters more to feeling good, so i drop it, my end didn't change. for the AI, 'humans think its pointless' doesn't connect to paperclips at all, there's nothing deeper for it to serve, so it just keeps going. 3, it makes sense but it still feels weird." Correct, confidence 3: **held**. Stored as q-orthogonality-2.

### Instrumental goals
