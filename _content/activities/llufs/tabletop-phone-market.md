# A paper market with prices on a phone

**Invented test game, LU’s proposal:** three sites, three resource types and one shared market. This is a small test of changing prices, not a model of the economy or a completed version of PL’s game.

1. **Make the table (5 min).** Draw three sites. Put out tokens for power, water and compute; each player starts with six paper credits. A project needs one of each resource. Write starting prices of one credit each on a sheet.
2. **Confirm the digital path (5 min).** Check PL’s Prices that move card and page, then try opening it on a phone using the host’s supplied address. Use it only if its available actions fit the exercise; otherwise one phone displays the handwritten price table while a human banker updates it. Do not imply the prototype implements these invented rules.
3. **Play three rounds (10 min).** Players take turns buying at most one token. After all have acted, the banker increases the next round’s price by one for a resource bought twice or more that round; other prices stay fixed. Record every purchase, balance and price change. Announce the change before the next purchase.
4. **Rebalance and replay (5 min).** Reset. Change only one starting price or one supply pile. Predict who might be helped; play one round and compare with the prediction.
5. **Debrief (5 min).** Distinguish the designer’s between-round experiment from price changes caused by demand during a run. What did the physical board show? What information did the phone or ledger hold?

**What it teaches:** Marlon’s Nov 14 direction separates printed values from phone-held prices. The phone can hold changeable state, while tokens make resource choices tangible. MDA helps fellows describe the behavior that emerged rather than judging the idea from its specification.

**Prepare:** check the prototype card and LAN path with PL; print a large price table. Phone access and current prototype features must be tested on the available network. No NFC is needed.

**Try it:** one LL laptop or phone, a sheet of paper, nine resource tokens and paper credits; a banker can run the entire comparison if the page is unavailable.

**What could go wrong:** the board, prices and ledger disagree. Pause, reconcile against the purchase log, and resume from the last agreed state. Do not add scarcity, bargaining and automated AI demand all at once.

*LU’s proposal, Nov 15; no activity or hardware test is reported as completed.*
