# From launchers to game mechanics

**Established in the local record:** week 10’s timeline attributes a Nov 10 recap to Marlon: the LLUFs made MediaPipe instruments, trackers and launchers, and wireless microphones were connected. This establishes a shared project precedent; it does not establish that every fellow can rebuild a tracker or that any particular project is ready to reuse. Ask for a demonstration before planning around one.

**How the fellows work:** the September setup and protocol snapshots cover AI accounts, GitHub, project branches and printing from an LL computer. The async-projects snapshot contains links, not a verified list of current assignments. Keep individual allocation with staff; use anonymous roles such as builder, player, observer and host in these cards.

**Schedule finding:** W11/TIMELINE.md lists the Wednesday Nov 18 class at 12:00–14:45 but does not list LLUF shifts. These cards are modules for staff to fit into confirmed shifts. No Tuesday rehearsal or Wednesday staffing is booked by this proposal.

**Reading bridge, from the repo’s bibliography notes:** Sicart’s mechanics are actions agents use to interact with game state; MDA separates components, their behavior during play, and the player’s experience. A tracker becomes a game mechanic when a gesture has a legible consequence in a game, rather than simply launching an effect. These are applications of the existing notes, not a fresh review of the original texts.

**LU’s proposed next move:** play a prototype before modifying it. Record one observed action, its visible consequence and the player’s interpretation. Then choose whether the next change concerns the mechanic, its feedback, its physical interface or its story, using Schell’s broader design frame.

**Prepare:** locate one demonstrable launcher, confirm a fellow’s interest, and choose a keyboard or paper fallback.

**Try it:** one laptop, one existing launcher and three paper tokens; use a gesture to claim one token, then repeat with a key.

**What could go wrong:** software that ran last week may need setup; familiarity with an instrument does not establish expertise in electronics. Marlon’s electronics-owner idea is explicitly for next term, not an assignment to a fellow this week.
