Marlon, Nov 14: "a tabletop board game version of it as well as a video game version of it," "maybe even something that has a kind of like AR mix XR kind of mechanic," and "definitely for my class. Oh my God, I totally should do that for my class."

**The idea:** three stations, one game. Paper (people enforce the rules and keep the state), screen (the code does), phone hybrid (the board is paper, the phone holds prices and the AI's turn). Tables rotate every fifteen minutes, as in week 10's stations, and then say which version made the AI feel most like a player. It shows Juul's point in the room: when the computer upholds the rules, players stop seeing them.

**What it needs:** a printable *Data Center* board and cards, and PL's prototypes for the phone layer. Probably too much to build for Wednesday; a candidate for Stafford on Nov 23 or a later LL session.
