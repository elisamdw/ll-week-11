Marlon, Nov 14: "doing some game mechanics with NFC is great, and we should try that this week," but for simpler tabletop games, not 52-card decks.

**The idea:** a corner with a USB reader and a few tagged index cards. A tap reveals a hidden card (the AI's turn), commits a move that can't be taken back, or simply writes a row; a screen beside it shows the rows piling up. It is the photo activity's opposite: instead of a camera guessing the state, every change is recorded because someone meant it.

**What it needs:** the NFC kit (a USB reader and NTAG213 stickers, on Marlon's Nov 14 task list; arrival not confirmed) and PB's [*Every tap is a row*](../../prototypes/hybrid/every-tap-is-a-row.md) (`/proto/hybrid/every-tap-is-a-row`, built Nov 15): badge and supply keys stand in for taps, "teach" maps a sticker to a chip, and the board is only the rows replayed, with a slider to unbuild it. It runs without the kit. Optional for Wednesday, as a station during a break or the remix block.
