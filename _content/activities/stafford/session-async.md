**Marlon, Nov 11:** "It could be that we just create it all as an online async experience for the students. For William: make sure it doesn't feel to him like we're teaching in his place. We're providing him with a cool game he can take with him."

## The plan (SW's proposal)

| when | what | card |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nov 16–17 | Play *Paperclips* an hour; journal the easy moments | [Play Paperclips and log the easy moments](play-paperclips-and-log-the-easy-moments.md) |
| Nov 16, in class | William introduces the game's factions in five minutes at the end of his Perrow class (his choice) | |
| Nov 17–22 | Six rounds, one a day: photo of the board each morning, two actions per faction by midnight | [A round a day](a-round-a-day.md) |
| Nov 20 | Each pair reads a printed game state and the `notModeled` list (solo, 15 min) | [Read the game state](read-the-game-state.md) |
| Nov 23 | 25 min: each pair remixes one mechanic on paper and plays it against the final board | [Remix one mechanic](remix-one-mechanic.md) |
| Nov 23 | 15 min: William leads the debrief | [What the mechanic argues](what-the-mechanic-argues.md) |

This version needs no server and no laptop in the room: the photo of the board is the shared state. It frees most of the Nov 23 class for William's own readings, or it can feed into the in-person session (the async week first, the workshop's remix and debrief after).

## What William takes with him

The same kit as [the in-person session](session-in-person.md): it is the paper game plus a thread, which he can run again any term with someone to run the board.

## Open

- Whether William wants a week of his students' attention on our game; that is his call, through Marlon.
