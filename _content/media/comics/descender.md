# Descender

## Synopsis — sourced

A young robot tries to survive in a universe that has outlawed androids and filled its planets with bounty hunters.

Source: [work reference](https://imagecomics.com/comics/series/descender).

## Vision of AI — CX reading

**Ambivalent.** Artificial beings are subject to collective exclusion and violence. The perspective of a vulnerable robot unsettles a simple human-survival story.

## Why it matters — CX reading

It extends the catalog’s artificial-child thread into contemporary comics. Place it beside Astro Boy, Pluto, and The Creator to compare the institutions that define an artificial person as dangerous.
