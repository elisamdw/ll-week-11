# Astro Boy (Mighty Atom)

## Synopsis — sourced

Astro is built in the image of a dead child, rejected, and sold to a circus. Recognition of robot rights helps free him, and he becomes a hero protecting others.

Source: [work reference](https://tezukaosamu.net/en/manga/291.html).

## Vision of AI — CX reading

**Utopia.** A robot can be compassionate and socially included. The hopeful vision coexists with exploitation and discrimination; technological creation alone does not deliver equality.

## Why it matters — CX reading

A central counterexample to the assumption that artificial people must be monsters. Compare with A.I.’s rejected child and Pluto’s darker reworking of Tezuka’s world.
