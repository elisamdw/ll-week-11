# The Private Eye

## Synopsis — sourced

After a catastrophic exposure of cloud-stored secrets, people conceal their identities. An unlicensed investigator becomes involved in a consequential case.

Source: [work reference](https://imagecomics.com/comics/series/the-private-eye).

## Vision of AI — CX reading

**Ambivalent.** This is an adjacent data-dystopia entry, not a central autonomous-AI narrative. The technological failure concerns privacy, trust, and the social consequences of accumulated information.

## Why it matters — CX reading

It widens the discussion beyond conscious machines: a system can transform society without becoming a person. That distinction should stay visible in an AI catalog.
