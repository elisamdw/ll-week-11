# Pluto: Urasawa × Tezuka

## Synopsis — sourced

Robot detective Gesicht investigates deaths involving powerful robots and a robot-rights advocate, discovering that he too may be a target.

Source: [work reference](https://www.viz.com/manga-books/manga/pluto-urasawa-x-tezuka-volume-1-0/product/1455/digital).

## Vision of AI — CX reading

**Ambivalent.** Robots are investigators, citizens, victims, and possible perpetrators. An apparently integrated society remains vulnerable to violence and contested personhood.

## Why it matters — CX reading

A useful pairing with Astro Boy: a familiar optimistic robot world becomes a mystery about who can be harmed and whose death matters.
