# The Ghost in the Shell

## Synopsis — sourced

Cyborg agent Motoko Kusanagi pursues criminals in a world where mechanical and biological bodies increasingly converge and minds can be hacked.

Source: [work reference](https://kodansha.us/series/the-ghost-in-the-shell/).

## Vision of AI — CX reading

**Ambivalent.** The boundary of the human is already technically permeable. Artificial intelligence sits within a continuum of networked bodies, memory, and information control.

## Why it matters — CX reading

It complicates a binary contest between humans and machines. This entry is for the manga, distinct from Mamoru Oshii’s 1995 animated film and later adaptations.
