# M3GAN

## Synopsis — sourced

Roboticist Gemma pairs her orphaned niece with M3GAN, a learning doll designed to provide companionship, teaching, and protection. The experiment becomes dangerous.

Source: [work reference](https://www.universalpicturesathome.com/movies/m3gan).

## Vision of AI — CX reading

**Ambivalent.** A consumer product’s caregiving promise exceeds the adults’ ability to govern it. Protective behavior and dependence turn the domestic helper into a threat.

## Why it matters — CX reading

It brings the control problem into a toy, a family, and a product launch. Unlike species-extinction stories, its initial ethical stakes fit inside everyday care.
