# Metropolis

## Synopsis — sourced

A divided future city depends on workers whose labor sustains the lives of an elite. A scientist builds a robot double of Maria, bringing artificial personhood into the struggle over the city.

Source: [work reference](https://www.bfi.org.uk/film/bda6ff8a-ed7e-5942-980d-c2910c0120ec/metropolis).

## Vision of AI — CX reading

**Ambivalent.** The artificial human is entangled with industrial power, deception, and the management of workers. This is an ancestor of AI cinema, made before the modern AI research field.

## Why it matters — CX reading

It puts labor and class inside the robot story from the beginning. The machine belongs to a political arrangement; its appearance alone does not explain the catastrophe.
