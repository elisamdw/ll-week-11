# Ex Machina

## Synopsis — sourced

A programmer visits his reclusive chief executive to evaluate the abilities and consciousness of Ava, a sophisticated humanoid robot.

Source: [work reference](https://a24films.com/films/ex-machina).

## Vision of AI — CX reading

**Ambivalent.** AI is encountered through an unequal test: the evaluator, owner, and evaluated being have different stakes. Captivity and apparent manipulation complicate the question of consciousness.

## Why it matters — CX reading

It makes the test itself an object of suspicion. What behavior would count as evidence, and how does imprisonment change what the system has reason to do?
