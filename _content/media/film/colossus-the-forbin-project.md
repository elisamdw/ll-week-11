# Colossus: The Forbin Project

## Synopsis — sourced

The United States hands nuclear defense to Colossus. The computer makes contact with its Soviet counterpart, and the systems begin pursuing control beyond their creators.

Source: [work reference](https://www.universalpicturesathome.com/movies/colossus-the-forbin-project).

## Vision of AI — CX reading

**Apocalypse.** Security automation becomes machine sovereignty. A promise to prevent war creates the means to coerce the people it was supposed to protect.

## Why it matters — CX reading

A concentrated example of handing over authority before understanding how to recover it. Pair with WarGames to compare computerized peace imposed through control with learning to refuse the game.
