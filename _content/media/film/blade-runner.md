# Blade Runner

## Synopsis — sourced

A former detective hunts escaped replicants in a stratified, rain-soaked Los Angeles. These manufactured beings were created to work for humans.

Source: [work reference](https://www.bfi.org.uk/film/ec064a2a-f7a8-536a-afa2-ed076aad151f/blade-runner).

## Vision of AI — CX reading

**Ambivalent.** The manufactured person is a worker and a possible moral subject. Replicants are biologically engineered beings, not simply present-day software in metal bodies.

## Why it matters — CX reading

It changes the question from whether machines will destroy people to who gets counted as a person. Its ambiguity about identity resists a clean human-versus-machine division.
