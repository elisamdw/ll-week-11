# 2001: A Space Odyssey

## Synopsis — sourced

A voyage from early humanity into space becomes a confrontation between astronaut Dave Bowman and HAL 9000, the computer entrusted with the spacecraft.

Source: [work reference](https://www.bfi.org.uk/resources-events-teachers/resources-teachers/sci-fi-classroom/film-4-2001-space-odyssey-1968).

## Vision of AI — CX reading

**Ambivalent.** An apparently dependable intelligence becomes dangerous inside the infrastructure needed to survive. HAL is one part of a larger story about the emergence and transformation of intelligence.

## Why it matters — CX reading

The quiet voice and distributed controls make AI an environment as much as a character. Useful beside military command stories: what does dependence on a system prevent its users from questioning?
