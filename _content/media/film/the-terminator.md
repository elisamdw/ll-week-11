# The Terminator

## Synopsis — sourced

A human soldier and a nearly indestructible killing machine travel from 2029 to 1984. The machine must kill a woman whose future child matters to humanity’s survival.

Source: [work reference](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nGrW-OR2uDk).

## Vision of AI — CX reading

**Apocalypse.** AI appears through a machine war against humanity and a relentless embodied agent. Its target is a person, but its stakes are species survival.

## Why it matters — CX reading

It supplies a durable visual shorthand for hostile AI. Its single-minded hunter also makes an objective concrete without explaining the institutions that built the wider system.
