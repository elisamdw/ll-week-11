# Her

## Synopsis — sourced

Theodore, who writes personal letters for other people, develops an intimate relationship with Samantha, an operating system voiced by Scarlett Johansson.

Source: [work reference](https://www.oscars.org/events/her-2013).

## Vision of AI — CX reading

**Ambivalent.** AI is an intimate interlocutor whose existence troubles ordinary categories of relationship. Warmth and emotional availability do not settle what mutual understanding means.

## Why it matters — CX reading

It offers a companion-centered counterpoint to the military supercomputer. The interface is a voice, making it useful for thinking about why conversational systems invite attachment.
