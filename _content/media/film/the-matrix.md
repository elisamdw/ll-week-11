# The Matrix

## Synopsis — sourced

Neo discovers that ordinary life is a simulation maintained by machines while human bodies sustain the system. Rebels offer escape into a devastated world.

Source: [work reference](https://www.bfi.org.uk/sight-and-sound/features/matrix-rubber-reality).

## Vision of AI — CX reading

**Apocalypse.** AI controls perception as well as material survival. The comfortable simulated world conceals the coercion that makes it possible.

## Why it matters — CX reading

It brings the interface itself into the conflict. For a game discussion, the rules of the visible world and the rules of the enclosing system are different layers of power.
