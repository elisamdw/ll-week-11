# A.I. Artificial Intelligence

## Synopsis — sourced

David, a robotic child programmed to love, is placed with a human family. When that arrangement fails, he travels with his toy companion Teddy to find where he belongs.

Source: [work reference](https://amblin.com/movie/a-i-artificial-intelligence/).

## Vision of AI — CX reading

**Ambivalent.** Artificial beings can be emotionally vulnerable while the society that buys them treats them as appliances. Scarce resources and controlled reproduction surround the domestic story.

## Why it matters — CX reading

The ethical pressure falls on the makers and owners of a dependent being. Programmed attachment can impose obligations on the people who requested it.
