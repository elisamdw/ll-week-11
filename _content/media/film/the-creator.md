# The Creator

## Synopsis — sourced

During a war between humans and AI, a soldier is assigned to destroy a weapon said to threaten humanity. He discovers that the weapon takes the form of an AI child.

Source: [work reference](https://www.20thcenturystudios.com/movies/the-creator).

## Vision of AI — CX reading

**Ambivalent.** The expected apocalypse narrative is complicated by an artificial person whose vulnerability challenges the human military mission.

## Why it matters — CX reading

Compare the child with David in A.I. and the hunter’s mission with The Terminator. The premise asks who defines a being as a weapon before anyone encounters it.
