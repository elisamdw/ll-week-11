# The Player of Games

## Synopsis — sourced

Gurgeh, a master player from the Culture, travels to the Empire of Azad to enter an extraordinarily consequential game.

Source: [work reference](https://www.littlebrown.co.uk/titles/iain-m-banks-3/the-player-of-games/9780356521640/).

## Vision of AI — CX reading

**Utopia.** The Culture is a human-machine society in which highly capable machine intelligence is compatible with abundance and freedom. Its encounter with another society complicates that promise.

## Why it matters — CX reading

This is both a positive AI counterexample and a direct bridge to this week’s game design. A society’s rules, hierarchy, and values can be expressed through a game.
