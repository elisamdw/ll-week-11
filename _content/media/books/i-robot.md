# I, Robot

## Synopsis — sourced

Linked robot stories, framed around robopsychologist Susan Calvin, explore machines whose built-in laws produce puzzles, apparent malfunctions, and increasingly consequential decisions.

Source: [work reference](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/5681/i-robot-by-isaac-asimov/teachers-guide/).

## Vision of AI — CX reading

**Ambivalent.** Safety rules do not make interpretation disappear. Robots may be dependable helpers while growing into guardians whose authority exceeds that of individual humans.

## Why it matters — CX reading

The stories are useful for thinking through conflicts inside rules. The collection is distinct from the 2004 film with the same title.
