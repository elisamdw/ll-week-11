# Machines Like Me

## Synopsis — sourced

In an alternative 1982 London, Charlie buys Adam, a synthetic human, and shapes his personality with Miranda. A love triangle develops.

Source: [work reference](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/607838/machines-like-me-by-ian-mcewan/).

## Vision of AI — CX reading

**Ambivalent.** A manufactured person enters intimate life as property and then exceeds the expectations of his purchasers. Ethical judgment and human attachment do not fit neatly together.

## Why it matters — CX reading

Its alternate history makes AI development a contingent social path rather than an inevitable future. Compare with companion stories set in apparently seamless technological progress.
