# The Lifecycle of Software Objects

## Synopsis — sourced

Artificial digital beings develop through sustained interaction with human trainers. Their lives depend on ongoing care and on the technological and commercial systems that host them.

Source: [work reference](https://jacobmcmurray.com/10-lifecycle-of-software-objects).

## Vision of AI — CX reading

**Ambivalent.** Intelligence grows through time, relationships, and work. Software ownership and market decisions can threaten beings whose needs outlast a product’s commercial life.

## Why it matters — CX reading

A counterweight to sudden-awakening narratives: maintaining a relationship and a platform is part of making intelligence possible. Care is an ongoing cost rather than a completed feature.
