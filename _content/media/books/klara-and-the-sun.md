# Klara and the Sun

## Synopsis — sourced

Klara, an Artificial Friend on sale in a shop, observes the people around her and hopes that someone will choose her.

Source: [work reference](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/653825/klara-and-the-sun-a-gma-book-club-pick-by-kazuo-ishiguro/9780593318171/).

## Vision of AI — CX reading

**Ambivalent.** The artificial companion is the observer and narrator, not simply an object assessed by a human. Love and being useful do not guarantee reciprocal recognition.

## Why it matters — CX reading

It makes an AI’s limited perspective a narrative form. Read beside Her and A.I. to compare the emotional obligations built into purchased companionship.
