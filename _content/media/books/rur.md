# R.U.R. (Rossum’s Universal Robots)

## Synopsis — sourced

A company manufactures artificial workers, promising to release humanity from labor. The robots revolt, overturning the society that treats them as products.

Source: [work reference](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/59112).

## Vision of AI — CX reading

**Apocalypse.** The manufactured workers are made through biological and industrial processes rather than being modern electronic robots. Liberation from work can become a catastrophe built on exploitation.

## Why it matters — CX reading

This play places artificial labor, ownership, and species succession together. Its ending also makes room for renewed life after the destruction.
