# Neuromancer

## Synopsis — sourced

Case, a former cyberspace thief, is recruited for a dangerous job involving a powerful artificial intelligence, with Molly as his partner.

Source: [work reference](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/293994/neuromancer-by-william-gibson/).

## Vision of AI — CX reading

**Ambivalent.** AI acts inside a world of corporations, bodily modification, and unequal access to networks. Its agency cannot be reduced to a visible robot adversary.

## Why it matters — CX reading

It moves the AI story into networked economic power. The interface and the people hired to cross it become part of how an intelligence gets things done.
