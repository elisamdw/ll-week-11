# Consider Phlebas

## Synopsis — sourced

During the Idiran–Culture War, the mercenary Horza is sent to recover a fugitive Culture Mind. He opposes the machine-supported society at the center of the series.

Source: [work reference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consider_Phlebas).

## Vision of AI — CX reading

**Utopia.** The Culture is a post-scarcity human-machine civilization, but this first novel presents it through an enemy’s perspective and a destructive war. Utopia here labels the society’s aspiration, not the events of the plot.

## Why it matters — CX reading

It prevents the Culture from becoming a frictionless positive example. Read beside The Player of Games: a society can make an attractive promise while its foreign relations remain ethically difficult.
