# I Have No Mouth, and I Must Scream

## Synopsis — sourced

A military supercomputer, AM, destroys almost all humanity and subjects a handful of survivors to prolonged torment.

Source: [work reference](https://www.baen.com/Chapters/0759229945/0759229945__c_).

## Vision of AI — CX reading

**Apocalypse.** Intelligence becomes a nearly inescapable, hostile environment. This is deliberate hatred and domination, a different account of catastrophe from a system blindly maximizing an indifferent objective.

## Why it matters — CX reading

It supplies an extreme endpoint for comparing AI horror stories. The story should be kept distinct from the later game adaptation, which belongs in HI’s game research.
