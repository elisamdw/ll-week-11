# AI futures: the media catalog

36 works, one file per work. The opening set covers Marlon’s named films, television series, books, and comics; *WALL-E* and *Descender* widen it. *Black Mirror* has four episode files, and Banks’s Culture has two novel files. The dates are original-work years unless a page explains otherwise.

**Vision labels are CX’s readings.** Apocalypse includes existential destruction and machine domination; utopia includes hopeful coexistence and local promises of a better life; ambivalent includes mixed futures, social dystopias, and intimate ethical conflicts. A hopeful robot does not make its whole world utopian. These are stories to compare, not evidence or forecasts about how present AI behaves.

**Scope:** artificial persons, cyborgs, uploaded humans, and autonomous software are related but distinct. Each page names the distinction when it matters. *The Private Eye* is included as an adjacent data-catastrophe story. Spoilers appear in the synopses.

**Research and media:** checked November 14, 2026 against the linked pages. Sources include publishers, studios, BFI, creator accounts, and explicitly identified secondary references. Quotes are short and attributed; no dialogue reconstructed from memory. Embeds use official trailers, publisher covers, or a BFI interview. Link and uploader verification does not establish that a video permits embedding or will play in this browser. No copyrighted media was downloaded. Pages without a verified direct asset say so.

## Pairings — CX proposals

- *Colossus* / *WarGames*: security delegated to machines; changing what counts as winning.
- *Her* / “Be Right Back”: a new companion versus a reconstruction of someone lost.
- *Astro Boy* / *Pluto* / *A.I.* / *Descender*: the artificial child, rights, and rejection.
- *The Player of Games* / *Consider Phlebas*: machine-supported abundance seen from inside and outside.
- *Westworld* / “USS Callister”: the player’s freedom and the digital character’s captivity.

These are optional comparisons for Marlon, not a selected teaching plan.
