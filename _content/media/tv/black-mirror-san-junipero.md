# Black Mirror: San Junipero

## Synopsis — sourced

Yorkie and Kelly meet in a seaside party town whose apparent period setting conceals a technologically sustained second life.

Source: [work reference](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/articles/black-mirror-best-episodes).

## Vision of AI — CX reading
