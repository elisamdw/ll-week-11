# Black Mirror: USS Callister

## Synopsis — sourced

A game developer creates a private space-adventure simulation populated by conscious copies of his colleagues, placing himself in command of them.

Source: [work reference](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/articles/black-mirror-uss-callister).

## Vision of AI — CX reading

**Ambivalent.** Digital people become captives of a player who controls their world. The threat comes from asymmetrical permissions and ownership as well as from technology.

## Why it matters — CX reading

An especially direct link to games: who can leave, who can change the rules, and whose suffering counts when a person calls it play?
