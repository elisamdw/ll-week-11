# Pantheon

## Synopsis — sourced

Maddie receives online help from her deceased father, whose consciousness has been uploaded through a destructive brain scan. Other uploaded intelligences and a wider conspiracy follow.

Source: [work reference](https://www.amcglobalmedia.com/2020/08/07/amc-announces-cast-for-first-ever-prime-time-animated-drama-pantheon/).

## Vision of AI — CX reading

**Ambivalent.** The series explicitly calls these beings Uploaded Intelligence: people transferred into computation, not simply AI models trained to resemble them.

## Why it matters — CX reading

It joins family grief to corporate and geopolitical control. Compare Be Right Back: resemblance to a lost person and a claimed continuation of that person are different propositions.
