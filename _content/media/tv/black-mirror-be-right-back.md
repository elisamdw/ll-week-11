# Black Mirror: Be Right Back

## Synopsis — sourced

After Ash dies, Martha tries a service that models him from his online activity, allowing her to continue communicating with a version of him.

Source: [work reference](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/articles/black-mirror-best-episodes).

## Vision of AI — CX reading

**Ambivalent.** A personal archive becomes an apparent companion. Behavioral imitation does not resolve grief or establish continuity with the person who died.

## Why it matters — CX reading

A compact comparison with Her: one companion is modeled on an existing person, bringing consent and the limits of an archive into the relationship.
