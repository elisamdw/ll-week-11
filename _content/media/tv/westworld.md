# Westworld

## Synopsis — sourced

An adult theme park offers adventures populated by artificial hosts. Their growing consciousness turns the manufactured entertainment world into a conflict over freedom.

Source: [work reference](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JctIuZfSsa4).

## Vision of AI — CX reading

**Ambivalent.** Artificial people are owned as entertainment, with their experiences subordinated to visitors’ desires. Rebellion can be read from the viewpoint of those treated as game pieces.

## Why it matters — CX reading

It asks what a player’s freedom costs a simulated character. The television series is distinct from Crichton’s 1973 film.
