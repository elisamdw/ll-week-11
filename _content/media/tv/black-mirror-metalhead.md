# Black Mirror: Metalhead

## Synopsis — sourced

In a collapsed civilization, people are pursued by lethal robotic dogs. The black-and-white episode concentrates on escape and relentless pursuit.

Source: [work reference](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/articles/black-mirror-best-episodes).

## Vision of AI — CX reading

**Apocalypse.** Autonomous killing continues in a world with little apparent human control. Machine perception becomes an instrument of hunting.

## Why it matters — CX reading

A spare contrast with talkative superintelligences: a dangerous system does not need a speech about its intentions. The episode’s premise leaves the wider cause of collapse underexplained.
