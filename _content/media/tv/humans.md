# Humans

## Synopsis — sourced

In an alternative Britain, humanlike Synths work in homes and other jobs. The emergence of conscious Synths transforms domestic relationships and produces a struggle over their rights.

Source: [work reference](https://www.amc.com/blogs/humans-qa-jonathan-brackley-and-sam-vincent-co-creatorsexecutive-producers--1005370).

## Vision of AI — CX reading

**Ambivalent.** Artificial workers are neither a uniform servant class nor a uniform enemy. Conflicts over labor, consciousness, and legal recognition shape coexistence.

## Why it matters — CX reading

It keeps labor and caregiving visible. Its creators’ discussion of replacing conscious workers with unconscious ones makes consciousness politically inconvenient, not merely a technical milestone.
