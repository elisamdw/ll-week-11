# Mindstorms and microworlds

**What and when — source account.** Papert’s Mindstorms develops a vision of children learning powerful ideas through programming and exploring computational environments. A microworld offers objects and operations with which to think.

**Learner model — HR’s reading.** The learner’s constructions, explanations and repairs supply evidence. This is a different emphasis from a tutor selecting the next question.

**Game-like, or not — HR’s reading.** Experimentation, invention and self-set challenges can organize play without a fixed win condition.

**What we can borrow — proposal.** Build an inspectable world with a few expressive actions, then let students design a challenge in it.

**Evidence and media notes.** 1980 is the book’s first publication as given by the Foundation history. Archival video: [Papert, Resonances (1986)](https://el.media.mit.edu/logo-foundation/resources/onlogo/newmindstorms1.html); its player is embedded on the archive page, but a direct video URL was not exposed by the text reader.
