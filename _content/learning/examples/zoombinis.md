# Logical Journey of the Zoombinis

**What and when — source account.** A 1996 logical-thinking adventure in which groups of distinct creatures must reach safety. TERC and partners relaunched it in 2015.

**Learner model — HR’s reading.** Players expose their hypotheses by classifying, arranging and testing creatures. The original game’s challenges should not be confused with later research analytics.

**Game-like, or not — HR’s reading.** Discovering the logic is how the player advances; the mathematics is in the action.

**What we can borrow — proposal.** Build puzzles that require a hypothesis and a test, then ask the learner to explain the inferred relation.
