# The Young Lady’s Illustrated Primer

**What and when — source account.** A fictional interactive book in The Diamond Age, first published in 1995. Nell’s education unfolds through a personalized guide; the engineer Hackworth’s purposes also shape it.

**Learner model — HR’s reading.** In the fiction, the teaching responds to a child and her circumstances. This is a narrative premise, not a validated learner model.

**Game-like, or not — HR’s reading.** Story, characters and challenges make education part of an unfolding life.

**What we can borrow — proposal.** Ask who authors the tutor’s values and who supplies the care behind its voice.
