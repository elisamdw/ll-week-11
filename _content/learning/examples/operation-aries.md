# Operation ARIES!

**What and when — source account.** A scientific-inquiry game in which players evaluate research while investigating alien infiltrators. Its creators describe animated tutor and peer agents, cases and interrogation tasks.

**Learner model — HR’s reading.** Assessment performance selects among tutorial dialogue formats. The creator description calls these performance-adapted trialogues.

**Game-like, or not — HR’s reading.** An unfolding spy story gives evaluating evidence a fictional purpose; learners may also turn off the storyline.

**What we can borrow — proposal.** Make questioning an action required to solve the mystery, and allow different levels of narrative involvement.

**Evidence and media notes.** Creator overview read 2026-11-15. The 2011 year marks a published chapter listed by IES; the creator overview itself is undated. No verified media embed. This is an AI tutoring game from before the LLM era.
