# PLATO

**What and when — source account.** A networked computer education system begun at Illinois in 1960. Tenczar’s authoring language supported lessons and answer judging; a wider social and game culture grew around the terminals.

**Learner model — HR’s reading.** Lesson authors could judge answers and route practice. PLATO was a platform with many lessons, not one uniform learner model.

**Game-like, or not — HR’s reading.** Tutorials coexisted with multiplayer games, messaging and creative work. The community exceeded the instructional program.

**What we can borrow — proposal.** Let learners author as well as answer; distinguish the platform’s record from each activity’s judgment.
