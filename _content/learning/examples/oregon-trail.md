# The Oregon Trail

**What and when — source account.** Created in 1971 for Minnesota history students, then distributed through MECC. Players manage a settler journey, supplies, pace and dangerous events.

**Learner model — HR’s reading.** The central model is the journey’s changing state. In this account, consequences teach through play rather than through an explicit mastery estimate.

**Game-like, or not — HR’s reading.** Resource management, risk and survival give decisions stakes.

**What we can borrow — proposal.** Let a decision change persistent world state, then discuss whose historical perspective and options the simulation leaves out.
