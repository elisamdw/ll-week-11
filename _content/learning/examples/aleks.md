# ALEKS

**What and when — source account.** A knowledge-space-based assessment and learning system. Its company history dates the UCI licensing agreement to 1997; this is not a claim that development began that year.

**Learner model — HR’s reading.** Adaptive questions locate a learner among feasible knowledge states and identify topics ready to learn.

**Game-like, or not — HR’s reading.** A visible knowledge structure and progress can motivate; the core is adaptive assessment rather than a game world.

**What we can borrow — proposal.** Expose the next learnable frontier while retaining a way to revisit prerequisites.
