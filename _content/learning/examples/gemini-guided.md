# Gemini Guided Learning

**What and when — source account.** Announced August 6, 2025 as a Gemini learning experience combining questions, step-by-step support and rich responses, drawing on LearnLM capabilities.

**Learner model — HR’s reading.** Prompts and learner responses guide the conversation. The announcement does not document an exportable longitudinal learner graph.

**Game-like, or not — HR’s reading.** Quizzes and multimodal explanations can add interaction; educational intent alone does not make a game.

**What we can borrow — proposal.** Offer different representations of the same idea and ask the learner to apply it after the explanation.
