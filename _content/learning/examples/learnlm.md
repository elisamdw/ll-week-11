# LearnLM

**What and when — source account.** A family of learning-oriented models and capabilities introduced in 2024. Google announced their integration into Gemini 2.5 in May 2025.

**Learner model — HR’s reading.** Training for pedagogical behavior is a property of a model; a durable record of an individual learner requires an application around it.

**Game-like, or not — HR’s reading.** LearnLM is not itself a game. An application can use its questioning and feedback within one.

**What we can borrow — proposal.** Separate the model, teaching policy, subject knowledge and learner record in our design.

**Evidence and media notes.** Official announcement and research abstract read 2026-11-15. Vendor preference evaluations are not treated here as proof of durable learning.
