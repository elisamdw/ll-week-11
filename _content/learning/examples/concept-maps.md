# Novak’s concept maps

**What and when — source account.** Developed in 1972 to represent changes in children’s science understanding. Labeled links join concepts into propositions; cross-links connect areas of a map.

**Learner model — HR’s reading.** The map is an external representation of conceptual relationships that can be compared and revised. A concept map is not inherently a prerequisite graph.

**Game-like, or not — HR’s reading.** Making and repairing connections can be a puzzle; scores and progression are optional additions.

**What we can borrow — proposal.** Require meaningful link labels and source evidence. Let a learner dispute a connection.
