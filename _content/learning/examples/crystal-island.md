# Crystal Island

**What and when — source account.** A narrative-centered science-learning environment in which players investigate an outbreak. The official project describes Bayesian pedagogical agents; a 2010 paper reports a preliminary user-knowledge model.

**Learner model — HR’s reading.** The research model updates probabilistic beliefs about narrative, strategic, solution and curricular knowledge from interaction evidence.

**Game-like, or not — HR’s reading.** A mystery gives information seeking a purpose. Characters and situated advice connect instructional support to the world.

**What we can borrow — proposal.** Let a world action provide evidence about understanding, while keeping inferred knowledge uncertain and revisable.
