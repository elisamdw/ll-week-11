# Anki

**What and when — source account.** An open-source flashcard environment for active recall and spaced review. Its current manual describes multimedia cards and scheduling; its official forum celebrated its eighteenth birthday in October 2024, with a reply from creator Damien Elmes (dae), supporting a 2006 origin.

**Learner model — HR’s reading.** Review history and recall ratings inform a card’s future schedule. A collection of cards is not automatically a map of prerequisites.

**Game-like, or not — HR’s reading.** A review queue and statistics support routine. Streaks or competitive elements are optional layers.

**What we can borrow — proposal.** Keep practice items editable and portable, and distinguish a review rating from proof of transferable understanding.

**Evidence and media notes.** Manual read 2026-11-15. The 2006 year is inferred from that 2024 eighteenth-birthday thread and the creator’s anniversary reply; the original 2011 origin post was not recovered. No verified media embed.
