# Duolingo’s gamification

**What and when — source account.** A language-learning product launched in 2012. Its official technology account describes Birdbrain estimating learner proficiency and exercise difficulty.

**Learner model — HR’s reading.** Personalization of exercise difficulty is distinct from a streak’s count of consecutive days with a lesson.

**Game-like, or not — HR’s reading.** Streaks, points and progression encourage returning. Returning is behavior; it is not itself evidence of language mastery.

**What we can borrow — proposal.** Show the difference between participation and demonstrated skill in both the data and the interface.
