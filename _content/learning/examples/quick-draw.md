# Quick, Draw!

**What and when — source account.** A 2016 drawing game: the player sketches a prompt and a neural network tries to recognize it before time expires.

**Learner model — HR’s reading.** The visible model classifies drawings, not the learner’s knowledge. The site also collects drawings for a public dataset.

**Game-like, or not — HR’s reading.** Twenty-second rounds, changing guesses and near misses make model behavior observable.

**What we can borrow — proposal.** Use a mistaken classification as the start of a discussion about representations and training data; do not equate recognition with good drawing.
