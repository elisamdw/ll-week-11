# Betty’s Brain

**What and when — source account.** A learning-by-teaching environment documented by 2001. Learners construct causal maps that Betty uses to answer science questions and explain chains of inference.

**Learner model — HR’s reading.** The learner-authored map is executable knowledge. A 2012 design study also examines conversational feedback and learners’ choices.

**Game-like, or not — HR’s reading.** Responsibility for an agent’s performance gives the learner a role; the agent is the pupil.

**What we can borrow — proposal.** Let learners teach a character, inspect its reasoning, and repair the representation when it fails.

**Evidence and media notes.** 2001 is an early publication marker, not a verified first-release date. Figure 1 in the 2012 paper shows the environment; no standalone media URL verified.
