# Logo

**What and when — source account.** A language and learning environment developed at BBN in the 1960s, with school trials in 1967; later work made turtle geometry especially recognizable.

**Learner model — HR’s reading.** Knowledge becomes visible in a learner’s program and debugging. The environment need not assign a hidden mastery score.

**Game-like, or not — HR’s reading.** A programmable turtle supports making, testing and revising. Projects can become games, but Logo itself is a medium.

**What we can borrow — proposal.** Give the learner a small world whose behavior they can change and explain.

**Evidence and media notes.** The participant-authored 2020 history dates invention to 1966; the Foundation calls 1967 the first version. Media: the Foundation’s On Logo archive, linked in the Mindstorms card.
