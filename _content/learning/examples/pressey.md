# Pressey’s teaching machine

**What and when — source account.** A mechanical multiple-choice question device from Ohio State; the Smithsonian dates its example to 1925. It presented questions on a drum and rewarded correct replies.

**Learner model — HR’s reading.** A record of responses and progress through questions, rather than an explanation of the learner’s concepts.

**Game-like, or not — HR’s reading.** Buttons, immediate consequences and a reward mechanism make answering into a repeated action; this is not a simulated world.

**What we can borrow — proposal.** Make the feedback loop visible: prompt → action → consequence. Decide what a correct button press can establish.
