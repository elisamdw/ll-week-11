# AI for Oceans

**What and when — source account.** Code.org’s 2019 activity has learners label fish and trash, train a classifier, and test its behavior. Later examples introduce subjective labels and biased data.

**Learner model — HR’s reading.** The model learns from labeled examples; the learner’s understanding appears in the choices and explanations around training and testing.

**Game-like, or not — HR’s reading.** Cleaning an ocean supplies a goal and visible consequences. Deliberately bad data can reveal the system’s dependency on its examples.

**What we can borrow — proposal.** Let students change the training set and predict the consequence before testing.

**Evidence and media notes.** Official creator article read 2026-11-15, including activity screenshots. This contains AI and teaches AI; it is not an adaptive tutor.
