# Skinner’s teaching machines

**What and when — source account.** Skinner’s 1958 Science article explains devices for self-instruction: learners construct responses in small steps and compare them with the program’s answer.

**Learner model — HR’s reading.** The authored sequence and observed response determine the next step; an individual can proceed at an individual pace.

**Game-like, or not — HR’s reading.** Frequent success and immediate confirmation organize the interaction. A reward loop alone does not make it a game.

**What we can borrow — proposal.** Ask for a response before revealing an explanation; keep steps small enough to diagnose an error.

**Evidence and media notes.** Date here is the article, not a claim that machines first appeared in 1958. Archival diagrams and photographs are in the linked PDF; no separate image URL verified.
