# Leitner’s card boxes

**What and when — source account.** A practical paper-card system published in 1972: successful cards move through boxes associated with less frequent review, while difficult cards receive more attention.

**Learner model — HR’s reading.** The card’s box position summarizes recall history. It does not represent why an answer was misunderstood.

**Game-like, or not — HR’s reading.** Movement between boxes gives tangible progress without needing a story or points.

**What we can borrow — proposal.** Make a review queue visible and explain why an item is due.

**Evidence and media notes.** Historical account is by a later spaced-repetition developer, not an independently opened copy of Leitner’s book. No verified media embed.
