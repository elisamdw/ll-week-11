**What happens (8 min)**

1. **The folder (3 min).** On the projector: a harness folder in Claude Desktop's Code tab. Ask Claude one practice question; answer it; open `graph/attempts.json` and show the answer in the file.
2. **The game (3 min).** `/learn` on the same folder: the map, a node unlocking, the progress bar. "Everything on this map came from that conversation."
3. **The take-home (2 min).** Over Thanksgiving: set up your own folder ([setup](setup.md)), learn one thing your Final Project needs, play it as a game, and try one remix. On Dec 2, show your map.

**Why here:** the closer asks students to take one mechanic into their Final Project; *unlocking by explaining* is a mechanic, and the harness is a tool they can use for their project's research.
