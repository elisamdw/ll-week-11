# What runs through the learning machines

*HR’s synthesis and design proposals, grounded in the linked example cards; not Marlon’s words. Source pages were read November 15, 2026. This is a comparison of mechanisms, not a comparative effectiveness review.*

## The learner model: what is the machine entitled to infer?

[Pressey’s buttons](../examples/pressey.md) register answers; [knowledge tracing](../examples/cognitive-tutors.md) estimates component learning; [concept maps](../examples/concept-maps.md) externalize relationships. These are different kinds of evidence. [NotebookLM’s source collection](../examples/notebooklm.md) describes what material is available, while [LearnHarness’s proposition graph](../examples/learnharness-blomqvist.md) preserves ideas and reasoning. Neither should automatically be read as a calibrated measure of an individual’s ability.

For the folder harness, the important question is what connects an attempt to a claim about understanding. Preserve the question, response, help supplied and later revision. An inference such as “ready for this next idea” should remain revisable. The learner should be able to inspect the evidence and disagree with the interpretation.

## Mastery and prerequisites: who chooses the destination?

[ALEKS](../examples/aleks.md) organizes what someone is ready to learn; [Khan Academy](../examples/khan-mastery.md) makes skill progression visible. [Logo and microworlds](../examples/mindstorms-microworlds.md) place more of the initiative in choosing a construction. The distinction matters for a class in which students design the environment itself. A prerequisite path can support a learner’s goal, but it can also make the system’s curriculum the only imaginable route.

Read this beside [Nguyen’s account of games as an art of agency](../../bibliography/nguyen-2020-games-agency-as-art.md): the environment gives someone a way of acting and something to care about. Our proposal is to make a suggested next step visible without making every neighboring experiment inaccessible. Choosing a goal is part of learning, too.

## Feedback loops: what changes after an action?

[Skinner’s small steps](../examples/skinner.md), [Rocky’s Boots’ working circuits](../examples/rockys-boots.md), and [Zoombinis’ hypothesis tests](../examples/zoombinis.md) all return consequences, but consequences teach different things. A correctness signal says whether an answer matches; a failing construction can expose a relation the learner has not understood. A tutor’s question can ask the learner to explain that failure.

[Sicart’s mechanics](../../bibliography/sicart-2008-defining-game-mechanics.md) directs attention to actions; [Bogost’s procedural rhetoric](../../bibliography/bogost-2007-persuasive-games.md) asks what a system argues through its processes. For our draft, ask the learner to predict, act, inspect the consequence, explain and try again. Reward the target intellectual action when possible. A [Duolingo streak](../examples/duolingo.md) records returning; it should occupy a different field from evidence that a concept transfers. [Bogost’s gamification critique](../../bibliography/bogost-2011-gamification-is-bullshit.md) helps keep that difference in view.

## The map of knowledge: several maps, several jobs

A [Novak concept map](../examples/concept-maps.md) links concepts into meaningful propositions. A prerequisite graph says one item is needed for another. A [spaced-review queue](../examples/anki.md) says when to revisit an item. A game map says where actions can happen. These can share nodes without becoming the same structure.

[Manovich’s database and algorithm](../../bibliography/manovich-1999-database-as-symbolic-form.md) offers a way to examine what the harness stores and what it does with the stored material. Our proposal is to label the edges: “supports,” “requires,” “contradicts” and “was demonstrated in” should not all become an unlabeled line. A graph can then become a playable interface while its claims remain inspectable.

## The tutor as character: care, authority and role reversal

The fictional [Primer](../examples/diamond-age-primer.md) makes education into a relationship and story. [Operation ARIES!](../examples/operation-aries.md) makes scientific evaluation part of investigating a mystery; [Crystal Island](../examples/crystal-island.md) situates support inside inquiry. [Betty’s Brain](../examples/bettys-brain.md) reverses the usual relation: the learner teaches the agent. A modern conversational voice inherits possibilities for encouragement and authority without automatically earning either.

[Murray’s account of digital narrative](../../bibliography/murray-1997-hamlet-on-the-holodeck.md) helps frame the appeal of an inhabited story; [Sicart on play](../../bibliography/sicart-2014-play-matters.md) helps leave room for what players do with it. Our proposal is a character whose role and limits are legible. It can ask for reasons, expose its own uncertainty and invite correction. A friendly voice should make explanation easier, while the evidence behind its judgments stays available outside the performance.
