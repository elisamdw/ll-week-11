Fiducial markers (ArUco or AprilTag squares) are easier to track than a picture: four on the corners give the board's plane, and virtual pieces can sit on exact squares. Week 10's research calls a QR code or ArUco marker "the most robust option"; whether a given JavaScript marker library runs well on an iPhone is not checked yet.

**Try it this week:** Print four markers on a Risk board's corners; find one JavaScript marker library that runs on an iPhone.

**Draws on:** week 10 research (markers as the robust option; no WebXR on iOS Safari).
