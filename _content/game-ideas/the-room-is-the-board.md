The projector, the network rack, the water fountain, the light board: each gets a tag and becomes a place on the map. To act on a place you have to go there. The AI's spread is shown on a screen, but the humans' moves happen by walking the room.

**Try it this week:** Tag six things in the LL and play one round of Data Center on foot.
