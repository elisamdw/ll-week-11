Simultaneous secret orders, as in *Diplomacy*: each faction puts its order token on its own reader under the table, and the phone reveals everyone's choice together. Placing is committing; there's no take-back once the reader has seen it.

**Try it this week:** Two readers, four tokens, one round of the Data Center game played with committed orders.

**Draws on:** Mechanics: simultaneous action selection, alliance and betrayal.
