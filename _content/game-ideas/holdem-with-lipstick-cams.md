Marlon: "let's just go with lipstick cams." Broadcast poker turned hidden information into spectator information. An LL table with small USB cameras at each seat sends the hands to a screen the players can't see. Who is the game for: the players or the audience?

**Try it this week:** One table, two cameras, one round, an audience screen in another room.

**Draws on:** Marlon's direction; T.L. Taylor, *Watch Me Play*; mechanics: the spect-actor.
