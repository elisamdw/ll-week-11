Each tap writes one row: who, what, where, when. After the game the class looks at the table instead of the board and reads the game from its data. What does the log show that the players didn't notice?

**Try it this week:** Log taps to a CSV during one round; print it next to the overhead photo of the same moment.

**Draws on:** Manovich, "Database as Symbolic Form"; HI's games-as-data pages.
