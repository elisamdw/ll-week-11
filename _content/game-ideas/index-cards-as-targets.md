MindAR can track several images at once and compiles targets in the browser. Each September 28 card becomes a target, and the phone shows that place's stats and owner floating above it. Plain line-art cards track badly, so add a printed pattern to each.

**Try it this week:** Compile three cards in MindAR's online compiler and test multi-tracking.

**Draws on:** Week 10 research (MindAR multi-track, target quality); template and instance.
