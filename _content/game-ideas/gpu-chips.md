Toys-to-life, as with amiibo and Skylanders: a physical token that the game reads. Selling a chip to the AI is a physical act with a price that changes as the AI buys; the company faction feels the trade in its hands.

**Try it this week:** A handful of tagged tokens and a reader labeled "the AI"; the price on screen rises with each one sold.

**Draws on:** Crawford, *Atlas of AI* (what AI consumes); mechanics: trading, dynamic prices.
