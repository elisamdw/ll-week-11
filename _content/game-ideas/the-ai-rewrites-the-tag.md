NFC tags can be rewritten. When the AI takes a place, the game writes the AI's mark into that place's tag. Tap it later and you get the AI's version of the place. A physical object whose data has been taken over.

**Try it this week:** Rewrite one NTAG213 sticker from a phone and read it back on another.

**Draws on:** Chun, *Control and Freedom*; Bostrom's infrastructure profusion.
