Take the LL's Monopoly set, keep the board, and move the rents to a phone that raises them as more players land on a color. A small economy in a game everyone knows, and a way to see the difference between a printed rule and a mechanic that responds.

**Try it this week:** Twenty minutes of Monopoly with rents on a shared screen.

**Draws on:** Marlon on Monopoly's printed prices (voice chat); TH's rules-versus-mechanics handout.
