Hand tracking in the browser (MediaPipe, which the LL already uses in its web prototypes) reads gestures from the phone or a laptop camera over the table. The body does the mechanic.

**Try it this week:** A laptop camera over the board, MediaPipe hands, and one gesture mapped to one action.

**Draws on:** LL precedent: the web prototypes with MediaPipe; mechanics: gesture.
