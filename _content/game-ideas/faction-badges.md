Identity tokens: tap your badge, then a place, and the game knows who did what. Private faction goals live on each player's phone, so the table stays public and the goals stay private.

**Try it this week:** Four badges, one reader, and a page that shows each faction's goal only after its badge is tapped.

**Draws on:** Nguyen on value capture (private metrics); mechanics: hidden role, roles.
