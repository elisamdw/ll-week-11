App-driven board games such as *Mansions of Madness* (2nd edition) and *Chronicles of Crime* let an app run the opponent. Here the phone draws the AI's card, decides where it spreads, and tells the table which pieces to place. The AI is a system no player controls.

**Try it this week:** Run the existing /game engine as the AI for a paper map; players move paper data centers when the phone says.

**Draws on:** Voice chat notes (precedents); Bogost: what the machine does also argues.
