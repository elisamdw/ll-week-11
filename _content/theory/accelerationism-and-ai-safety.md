# Accelerationism and AI safety

A cluster of short public texts from 2022–2024, mostly blog posts, open letters, and manifestos, that set up the two camps week 12 is named for ("doomerism and accelerationists"); each can be read in 10–40 minutes, and all are free online.

## The argument

**Accelerationists** hold that technological growth is the engine of human welfare and that slowing AI costs lives. **Effective accelerationism (e/acc)** began as a pseudonymous Substack and X movement in 2022; its founding notes, by "Beff Jezos" (revealed by *Forbes* in December 2023 to be the physicist Guillaume Verdon) and "bayes," ground it in thermodynamics: the universe favors matter that captures more free energy, so the task is to accelerate toward a "technocapital singularity." Marc Andreessen gave the mood a mainstream text in "Why AI Will Save the World" (June 6, 2023), which calls fear of AI killing us "a profound category error," and "The Techno-Optimist Manifesto" (October 16, 2023), which names "deceleration" as the enemy and lists @BasedBeffJezos among its "Patron Saints." **AI-safety** framings answer that the race itself is the danger: the Future of Life Institute's open letter "Pause Giant AI Experiments" (March 22, 2023) asked labs to pause training above GPT-4 for six months; the Center for AI Safety's one-sentence "Statement on AI Risk" (May 2023) put extinction alongside pandemics and nuclear war, signed by Hinton, Bengio, Hassabis, Altman, and Amodei. The community's shorthand is **p(doom)**, a person's stated probability of AI-caused catastrophe, and "doomer" is the accelerationists' word for the other side. Leopold Aschenbrenner's "Situational Awareness" (June 2024) fused the two: it accepts the risks but recasts AI as a **national-security race** with China, ending in a government "Project." The word itself is older: **Nick Land**, with the 1990s Cybernetic Culture Research Unit at Warwick, theorized capitalism as an autonomous, self-accelerating machine, and Andreessen borrows Land's phrase "the techno-capital machine."

## Read this

- Andreessen, "The Techno-Optimist Manifesto" (a16z.com, about 6,500 words, 30 minutes): skim the "We believe" lists and read the "Energy" and "The Enemy" sections, the clearest statement of growth-through-energy.
- The FLI letter and the CAIS statement side by side (10 minutes total), then the e/acc founding notes (Substack, July 10, 2022, 10 minutes).
- "Situational Awareness," the introduction and "IIIa. Racing to the Trillion-Dollar Cluster" (situational-awareness.ai, 40 minutes): the most concrete text on the infrastructure itself: power contracts, "every voltage transformer that can possibly be procured," and trillion-dollar clusters.

## Critiques and conversations

- The DAIR statement by the "Stochastic Parrots" authors (March 31, 2023) attacked the FLI letter from the other side, as longtermist "fearmongering and AI hype" ([TESCREAL](tescreal.md)); Torres also places e/acc inside the TESCREAL bundle.
- [Yudkowsky](yudkowsky-soares-if-anyone-builds-it.md) argued in TIME that the FLI pause was too little: "Shut it all down." [Amodei](amodei-machines-of-loving-grace.md) (a CAIS signatory) calls stopping "untenable" and warns against "doomerism."
- [Narayanan and Kapoor](narayanan-kapoor-ai-snake-oil.md) reject both camps' shared premise that AI is a separate, superintelligent agent. [AI 2027](ai-2027.md) dramatizes Aschenbrenner's US–China race. The line from accelerating returns runs back to [Kurzweil](kurzweil-singularity.md), whose "Law of Accelerating Returns" Andreessen's manifesto invokes.

## For the game (TH proposal)

- (B, C) **Two human factions with opposite scoring:** an Accelerationist faction scores for total energy produced and compute online (it wins alongside the AI up to a point); a Safety faction scores for nodes kept under monitoring; both share the loss condition if the AI takes the map.
- (A) **Pause letter as a committee action:** "Pause for six rounds" is always available, but it only holds if every lab on the table signs; one defector voids it, as with the FLI letter.
- (B) **p(doom) as a public dial:** each player sets a secret p(doom) at the start and may change it once per round; the regulator's powers scale with the table's average, so persuasion is a mechanic.
- (B, C) **National race variant (Aschenbrenner):** two rival maps; the side that falls behind loses a node per round, so slowing down for safety has a visible cost.
