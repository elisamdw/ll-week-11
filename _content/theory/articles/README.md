# Articles: stored, with segments

Marlon, Nov 14: "I also want to find some actual articles and store them and pull segments. like the two in here."

Each article is a pair: the PDF, and a Markdown page beside it with what it is, about 8–13 **segments** (verbatim passages with page numbers as printed), a labeled **For the game (TH proposal)**, and links to the thinker's entry in [theory](../README.md). Open the PDF from the link at the top of each page.

What gets stored: papers Marlon adds, and papers that are openly available (author's site, open-access journal, publisher's open copy, arXiv, government archive). Where the only free copy is not legitimate, the page has segments and a link but no stored file. This repo is private and not deployed; the PDFs stay here for study and are not for redistribution.

## Marlon's papers (Nov 14)

- [Isaksen, Togelius, Lantz, and Nealen, "Playing Games Across the Superintelligence Divide" (AAAI-16 workshop)](isaksen-et-al-2016-superintelligence-divide.md): what games would a superintelligence play with us? Friendly, learning, observational, and domination games, by analogy with adult–child, expert–novice, and human–animal play. ([PDF](isaksen-et-al-2016-superintelligence-divide.pdf))
- [Lantz, Isaksen, Jaffe, Nealen, and Togelius, "Depth in Strategic Games" (AAAI-17 workshop)](lantz-et-al-2017-depth-in-strategic-games.md): depth as a ladder of partial strategies that improve with computational resources; deep games are semi-ordered, between pure search and one weird trick. ([PDF](lantz-et-al-2017-depth-in-strategic-games.pdf))
