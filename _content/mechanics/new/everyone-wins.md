# Everyone or no one

## Definition

The players win only if every one of them meets their own goal, so no one can win alone.

In one line (HI's row): "The humans win only if every faction meets its private goal." [1]

## Description

In games:

- **[Data Center](../games/data-center.md)**: When the rounds run out, each faction checks its own goal: 'did you win together, or did someone win alone?' [2]
- **[Dead of Winter](../games/dead-of-winter.md)**: Players share a colony's goal but each has a secret objective, and one may be a betrayer. [3]

**Shows it** (HI's row): Galloway; *Pandemic* [1]

**With AI** (HI's row): Beating the AI isn't enough if one group paid for it. [1]

**In our game** (Nov 14 rulesets): partly: Data Center's survival ending asks each faction to check its own goal [1]

## How to

**Try it in two minutes** (HI's row): At the end, go faction by faction: if any missed, the table lost. [1]

**Build it** (HI's row): An end check over every faction's goal. [1]
