# Rules apart from fiction

## Definition

A game's rules and its fiction are kept apart, so the names and story can change without the numbers, or the numbers without the story. Juul describes games as half rules, half fiction. [2]

In one line (HI's row): "Keep the numbers and the names in separate fields, so one can change without the other." [1]
