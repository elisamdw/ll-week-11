# Present-harms track

## Definition

A track for harms that are already happening (water bills, layoffs, outages), kept apart from the long-term risk, on which players can lose even while they hold off the big threat.

In one line (HI's row): "A track of today's harms (water bills, layoffs, outages) that you can lose on while beating the AI." [1]

## Description

In games:

- **[Data Center](../games/data-center.md)**: Present harms start at 0 and the game is lost at 10; a shortage adds one; the rule cites Cegłowski's point that you can lose to today's harms while fighting tomorrow's superintelligence. [2]
- **[Pandemic](../games/pandemic.md)**: The outbreak marker is a track the players can lose on while they are busy curing diseases elsewhere. [3]
- **[Frostpunk](../games/frostpunk.md)**: Discontent rises with harsh laws and hardship, and the city can remove the player even while it survives the cold. [4]

**Shows it** (HI's row): The `harm` track in every version [1]

**With AI** (HI's row): Moves the fight from a future superintelligence to the costs of AI now. [1]

**In our game** (Nov 14 rulesets): yes: every version [1]

## How to

**Try it in two minutes** (HI's row): Keep two scores on the board: how far the AI got, and what it cost the town on the way. [1]

**Build it** (HI's row): A track that shortages and AI upkeep raise; a second loss condition beside the AI's share. [1]
