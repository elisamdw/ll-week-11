# Combat

## Definition

A contest between characters or units in which each tries to damage or defeat the other, resolved by the player's skill, by numbers, or by dice.

In one line (EG's row): "A contest between characters or units, resolved by skill, numbers or dice." [1]

## Description

In games:

- **[League of Legends](../games/league-of-legends.md)**: Champions fight minions, towers and each other, and gold from kills buys the items that make the next fight easier. [2]
- **[God of War (2018)](../games/god-of-war-2018.md)**: Kratos fights at close range with the axe and his fists, and at a distance by throwing the axe. [3]
- **[Risk](../games/risk.md)**: Battles are dice rolls: the attacker rolls up to three dice and the defender up to two, ties going to the defender. [4]

**Shows it** (EG's row): League of Legends; God of War; Risk's dice battles [1]

**With AI** (EG's row): The default loop of video games; what would a game about AI look like without it? [1]

**In our game** (Nov 14 rulesets): no [1]

## How to

**Try it in two minutes** (EG's row): Two players, two numbers each (strength, health); take turns rolling a die and subtracting. [1]

**Build it** (EG's row): Health and damage as numbers on each unit, a resolve function, and feedback when a hit lands. [1]

## Why to

The ENGL 189VG brainstorm treats combat as the default loop of video games and asks "what would be a loop that's not combat?" [5]
