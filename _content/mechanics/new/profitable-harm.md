# Profitable harm

## Definition

The move that pays best for one player is the one that hurts everyone, and the game shows the private gain and the shared cost together.

In one line (HI's row): "The most profitable move for one faction is the one that hurts everyone." [1]

## Description

In games:

- **[Data Center](../games/data-center.md)**: The company's goal is to end with twelve Money, and its best actions sell power to the AI and sign data deals that give the AI nodes. [2]
- **[McDonald's Video Game](../games/mcdonalds-video-game.md)**: Molleindustria's satire makes the profitable moves the destructive ones: plowing over rainforest, feeding cattle genetically altered grain, bribing public officials. [3]
- **[Fishbanks](../games/fishbanks.md)**: Teams running fishing companies buy and build ships to grow their assets, and too many ships can deplete the fish stock they all share. [4]

**Shows it** (HI's row): Selling power and water to the AI in Data Center; Molleindustria's *McDonald's Video Game* [1]

**With AI** (HI's row): The argument sits in the score table: the AI grows because someone is paid to feed it. [1]

**In our game** (Nov 14 rulesets): yes: Data Center and Normal Technology [1]

## How to

**Try it in two minutes** (HI's row): Give one player a bonus every time they sell to the AI, and see how long the table holds out. [1]

**Build it** (HI's row): A `sell` action that pays its faction and feeds the AI's upkeep. [1]

## Why to

**Proposed, for Marlon to choose:**

1. It turns a tragedy of the commons into something students do rather than read about.
2. Ask the company player to defend their moves in the debrief.

## Home game version

### In a regular classroom

**Proposed, for Marlon to choose:**

1. Give one group a goal that pays them for what costs the others, and do not tell the others.
2. A shared pool of tokens that every group can draw from for profit.

### Digitally

**Proposed, for Marlon to choose:**

1. A shared resource number on the projector that every group can spend.
2. Play a short commons game online and compare scores with the shared total.
