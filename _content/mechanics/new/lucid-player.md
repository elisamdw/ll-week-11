# The lucid player

## Definition

A player wins only by naming a rule that makes the game unfair and getting it changed by vote, so changing the rules is the move. [2]

In one line (HI's row): "You win only by naming a rule that makes the game unfair and getting it changed by vote." [1]

## Description

In games:

- **[Nomic](../games/nomic.md)**: Every move is a proposal to change the rules, voted on, and any rule, including the rules for changing rules, can be changed. [3]

**Shows it** (HI's row): Wark, *Gamer Theory*; *Nomic* [1]

**With AI** (HI's row): Seeing the AI's rules as rules is the win condition. [1]

**In our game** (Nov 14 rulesets): no [1]

## How to

**Try it in two minutes** (HI's row): Any player may stop play, name an unfair rule, and call a vote to change it. [1]

**Build it** (HI's row): An amend action: the table votes, and the change is written into a new ruleset (by Claude Code). [1]
