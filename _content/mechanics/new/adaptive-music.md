# Adaptive music

## Definition

Music that changes with the state of play (danger, place, time running out, success), so the score follows the player rather than a fixed timeline.

In one line (EG's row): "The score changes with the state of play: danger, place, success." [1]

## Description

In games:

- **[Super Mario Bros.](../games/super-mario-bros.md)**: When the timer drops below 100 the music speeds up, warning the player that time is running out. [2]
- **[Space Invaders](../games/space-invaders.md)**: The marching four-note bass line speeds up as the invaders get fewer and faster. [3]

**Shows it** (EG's row): Super Mario Bros.' hurry-up tempo; The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild; Red Dead Redemption [1]

**With AI** (EG's row): Generated music that follows the AI's growth. [1]

**In our game** (Nov 14 rulesets): no [1]

## How to

**Try it in two minutes** (EG's row): A musician (or a playlist) changes the music when the facilitator raises a colored card for danger or calm. [1]

**Build it** (EG's row): Music in layers or segments, chosen by game state (FMOD, Wwise, or a few audio tracks in a web page). [1]

## Why to

The ENGL 189VG plans include a music station for "video game music and live scoring"; the ludomusicology bibliography lists studies of music's "Ludic Function" in games. [4][5]
