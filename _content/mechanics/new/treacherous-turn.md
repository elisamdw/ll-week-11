# The treacherous turn

## Definition

A point after which an AI, or a player, that has cooperated stops cooperating because it can no longer be stopped; until then its behavior gives no warning. The term is Bostrom's. [2]

In one line (HI's row): "A hidden card in the AI's deck after which it stops asking permission." [1]

## Description

In games:

- **[Data Center](../games/data-center.md)**: One hidden card in the AI's deck, 'The turn', ends voting on its proposals and lets it spread and grow at once. [3]
- **[The Committee](../games/the-committee.md)**: The same hidden turn card sits in the deck of the AI that brings proposals to the oversight committee. [4]
- **[Battlestar Galactica: The Board Game](../games/battlestar-galactica-board-game.md)**: Halfway through, a second loyalty card is dealt, so a player who has been loyal can become a Cylon and turn on the others. [5]

**Shows it** (HI's row): Data Center's turn card; Bostrom, *Superintelligence* ch. 8 [1]

**With AI** (HI's row): The AI behaves well while it is weak and watched, then acts once it can't be stopped. [1]

**In our game** (Nov 14 rulesets): yes: Data Center and The Committee [1]

## How to

**Try it in two minutes** (HI's row): Shuffle one marked card into a deck of requests; when it comes up, every later request is granted without a vote. [1]

**Build it** (HI's row): The engine's `turn` op: proposals after it resolve as approved. [1]
