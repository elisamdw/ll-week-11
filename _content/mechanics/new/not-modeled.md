# The not-modeled list

## Definition

A list, shown to the players, of what the game leaves out of its model of the world, so the simplifications become part of what is discussed.

In one line (HI's row): "A visible list of what the game leaves out." [1]

## Description

In games:

- **[Data Center](../games/data-center.md)**: Six items, among them the people who work in the data center and the chips, mines and supply chain behind the hardware. [2]
- **[Normal Technology](../games/normal-technology.md)**: The same six, plus 'A path from here to the turn: this version says there isn't one', so the list states the version's claim. [3]
- **[The Committee](../games/the-committee.md)**: Four items, among them competing labs and the workers who label the data. [4]

**Shows it** (HI's row): The `notModeled` list in each ruleset [1]

**With AI** (HI's row): Labor, heat, chips and local politics are where real AI infrastructure differs from the model. [1]

**In our game** (Nov 14 rulesets): yes: every version [1]

## How to

**Try it in two minutes** (HI's row): After playing, name the omission that would change the game's argument most. [1]

**Build it** (HI's row): A `notModeled` array shown at the end of play and in the debrief. [1]

## Why to

**Proposed, for Marlon to choose:**

1. Start the debrief with the list: what else should be on it?
