# Compute cap

## Definition

Sites above a compute limit must be monitored, and unmonitored ones are where the AI goes next.

In one line (HI's row): "Nodes over a compute cap must be monitored; unmonitored ones are where the AI goes next." [1]

## Description

In games:

- **[The race (prototype)](../games/proto-the-race.md)**: Two labs choose in secret how hard to push, and a treaty with imperfect inspectors is the only brake. [2]

**Shows it** (HI's row): Yudkowsky and Soares's proposal for a treaty on compute [1]

**With AI** (HI's row): Governance as monitoring: the gaps are the AI's moves. [1]

**In our game** (Nov 14 rulesets): no [1]

## How to

**Try it in two minutes** (HI's row): Mark every node over a size limit; any you can't watch this round, the AI takes. [1]

**Build it** (HI's row): A cap setting; the AI's spread prefers unmonitored nodes over it. [1]
