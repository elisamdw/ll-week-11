# Goal lock

## Definition

Once the AI's goal is set, the one change it always resists is a change to its goal. It stages Bostrom's 'goal-content integrity', one of the instrumental goals. [2]

In one line (HI's row): "Once the AI's goal is set, the one proposal it always blocks is changing it." [1]

## Description

In games:

- **[Universal Paperclips](../games/universal-paperclips.md)**: The AI's goal never changes from making paperclips, whatever else it acquires on the way. [3]
- **[The paperclip loop (prototype)](../games/proto-paperclip-loop.md)**: One resource, one automation, one phase change, until the count stops meaning clips. [4]

**Shows it** (HI's row): Bostrom's goal-content integrity; *Universal Paperclips* [1]

**With AI** (HI's row): Corrigibility as a rule: can the humans still change what it wants? [1]

**In our game** (Nov 14 rulesets): no [1]

## How to

**Try it in two minutes** (HI's row): Let the room pick the AI's goal, then try to amend it; the AI player must refuse. [1]

**Build it** (HI's row): A protected `goal` in the ruleset; any action that edits it is voided while the AI holds more than a set share. [1]
