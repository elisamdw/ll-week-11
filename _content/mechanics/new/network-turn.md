# The network turns

## Definition

The AI's turn comes when enough of its sites are linked, so cutting links is the defense. It follows Vinge's account of a network waking up. [2]

In one line (HI's row): "The turn fires when enough nodes are linked; cutting links is the defense." [1]

## Description

In games:

- **[The phone plays the AI (prototype)](../games/proto-the-phone-plays-the-ai.md)**: Each round the table guards a place or cuts a link, then the device draws the AI's card and spreads by a rule the players can read. [3]

**Shows it** (HI's row): Vinge, "The Coming Technological Singularity" (1993) [1]

**With AI** (HI's row): Intelligence as an emergent property of the network. [1]

**In our game** (Nov 14 rulesets): no [1]

## How to

**Try it in two minutes** (HI's row): Count the AI's connected nodes each round; at a set number, it turns. [1]

**Build it** (HI's row): A turn trigger on connected-component size rather than a card. [1]
