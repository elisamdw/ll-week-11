# Write a faction

## Definition

Players design one faction (a goal, two actions, one forbidden act) for another group to play. [2]

In one line (HI's row): "Design one faction (a goal, two actions, one forbidden act) for another group to play." [1]

## Description

In games:

- **[Data Center](../games/data-center.md)**: Each of the four factions is a goal and a short list of actions, the model for writing a new one. [3]

**Shows it** (HI's row): Nguyen, *Games: Agency as Art* [1]

**With AI** (HI's row): Designing agency is the assignment; a faction is an agency handed to someone else. [1]

**In our game** (Nov 14 rulesets): no (factions exist; writing new ones would be a Claude Code remix) [1]

## How to

**Try it in two minutes** (HI's row): On an index card: who you are, what you want, two things you can do, one thing you won't. [1]

**Build it** (HI's row): Students describe the faction to Claude Code, which adds it to a ruleset. Students remix through Claude Code and the game's repo (Marlon, Nov 14 voice chat); there is no editor. [1]
