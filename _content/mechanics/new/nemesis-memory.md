# Nemesis memory

## Definition

The AI remembers who opposed it and comes after them, so enemies have a history with the player.

In one line (HI's row): "The AI remembers who opposed it and targets them next." [1]

## Description

In games:

- **[Middle-earth: Shadow of Mordor](../games/middle-earth-shadow-of-mordor.md)**: The Nemesis system lets an orc who defeats or escapes the player rise in rank and remember the encounter when they meet again. [2]

**Shows it** (HI's row): *Shadow of Mordor*'s Nemesis system (2014) [1]

**With AI** (HI's row): Memory makes a system into a character, and an opponent into an enemy. [1]
