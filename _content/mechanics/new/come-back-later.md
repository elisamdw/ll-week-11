# Come back later

## Definition

The game world runs while the player is away, and returning shows what happened.

In one line (HI's row): "The world runs while you're away, and you return to a report of what happened." [1]

## Description

In games:

- **[Animal Crossing](../games/animal-crossing.md)**: The village runs on the real-world clock, so days and seasons pass while the player is not playing. [2]
- **[Cookie Clicker](../games/cookie-clicker.md)**: With the Twin Gates of Transcendence upgrade, buildings keep producing at a reduced rate while the game is closed. [3]

**Shows it** (HI's row): Idle games; *SimEarth* [1]

**With AI** (HI's row): The AI acts whether or not the humans have moved. [1]

**In our game** (Nov 14 rulesets): no (proposed for the async Stafford version) [1]

## How to

**Try it in two minutes** (HI's row): Resolve the AI's turns between sessions and open the next class with the report. [1]

**Build it** (HI's row): Async mode: the AI's phase runs on a clock; players see a log since their last visit. [1]
