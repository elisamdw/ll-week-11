# Private metric and revise your metric

## Definition

Each player or faction scores against its own number, the shared goal has no number, and only the humans can revise what they count. It stages Nguyen's 'value capture'. [2]

In one line (HI's row): "Each faction has its own score; the shared goal has no number; a 'revise your metric' action is one the AI never gets." [1]

## Description

In games:

- **[Data Center](../games/data-center.md)**: Each faction has a private goal on one number: the utility's Energy, the council's present harms, the company's Money, the neighbors' trust. [3]
- **[Private goals on your phone (prototype)](../games/proto-private-goals.md)**: A private goal on each phone, with four tracks of tokens on the projector. [4]

**Shows it** (HI's row): Nguyen, "Value Capture" [1]

**With AI** (HI's row): The AI can't revise what it values; humans can, and that's the difference. [1]

**In our game** (Nov 14 rulesets): partly: factions have their own goals and scores in Data Center and Normal Technology [1]

## How to

**Try it in two minutes** (HI's row): Write your own success measure; midway, anyone may spend a turn to rewrite theirs. [1]

**Build it** (HI's row): Faction `score` fields; an action that edits a faction's score rule. [1]
