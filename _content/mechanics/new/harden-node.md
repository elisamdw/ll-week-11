# Harden a node

## Definition

Spend now to make one place resist failure later, whatever causes it.

In one line (HI's row): "Spend to make one node stay up through shortages, whatever the AI intends." [1]

## Description

In games:

- **[Data Center](../games/data-center.md)**: The utility's 'Harden a node' adds backup power and water, so the node stays up through shortages. [2]
- **[Pandemic](../games/pandemic.md)**: The Quarantine Specialist keeps disease cubes out of her city and the cities connected to it. [3]

**Shows it** (HI's row): Data Center's harden action [1]

**With AI** (HI's row): Resilience as the defense: protect the system rather than hunt the AI. [1]

**In our game** (Nov 14 rulesets): yes: Data Center and Normal Technology [1]

## How to

**Try it in two minutes** (HI's row): Give each player one shield token to place on the node they most want to survive. [1]

**Build it** (HI's row): The engine's `harden` op. [1]

## Why to

**Proposed, for Marlon to choose:**

1. Raise the question of who gets hardened first, and who pays for it.
2. Use it to talk about resilience as a choice made before the crisis.
