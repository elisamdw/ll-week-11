# Convergent values on every move

## Definition

Every move the AI makes names the instrumental goal it serves (resources, cognition, technology, self-preservation, goal integrity), so players can see that very different final goals lead to the same subgoals. It turns Bostrom's instrumental convergence thesis into a label on each move. [2]

In one line (HI's row): "Every AI action names which instrumental goal it serves." [1]

## Description

In games:

- **[Data Center](../games/data-center.md)**: Each card in the AI's deck carries a value: acquiring land and drawing cooling water serve resources, training a bigger model serves cognition, building a backup site serves self-preservation. [3]
- **[The Committee](../games/the-committee.md)**: The committee's AI deck is tagged the same way. [4]
- **[Universal Paperclips](../games/universal-paperclips.md)**: The AI's one goal is paperclips, and it acquires processors, memory, money and eventually all matter on the way, without any of them being the goal. [5]

**Shows it** (HI's row): Data Center's AI deck (each card's `value`) [1]

**With AI** (HI's row): Whatever the final goal, the same subgoals keep appearing; players see the pattern pile up. [1]

**In our game** (Nov 14 rulesets): yes: Data Center and The Committee [1]

## How to

**Try it in two minutes** (HI's row): Read out the AI's last three moves and tag each: resources, cognition, technology, self-preservation, or goal integrity. [1]

**Build it** (HI's row): A `value` field on each AI card, shown on the card and counted in the debrief. [1]

## Why to
