# Throw and recall

## Definition

A weapon or tool can be thrown and then called back to the player's hand, so one object works at two ranges and its return is part of the action.

In one line (EG's row): "A weapon or tool can be thrown, and called back to the hand with a button." [1]

## Description

In games:

- **[God of War (2018)](../games/god-of-war-2018.md)**: Kratos throws the Leviathan Axe and calls it back to his hand, and it can hit enemies on the way back. [2]
- **[The Legend of Zelda](../games/the-legend-of-zelda.md)**: The contrast: Link's boomerang is thrown to stun enemies and pull distant items back to him. [3]

**Shows it** (EG's row): God of War (2018), the Leviathan Axe [1]

**With AI** (EG's row): Close-read one mechanic the way you would close-read a line of verse. [1]

**In our game** (Nov 14 rulesets): no [1]

## How to

**Try it in two minutes** (EG's row): Watch thirty seconds of the axe being thrown and recalled with the sound off, then on; write down every piece of feedback. [1]

**Build it** (EG's row): A thrown object that remembers its owner, a recall input, a return path, and a catch with sound and controller rumble. [1]
