# Body as cursor

## Definition

A tracked point on the player's body (a hand, a fingertip, the nose) steers a point on the screen, so the body does what a mouse or joystick would. It is one kind of gesture input: continuous steering rather than a pose or a swing.

In one line (EG's row): "A hand, a nose or the whole body steers a point on the screen, as a mouse would." [1]

## Description

In games:

- **[EyeToy: Play](../games/eyetoy-play.md)**: The camera shows the player on screen, and moving a hand over targets hits them. [2]
- **[Fruit Ninja Kinect](../games/fruit-ninja-kinect.md)**: The player's hands are blades on screen, slicing fruit as it is thrown up. [3]

**Shows it** (EG's row): EyeToy: Play; Fruit Ninja Kinect [1]

**With AI** (EG's row): The machine decides where your hand is; lag and misreads become part of play. [1]

**In our game** (Nov 14 rulesets): no [1]

## How to

**Try it in two minutes** (EG's row): One student steers a laser pointer by leaning; the others call out targets to hit. [1]

**Build it** (EG's row): Track one landmark (an index fingertip, the nose) with a pose or hand model and map its position to the screen each frame. [1]
