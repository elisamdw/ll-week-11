# Stop button and open board

## Definition

A player can end the game at any time, or the game goes on after its goal is reached, so stopping becomes a choice the player can make.

In one line (HI's row): "A button that ends the game whenever a player chooses, and a board left open after the goal is gone." [1]

## Description

In games:

- **[Cow Clicker](../games/cow-clicker.md)**: Bogost ended his Facebook game with the 'Cowpocalypse', a countdown players could delay by paying. [2]

**Shows it** (HI's row): Bogost's *Cow Clicker*; the end of *Universal Paperclips* [1]

**With AI** (HI's row): Tests whether the AI loop is compelling or only compulsive. [1]

**In our game** (Nov 14 rulesets): no [1]

## How to

**Try it in two minutes** (HI's row): After the game ends, leave it running and see who keeps clicking. [1]

**Build it** (HI's row): A 'stop now' control and a post-game state that still accepts actions but scores nothing. [1]
