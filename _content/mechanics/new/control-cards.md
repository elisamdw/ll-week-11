# Control methods

## Definition

Human players hold cards for ways of controlling an AI (boxing it, stunting it, tripwires, incentives), each with how it fails printed on the back. The four methods are from Bostrom's chapter on the control problem. [2]

In one line (HI's row): "Human cards that box, stunt, tripwire or bribe the AI, each with its failure mode on the back." [1]

## Description

No published game with this mechanic was found on Nov 17; it is a proposal for the week's game, and the examples wait on one being built or found.

**Shows it** (HI's row): Bostrom, *Superintelligence* ch. 9 [1]

**With AI** (HI's row): Every control has a known way to fail; the game is choosing which failure you can live with. [1]

**In our game** (Nov 14 rulesets): no [1]

## How to

**Try it in two minutes** (HI's row): Each player holds one control card face down; reveal it when the AI acts, then read the back aloud. [1]

**Build it** (HI's row): Action cards with an effect and a `failure` effect that fires later with some chance. [1]
