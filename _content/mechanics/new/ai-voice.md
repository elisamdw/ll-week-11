# The AI's voice

## Definition

The lines the AI says when it acts are written apart from its numbers, so its personality can change without its behavior.

In one line (HI's row): "The lines the AI says when it acts, written as a field apart from its numbers." [1]

## Description

In games:

- **[Portal](../games/portal.md)**: GLaDOS, the facility's AI, comments on the player's progress through the test chambers. [2]
- **[System Shock](../games/system-shock.md)**: SHODAN, the station's AI, taunts the player as they move through the station. [3]

**Shows it** (HI's row): SHODAN, GLaDOS, Elohim, Friend Computer [1]

**With AI** (HI's row): The same rules argue differently in a different voice. [1]

**In our game** (Nov 14 rulesets): partly: cards carry `text`; a separate voice field is proposed, and per-card fields were not adopted on Nov 14 [1]

## How to

**Try it in two minutes** (HI's row): Replay a round with the AI's cards read in a different voice: command, needle, forbid, accuse. [1]

**Build it** (HI's row): A `voice` field on the AI and on each card. [1]
