# Proving ground

## Definition

The AI grows by winning benchmark games, and each win unlocks a capability and draws investment.

In one line (HI's row): "The AI grows by winning benchmarks; each win unlocks a capability and raises investment." [1]

## Description

In games:

- **[Chess](../games/chess.md)**: IBM's Deep Blue beat world champion Garry Kasparov in a match in 1997. [2]
- **[Go](../games/go.md)**: DeepMind's AlphaGo beat Lee Sedol four games to one in 2016. [3]
- **[Pokémon Red and Blue](../games/pokemon-red-and-blue.md)**: Anthropic streamed Claude playing Pokémon Red in 2025 as a public measure of its progress. [4]
