# Held position claims

## Definition

Standing in a zone holds it for your side, and leaving gives it up, so players' bodies are the game pieces. It is one kind of gesture input in which presence, not motion, is the move.

In one line (EG's row): "Standing in a zone holds it; leave and it is lost, so bodies are the pieces." [1]

## Description

In games:

- **[The room is the board (prototype)](../games/proto-the-room-is-the-board.md)**: A webcam watches the room cut into five places; stand to hold a place, two to take one back, and the AI spreads where no one stands. [2]
- **[King of the hill](../games/king-of-the-hill.md)**: One player holds the top of a mound or pile, and the others try to push them off and take it. [3]

**Shows it** (EG's row): King of the hill; the LL's 'The room is the board' prototype [1]

**With AI** (EG's row): The AI takes every place no body is defending. [1]

**In our game** (Nov 14 rulesets): no [1]

## How to

**Try it in two minutes** (EG's row): Tape five zones on the floor; each round a zone with no one standing in it is lost to the AI. [1]

**Build it** (EG's row): A camera watches the room, cut into zones; count the people detected in each zone each turn. [1]
