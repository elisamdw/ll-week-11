# AI advisor

## Definition

Each human player or faction gets an AI advisor that suggests a move, and must decide whether to trust it. [2]

In one line (HI's row): "Each human faction gets an advisor card that whispers a move." [1]

## Description

In games:

- **[The committee (prototype)](../games/proto-committee.md)**: The room votes with an AI advisor that is right eight times in ten; its two mistakes are either random or all in its own favor. [3]
- **[Chess](../games/chess.md)**: In 'advanced chess', which Garry Kasparov introduced in 1998, each human player consults a chess program during the game. [4]

**Shows it** (HI's row): Centaur chess; Isaksen et al. (2016) [1]

**With AI** (HI's row): How much do people defer to the machine, and when? [1]

**In our game** (Nov 14 rulesets): no [1]

## How to

**Try it in two minutes** (HI's row): Before each turn, draw a suggestion card; follow it or not, and keep count. [1]

**Build it** (HI's row): A per-faction suggestion drawn from the faction's best-scoring action. [1]
