# Externality card

## Definition

Each AI build draws a card placed on a distant place (a mine, e-waste, a dry well), so costs land where the builder does not see them. [2]

In one line (HI's row): "Each AI build draws a card placed on a distant node: mines, e-waste, a dry well." [1]

## Description

In games:

- **[Leaks (prototype)](../games/proto-leaks.md)**: Power and water become compute on the dashboard, while the costs land as cards on distant places that stay dark in the company's view until the end. [3]

**Shows it** (HI's row): Dyer-Witheford and de Peuter, *Games of Empire*; Crawford, *Atlas of AI* [1]

**With AI** (HI's row): Moves the AI's costs off the board, where players can choose not to look. [1]

**In our game** (Nov 14 rulesets): no [1]

## How to

**Try it in two minutes** (HI's row): Each time something is built, put a cost card on the far side of the map. [1]

**Build it** (HI's row): A side deck; the AI's spread adds a harm card to a node far from it. [1]
