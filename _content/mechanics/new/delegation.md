# Delegation card

## Definition

A player hands part of the game to an automated manager to save effort, and does not see it again until it fails. Fizek writes about delegated play. [2]

In one line (HI's row): "Hand a node to an automated manager to save actions; you don't see it again until it fails." [1]

## Description

In games:

- **[Civilization](../games/civilization.md)**: Workers can be set to automate, choosing their own improvements without the player's orders. [3]

**Shows it** (HI's row): Fizek on interpassivity [1]

**With AI** (HI's row): Delegation to AI saves effort and costs sight. [1]

**In our game** (Nov 14 rulesets): no [1]

## How to

**Try it in two minutes** (HI's row): Give a teammate your piece and look away; you hear about it only when something breaks. [1]

**Build it** (HI's row): A card that hides a node's state and acts on it each round by a fixed rule. [1]
