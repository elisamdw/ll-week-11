# Strike

## Definition

A workers' faction can stop production for a round; its goal is wages, not winning.

In one line (HI's row): "A workers' faction can stop production for a round; its goal is wages, not winning." [1]

## Description

No published game with this mechanic was found on Nov 17; it is a proposal for the week's game, and the examples wait on one being built or found.

**Shows it** (HI's row): The SAG-AFTRA video-game strike (2024–25); *Games of Empire* [1]

**With AI** (HI's row): Labor is the omitted input to AI; a strike makes it visible. [1]

**In our game** (Nov 14 rulesets): no [1]

## How to

**Try it in two minutes** (HI's row): One player is labor; once a game they can freeze every node for a round unless their demand is met. [1]

**Build it** (HI's row): A workers' faction with a strike action that sets every node to no yield for a round. [1]
