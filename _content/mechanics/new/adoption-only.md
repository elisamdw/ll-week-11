# Adoption only

## Definition

The AI can grow only where a human player chooses to adopt it; it has no move of its own. The version follows Narayanan and Kapoor's 'AI as normal technology'. [2]

In one line (HI's row): "The AI can build only where a human faction chose to adopt it; there is no turn." [1]

## Description

In games:

- **[Normal Technology](../games/normal-technology.md)**: Every new site is a proposal someone approves or a deal someone signs, each site the AI holds pays one Money back, and there is no turn. [2]
- **[Data Center](../games/data-center.md)**: The contrast: here the AI's own deck spreads it, and one card is the turn. [3]

**Shows it** (HI's row): The Normal Technology version [1]

**With AI** (HI's row): AI as normal technology: diffusion is a human decision, slow and uneven. [1]

**In our game** (Nov 14 rulesets): yes: Normal Technology [1]

## How to

**Try it in two minutes** (HI's row): The AI player may only place a token where another player has invited it. [1]

**Build it** (HI's row): AI cards as pitches (proposals); no `turn` card in the deck. [1]
