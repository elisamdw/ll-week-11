# Character creator

## Definition

Before or during play, the player shapes their character's body, face, class or look, usually with sliders, swappable parts and skins.

In one line (EG's row): "Before play, the player shapes their character with sliders, parts and skins." [1]

## Description

In games:

- **[The Sims](../games/the-sims.md)**: Create a Sim sets each character's appearance and personality before they move into a house. [2]
- **[Mass Effect](../games/mass-effect.md)**: The player picks Commander Shepard's face, gender and class before the story starts. [3]

**Shows it** (EG's row): The Sims; Baldur's Gate 3; the ENGL 189VG Blender station [1]

**With AI** (EG's row): Describe a character in words and let a model build it; what did it assume? [1]

**In our game** (Nov 14 rulesets): no [1]

## How to

**Try it in two minutes** (EG's row): A paper doll with three swappable parts and two sliders drawn as scales. [1]

**Build it** (EG's row): A character model with shape keys driven by sliders, and materials for skins (the Blender station, with Claude driving Blender through an MCP server). [1]

## Why to

The ENGL 189VG Blender station's goal: "make a base character, give it at least two sliders, and design a skin for it". [4]
