# Pose as the move

## Definition

The player acts by holding a body pose, and the game reads the pose and turns it into a move. It is one kind of gesture input: the shape held, not the motion made.

In one line (EG's row): "Hold a body pose, and holding it is the action." [1]

## Description

In games:

- **[Hole in the Wall](../games/hole-in-the-wall.md)**: Players contort their bodies to fit a cut-out shape in a wall moving toward them. [2]
- **[Wii Fit](../games/wii-fit.md)**: In the yoga exercises the player holds a pose on the Balance Board while the game measures how steady they are. [3]

**Shows it** (EG's row): Hole in the Wall; Wii Fit's yoga [1]

**With AI** (EG's row): An AI judges whether the pose is close enough, so the threshold is the AI's call. [1]

**In our game** (Nov 14 rulesets): no [1]

## How to

**Try it in two minutes** (EG's row): Call a shape (a tree, a letter of the alphabet); the room holds it for three seconds to score. [1]

**Build it** (EG's row): A pose model (MediaPipe Pose) gives joint positions each frame; compare joint angles with a target pose and fire the action when they match for a set time. [1]
