# AI stance ladder

## Definition

The AI's attitude to the players moves through stages (friendly, learning, watching, dominating) as its power crosses thresholds. Isaksen and colleagues classify games' AIs this way. [2]

In one line (HI's row): "The AI's stance moves from friendly to learning to observational to domination as compute crosses thresholds." [1]
