# Secret p(doom) dial

## Definition

Each player secretly sets how dangerous they think the AI is, and a rule in the game scales with the table's average.

In one line (HI's row): "Each player secretly sets how dangerous they think the AI is; the regulator's powers scale with the average." [1]

## Description

No published game with this mechanic was found on Nov 17; it is a proposal for the week's game, and the examples wait on one being built or found.

**Shows it** (HI's row): AI-risk surveys [1]

**With AI** (HI's row): Belief about AI is itself a resource that shapes policy. [1]

**In our game** (Nov 14 rulesets): no [1]

## How to

**Try it in two minutes** (HI's row): Everyone writes a number from 0 to 100 on a slip; reveal the average and give the regulator that many actions per ten. [1]

**Build it** (HI's row): Private sliders per player; a derived setting that scales one faction's actions. [1]
