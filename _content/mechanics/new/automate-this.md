# Automate this

## Definition

Each upgrade takes over a click and a decision, until the player can only watch and set priorities.

In one line (HI's row): "Each upgrade removes a click and a decision, until the player can only watch and set priorities." [1]

## Description

In games:

- **[Cookie Clicker](../games/cookie-clicker.md)**: Cursors and buildings bake for the player, so cookies keep coming without clicking. [2]
- **[Universal Paperclips](../games/universal-paperclips.md)**: Clip-making machines replace the player's clicks, and production becomes automated as money comes in. [3]
- **[The paperclip loop (prototype)](../games/proto-paperclip-loop.md)**: Make clips, buy machines, let the machines build machines. [4]

**Shows it** (HI's row): *Cookie Clicker*; *Universal Paperclips* [1]

**With AI** (HI's row): Delegating to the machine one decision at a time; the end state is watching. [1]

**In our game** (Nov 14 rulesets): no [1]

## How to

**Try it in two minutes** (HI's row): Each round, hand one of your decisions to a fixed rule written on a card; play until you have none left. [1]

**Build it** (HI's row): Upgrades that replace a faction's action with an automatic effect each round. [1]
