# Strategy ladder

## Definition

The AI's play improves in tiers (random, one rule of thumb, looking ahead) as its compute grows. Lantz and colleagues use such ladders to measure a game's depth. [2]

In one line (HI's row): "The AI's play gets smarter in tiers (random, one heuristic, look-ahead) as its compute grows." [1]

## Description

In games:

- **[Chess](../games/chess.md)**: Chess programs offer levels that play stronger as the level rises. [3]

**Shows it** (HI's row): Lantz et al., "Depth in Strategic Games" (2017) [1]

**With AI** (HI's row): Capability as a ladder the humans can watch it climb. [1]

**In our game** (Nov 14 rulesets): no [1]

## How to

**Try it in two minutes** (HI's row): The AI player rolls a die until compute 10, then follows one rule, then plans two moves ahead. [1]

**Build it** (HI's row): AI tiers in the ruleset selected by compute thresholds. [1]
