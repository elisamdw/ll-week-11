# Constraint (the forbidden move)

## Definition

A rule forbids something players would normally do, and play becomes working around the gap.

On GM's card: "A rule takes something away, and play has to work around it." [1]

## Description

In games:

- **[Taboo](../games/taboo.md)**: A player must get their team to guess a word without saying it or the listed words most closely related to it. [2]
- **[Twenty Questions](../games/twenty-questions.md)**: Players identify an object by asking questions that can only be answered yes or no. [3]

**Shows it** (GM's card): *Taboo* [1]

**With AI** (HI): A self-imposed rule (no AI on this node) costs efficiency and keeps meaning (Bostrom's Deep Utopia; Flanagan's unplaying). [1]

**In our game** (Nov 14 rulesets): partly: actions like moratorium and zoning forbid the AI on a node [1]

## How to

**Try it in two minutes** (GM's card): Describe your favorite food without using the letter E. [1]

**Build it** (GM's card): `/voice` listens for the forbidden words and fires a buzzer. [1]
