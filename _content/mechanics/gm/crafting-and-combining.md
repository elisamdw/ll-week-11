# Crafting and combining

## Definition

Players combine items to make new ones, so gathering ingredients and knowing recipes are the play.

On GM's card: "Put two things together to make a third." [1]

## Description

In games:

- **[Minecraft](../games/minecraft.md)**: Players arrange gathered materials on a 3x3 crafting grid to make tools, weapons and armor. [2]
- **[Infinite Craft](../games/infinite-craft.md)**: Players combine two elements to make a new one, and a language model decides what each new combination becomes. [3]

**Shows it** (GM's card): *Minecraft*; *Infinite Craft* (a model decides) [1]

**With AI** (HI): Combine inputs (chips, power, water, data) to build the AI's next capability. [1]

**In our game** (Nov 14 rulesets): no [1]

## How to

**Try it in two minutes** (GM's card): Two people each hold up an object; the room names what they make together. [1]

**Build it** (GM's card): Two cards under `/draw-a-box`; Claude names what they make and adds it to the board. [1]
