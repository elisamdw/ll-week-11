# Interruption and event cards

## Definition

An event from outside the players' plans breaks into play and has to be dealt with: a windfall, a disaster, a rule change for one round.

On GM's card: "Something from outside breaks in and must be absorbed." [1]

## Description

In games:

- **[Monopoly](../games/monopoly.md)**: Landing on Chance or Community Chest draws a card that pays, charges or moves you. [2]
- **[The Oregon Trail](../games/the-oregon-trail.md)**: Random events (illness, a broken wagon part) interrupt the journey and cost supplies or time. [3]
- **[Pandemic](../games/pandemic.md)**: Epidemic cards shuffled into the player deck interrupt a turn and raise the infection rate. [4]
- **[Data Center](../games/data-center.md)**: The AI's card each round breaks into the humans' plans before they act. [5]

**Shows it** (GM's card): *Monopoly* (Chance cards) [1]

**With AI** (HI): The AI is the interruption: each round it draws a card the humans must absorb. [1]

**In our game** (Nov 14 rulesets): yes: the AI's cards break in each round [1]

## How to

**Try it in two minutes** (GM's card): Mid-scene, a partner hands you a card; play it at once. [1]

**Build it** (GM's card): A MIDI pad throws a random event card onto the projector. [1]

## Why to
