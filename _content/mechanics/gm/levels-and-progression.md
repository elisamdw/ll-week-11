# Levels and progression

## Definition

Experience earned in play adds up to new levels, abilities or stages, so the player grows stronger over time. Juul's 'progression' is the related sense of challenges presented one after another. [2]

On GM's card: "Experience adds up to new abilities." [1]

## Description

In games:

- **[Pokémon Red and Blue](../games/pokemon-red-and-blue.md)**: Creatures gain experience in battle, level up, learn moves and evolve. [3]
- **[Dungeons & Dragons](../games/dungeons-and-dragons.md)**: Characters earn experience points and gain levels that bring new abilities. [4]

**Shows it** (GM's card): *Pokémon* [1]

**With AI** (HI): The AI levels up; its stance moves from friendly to domination as it does (Isaksen et al.'s table). [1]

**In our game** (Nov 14 rulesets): no (proposed: the AI climbs a strategy ladder as compute crosses thresholds) [1]

## How to

**Try it in two minutes** (GM's card): Every three tasks you finish, you unlock a new tool you're allowed to use. [1]

**Build it** (GM's card): A level bar per player on a wall display. [1]
