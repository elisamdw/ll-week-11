# Worker placement

## Definition

Each player has a few workers to place on action spaces; a space taken by one player is closed to the others that round.

On GM's card: "Each player sends a few workers to claim scarce actions." [1]

## Description

In games:

- **[Agricola](../games/agricola.md)**: Farmers take turns placing family members on action spaces, and a space taken is closed to the others until the next round. [2]

**Shows it** (GM's card): *Agricola* [1]

**With AI** (HI): Delegate a worker to an automated manager to save actions, and lose sight of it. [1]

**In our game** (Nov 14 rulesets): partly: each faction has a few actions per turn [1]

## How to

**Try it in two minutes** (GM's card): Five action cards on the table, three coins each: place a coin to claim an action, one coin per card. [1]

**Build it** (GM's card): Tokens on printed action spaces, read by the overhead camera. [1]
