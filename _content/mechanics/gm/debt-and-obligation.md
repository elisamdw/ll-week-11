# Debt and obligation

## Definition

A player owes something (money, a service, a promise) and must pay it before doing other things, or pay a penalty for not paying.

On GM's card: "You owe something and cannot leave until it is paid." [1]

## Description

In games:

- **[Monopoly](../games/monopoly.md)**: Landing on another player's property means paying rent, and a player who cannot pay must mortgage or sell, or go bankrupt. [2]
- **[Animal Crossing](../games/animal-crossing.md)**: Paying off the house loan earns a bigger house and a bigger loan (Bogost's example, as quoted in the glossary). [3]

**Shows it** (GM's card): *Monopoly* (rent and mortgages) [1]

**With AI** (HI): Compute and data-center deals as debt: the town that took the money now owes the AI water and power. [1]
