# Territory and area control

## Definition

Players compete to hold parts of a board or map, and holding them brings points, income or power.

On GM's card: "Hold more of the board than the others." [1]

## Description

In games:

- **[Go](../games/go.md)**: Players place stones to surround empty points, and the larger surrounded territory wins. [2]
- **[Risk](../games/risk.md)**: Holding a whole continent at the start of your turn brings extra armies. [3]
- **[Data Center](../games/data-center.md)**: The AI spreads across fourteen linked nodes and wins when it holds half of them; zoning freezes and occupations take nodes back. [4]

**Shows it** (GM's card): Go; *Risk* [1]

**With AI** (HI): The AI's territory is infrastructure; control of the map is Bostrom's singleton. [1]

**In our game** (Nov 14 rulesets): yes: the AI spreads across linked nodes; the game ends if it holds half the map (Data Center) [1]

## How to

**Try it in two minutes** (GM's card): Take turns placing sticky notes on a grid; you hold a square when you surround it. [1]

**Build it** (GM's card): Colored paper on a grid read by `/draw-a-box`; the projector shades who holds what. [1]
