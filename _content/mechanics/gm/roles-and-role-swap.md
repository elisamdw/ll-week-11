# Roles and role swap

## Definition

Each player has a role with its own powers, limits or goal, so the same game asks different things of different seats; some games let roles change hands.

On GM's card: "Each player has a different power, and the roles can change hands." [1]

## Description

In games:

- **[Pandemic](../games/pandemic.md)**: Each player is dealt a role card (such as the Medic or the Scientist) whose special abilities change how some actions work. [2]
- **[Puerto Rico](../games/puerto-rico.md)**: Each round players choose roles in turn, and the chosen role's action applies to everyone, with a bonus for the one who chose it. [3]
- **[Data Center](../games/data-center.md)**: Four factions (the utility, the city council, the company, the neighbors) each have their own actions and goal. [4]

**Shows it** (GM's card): *Pandemic* (role cards) [1]

**With AI** (HI): Roles are the actors hidden behind 'the AI': companies, utilities, regulators, workers. [1]

**In our game** (Nov 14 rulesets): yes: Data Center and Normal Technology (utility, council, company, neighbors) [1]

## How to

**Try it in two minutes** (GM's card): Play a short scene; halfway through, the highest-status character becomes the lowest. [1]

**Build it** (GM's card): Roles dealt as cards; a cue on `/launch` reshuffles them mid-game. [1]

## Why to

**Proposed, for Marlon to choose:**

1. Roles let students argue from a position they do not hold, as in a role-play or a moot.
2. Swap roles halfway so each group has to play against its own earlier strategy.
