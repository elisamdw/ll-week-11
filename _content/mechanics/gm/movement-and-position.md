# Movement and position

## Definition

Where a piece or a body is decides what it can do, so moving is the main choice.

On GM's card: "Where your body is changes what you can do." [1]

## Description

In games:

- **[Chess](../games/chess.md)**: Each piece moves in its own pattern, and what it threatens depends on where it stands. [2]
- **[Twister](../games/twister.md)**: A spinner names a hand or foot and a color, and players must place it on a matching circle on the mat. [3]
- **[The room is the board (prototype)](../games/proto-the-room-is-the-board.md)**: A webcam watches the room cut into five places; standing in a place holds it. [4]

**Shows it** (GM's card): *Twister* [1]

**With AI** (HI): Where the AI is matters: adjacency makes the network the battlefield (Vinge: cut the links). [1]

**In our game** (Nov 14 rulesets): partly: the AI spreads only to nodes linked to its own [1]

## How to

**Build it** (GM's card): MediaPipe pose: a cue fires when a hand and a foot are on the called spots. [1]
