# Puzzle and lock-and-key

## Definition

A problem with a definite solution blocks the way, and solving it, or finding its key, opens the next part.

On GM's card: "A problem with a solution that opens the next part." [1]

## Description

In games:

- **[Portal](../games/portal.md)**: Each test chamber is a puzzle the player solves by placing portals to move themselves and objects through it. [2]
- **[The Legend of Zelda](../games/the-legend-of-zelda.md)**: Locked doors in dungeons need keys found in the dungeon, and each dungeon's major item is needed to solve its puzzles. [3]

**Shows it** (GM's card): *Portal*; escape rooms [1]

**With AI** (HI): An AI solves the lock instantly; the puzzle becomes knowing what to ask. [1]

**In our game** (Nov 14 rulesets): no [1]

## How to

**Try it in two minutes** (GM's card): Lock a box with a three-digit code; hide the three digits in three riddles. [1]

**Build it** (GM's card): A word said to `/voice` or a card shown to the camera unlocks the next cue. [1]
