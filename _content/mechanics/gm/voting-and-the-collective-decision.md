# Voting and the collective decision

## Definition

The players decide together by vote, and the result binds everyone, including those who voted against it.

On GM's card: "The group chooses, and everyone lives with the result." [1]

## Description

In games:

- **[Quiplash](../games/quiplash.md)**: Players and the audience vote between two answers to a prompt on their phones, and the votes are the points. [2]
- **[Werewolf (Mafia)](../games/werewolf.md)**: Each day the village votes to eliminate one player, without knowing who the werewolves are. [3]
- **[Data Center](../games/data-center.md)**: An AI proposal waits for the humans to approve or block it, until the turn card, after which its proposals pass without a vote. [4]

**Shows it** (GM's card): *Quiplash* (the audience votes) [1]

**With AI** (HI): The group decides whether to approve the AI's requests; after the turn, it stops asking. [1]

**In our game** (Nov 14 rulesets): yes: Data Center, The Committee and Normal Technology (the AI's proposals wait for an approve or block vote until the turn) [1]

## How to

**Try it in two minutes** (GM's card): Show three possible endings, ten seconds each. The room picks one, and you play it. [1]

**Build it** (GM's card): Phones vote; the tally fires the winning cue on `/stage`. [1]

## Why to

**Proposed, for Marlon to choose:**

1. A vote makes every student a player at once, which suits a large room.
2. Use it to stage governance: a committee that votes on an AI's requests is the Stafford workshop's subject.
