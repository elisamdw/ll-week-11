# Hidden rule

## Definition

One player, or the game, holds a secret rule, and the others discover it by trying moves and seeing which ones fit.

On GM's card: "One player invents a secret rule; the others discover it by playing." [1]

## Description

In games:

- **[Zendo](../games/zendo.md)**: The game master makes a secret rule about arrangements of pieces, and players build arrangements and are told whether each follows it. [2]
- **[The intent (prototype)](../games/proto-the-intent.md)**: Only the AI player's phone says what it is optimizing for; everyone else reads its intent from the moves it picks and passes over. [3]

**Shows it** (GM's card): *Zendo* [1]

**With AI** (HI): The AI's real objective is a hidden rule players infer from its moves. [1]

**In our game** (Nov 14 rulesets): no [1]

## How to

**Try it in two minutes** (GM's card): One person thinks of a rule about objects on the table. Others place objects and hear only yes or no. [1]

**Build it** (GM's card): Claude holds the rule; players show cards to `/draw-a-box`, and Claude says yes or no. [1]
