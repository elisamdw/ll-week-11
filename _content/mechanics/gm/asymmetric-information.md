# Asymmetric information

## Definition

Each player or team holds information the others lack and need, so play becomes communicating across the gap. It is hidden information arranged so that the parts fit together.

On GM's card: "Each group holds a part the others need." [1]

## Description

In games:

- **[Keep Talking and Nobody Explodes](../games/keep-talking-and-nobody-explodes.md)**: One player sees the bomb and the others hold the defusal manual, so neither side can win without describing what it sees. [2]
- **[Hanabi](../games/hanabi.md)**: Each player sees everyone's cards but their own, and must rely on the others' limited hints. [3]
- **[Private goals on your phone (prototype)](../games/proto-private-goals.md)**: Four tracks of tokens on the projector, a private goal on each phone, and one player secretly the AI. [4]

**Shows it** (GM's card): *Keep Talking and Nobody Explodes* [1]

**With AI** (HI): The company knows what the AI pays; the neighbors know what it costs. No one sees the whole system. [1]

**In our game** (Nov 14 rulesets): partly: Data Center and Normal Technology factions have private goals [1]

## How to

**Try it in two minutes** (GM's card): One person describes a drawing back to back with a partner who has to redraw it. [1]

**Build it** (GM's card): One player sees the puzzle on `/stage`, the other holds the printed manual. [1]
