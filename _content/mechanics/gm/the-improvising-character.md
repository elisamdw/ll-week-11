# The improvising character (AI co-star)

## Definition

A character answers whatever the player says or does, in role, rather than choosing from lines written in advance; today this usually means a language model.

On GM's card: "A character who answers whatever you say, in role." [1]

## Description

In games:

- **[Façade](../games/facade.md)**: The player types anything to a couple in their apartment, and the game's characters respond in a short interactive drama. [2]
- **[AI Dungeon](../games/ai-dungeon.md)**: A language model narrates a text adventure in response to whatever the player types. [3]

**Shows it** (GM's card): *Façade*; *AI Dungeon* [1]

**With AI** (HI): This is the AI as a character: the Nov 18 co-star, from Façade and Event[0] to LLM NPCs. [1]

**In our game** (Nov 14 rulesets): no (proposed: a voice field for the AI's lines, and a tutor face) [1]

## How to

**Try it in two minutes** (GM's card): One person plays a shopkeeper and must answer everything in character, however strange. [1]

**Build it** (GM's card): A voiced character, built like week 9's Ensemble agents, that hears the player through `/voice`. [1]
