# Hidden information

## Definition

Part of the game state is kept from some players (a hand of cards, a face-down piece, a secret goal), so players act on guesses and read each other.

On GM's card: "Some players know what others do not." [1]

## Description

In games:

- **[Texas Hold'em](../games/texas-holdem.md)**: Each player's two hole cards are private and the five community cards are shared, so betting is a claim about cards no one else can see. [2]
- **[Stratego](../games/stratego.md)**: Each piece's rank faces its owner only, and is revealed when it attacks or is attacked. [3]
- **[Data Center](../games/data-center.md)**: The turn card is hidden in the AI's deck, and each faction holds a private goal. [4]

**Shows it** (GM's card): Poker; *Stratego* [1]

**With AI** (HI): The AI knows its plan; the humans see only its moves. The treacherous turn is a hidden-information mechanic. [1]

**In our game** (Nov 14 rulesets): yes: Data Center and The Committee (the turn card is hidden in the AI's deck) [1]

## How to

**Try it in two minutes** (GM's card): Tell the room a secret your scene partner must never learn. [1]

**Build it** (GM's card): Each player's phone shows a private card; the projector shows only the public state. [1]
