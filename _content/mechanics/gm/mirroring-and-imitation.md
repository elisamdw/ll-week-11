# Mirroring and imitation

## Definition

Players copy what a leader does, and the game lives in the gap between the copy and the model.

On GM's card: "Copy what the leader does." [1]

## Description

In games:

- **[Simon Says](../games/simon-says.md)**: Players copy the leader's actions only when the command starts with 'Simon says'. [2]
- **[Just Dance](../games/just-dance.md)**: Players copy an on-screen dancer's moves and are scored on how closely they match. [3]

**Shows it** (GM's card): Simon Says [1]

**With AI** (HI): The AI copies its players; imitation learning makes the mirror the method. [1]

**In our game** (Nov 14 rulesets): no [1]

## How to

**Try it in two minutes** (GM's card): Face a partner and mirror every move; switch leaders without saying so. [1]

**Build it** (GM's card): MediaPipe pose compares a player to the leader and scores the match. [1]
