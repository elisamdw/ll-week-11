# Chance

## Definition

A random device (dice, a shuffled deck, a spinner, a random number) decides part of what happens, so players plan for odds rather than certainties.

On GM's card: "Dice, cards, or coins decide part of what happens." [1]

## Description

In games:

- **[Yahtzee](../games/yahtzee.md)**: A player rolls five dice up to three times a turn, keeping some between rolls, and chooses which scoring box the result fills. [2]
- **[The Game of Life](../games/the-game-of-life.md)**: A spinner decides how far you move; the ENGL 189VG planning names 'rolling dice and spinning spinner' as a difference worth noticing. [3][4]
- **[Risk](../games/risk.md)**: Dice decide each battle, the attacker rolling up to three and the defender up to two. [5]
- **[Data Center](../games/data-center.md)**: The AI's deck is shuffled, so the humans know what is in it but not the order. [6]

**Shows it** (GM's card): *Yahtzee* [1]

**With AI** (HI): Chance stands in for what the humans can't predict about the AI; a seeded shuffle lets every table face the same machine. [1]

**In our game** (Nov 14 rulesets): yes: the AI's deck is shuffled with a seed in every version [1]

## How to

**Try it in two minutes** (GM's card): Flip a coin: heads, your character tells the truth; tails, they lie until the end. [1]

**Build it** (GM's card): A dice roller on `/stage` triggered by a pad; the roll picks the next cue. [1]

## Why to

**Proposed, for Marlon to choose:**

1. The dice-versus-spinner comparison from the ENGL 189VG planning: the same randomness through a different object feels different, which is a close-reading exercise.
2. Ask what chance hides: who benefits when outcomes look like luck?
