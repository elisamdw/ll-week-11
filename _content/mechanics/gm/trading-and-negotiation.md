# Trading and negotiation

## Definition

Players exchange resources, promises or favors on terms they settle by talking, rather than by a fixed rule.

On GM's card: "Players exchange what they have for what they need." [1]

## Description

In games:

- **[Catan](../games/catan.md)**: On your turn you may trade resource cards with any player at any rate you agree, or with the bank at a fixed rate. [2]
- **[Monopoly](../games/monopoly.md)**: Players can trade properties and cash in any deal they agree on, and trading is a key way to complete a color set. [3]
- **[Data Center](../games/data-center.md)**: The company's 'Sign a data deal' gives the AI a node and the company money, a trade between a faction and the AI. [4]

**Shows it** (GM's card): *Catan* [1]

**With AI** (HI): Players trade with the AI itself: selling it power and water is profitable and harms everyone (anti-advergame scoring). [1]

**In our game** (Nov 14 rulesets): yes: Data Center and Normal Technology (sell and deal actions) [1]

## How to

**Try it in two minutes** (GM's card): Everyone gets three random objects; you need a set of three alike. Trade until someone has one. [1]

**Build it** (GM's card): Tokens on the table read by `/draw-a-box`; the ledger updates on `/stage`. [1]

## Why to

**Proposed, for Marlon to choose:**

1. Trading puts talk at the center of play, which suits classes that are about argument.
2. Use it to show how an exchange both sides want can still hurt a third party.

## Home game version

### In a regular classroom

**Proposed, for Marlon to choose:**

1. Colored paper as resources, with a two-minute trading window each round.
2. Contracts written on index cards and signed.

### Digitally

**Proposed, for Marlon to choose:**

1. A shared ledger in a spreadsheet.
2. Direct messages between groups during a timed trading phase.
