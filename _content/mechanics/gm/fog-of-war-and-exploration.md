# Fog of war and exploration

## Definition

Parts of the map stay hidden until a player's units go there or can see them, so exploring is itself a move.

On GM's card: "The map is hidden until you go there." [1]

## Description

In games:

- **[Civilization](../games/civilization.md)**: The map starts hidden, and units explore it to lift the fog of war and reveal what is nearby. [2]
- **[League of Legends](../games/league-of-legends.md)**: Players see only what their team's champions, minions, turrets and wards can see. [3]

**Shows it** (GM's card): *Civilization* [1]

**With AI** (HI): Players see the AI's infrastructure only where they have looked: the invisibility of data centers. [1]

**In our game** (Nov 14 rulesets): no (proposed: an AI seat that sees only through its sensors) [1]

## How to

**Try it in two minutes** (GM's card): Cover a map with sticky notes; lift one each turn. [1]

**Build it** (GM's card): The projector darkens a paper map on the table and lifts the fog wherever a hand or token goes. [1]
