# Gesture input

## Definition

The player's body movement is the input, read by a camera or motion sensor, so the gesture is the move.

On GM's card: "The body is the controller." [1]

## Description

In games:

- **[Wii Sports](../games/wii-sports.md)**: Players swing the Wii Remote like a racket, a bat or a bowling ball. [2]
- **[Just Dance](../games/just-dance.md)**: A camera or controller reads the player's dance moves and scores them. [3]

**Shows it** (GM's card): *Wii Sports*; *Just Dance* [1]

**With AI** (HI): A gesture model reads the body; the game is whether it reads you right. [1]

**In our game** (Nov 14 rulesets): no [1]

## How to

**Try it in two minutes** (GM's card): Conduct the room: raise your hand and they get louder, lower it and they hush. [1]

**Build it** (GM's card): `/track/instruments` maps hand height to a value the game reads. [1]
