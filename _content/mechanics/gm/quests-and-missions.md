# Quests and missions

## Definition

A task given to the player, with a goal and a reward, that gives play a direction.

On GM's card: "A task with a reward gives play a direction." [1]

## Description

In games:

- **[The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild](../games/zelda-breath-of-the-wild.md)**: The main quest sends Link to free four Divine Beasts, while side quests from villagers offer rewards along the way. [2]
- **[Mass Effect](../games/mass-effect.md)**: Missions on the galaxy map give Shepard goals, some required for the story and some optional. [3]

**Shows it** (GM's card): *The Legend of Zelda* [1]

**With AI** (HI): Who gives the AI its quest, and what does it count as done? [1]
