# Resource management

## Definition

Players hold limited amounts of things (money, food, energy, time) and decide what to spend, save or convert, knowing that what goes to one goal is lost to another. [2]

On GM's card: "Spend limited things to get what you want." [1]

## Description

Costikyan separates resources, which you manage to reach your goals, from tokens, which you move to manage them (as quoted in the glossary). [2]

In games:

- **[Catan](../games/catan.md)**: Five resources come from dice rolls on the hexes you border, and every road, settlement and city costs a set mix of them. [3]
- **[Civilization](../games/civilization.md)**: Each city turns its land into food, production and gold, and the player chooses what each city works on. [4]
- **[Data Center](../games/data-center.md)**: Energy, water and money start at six each and are shared; the AI's nodes draw upkeep in energy and water. [5]

**Shows it** (GM's card): *Civilization*; *Catan* [1]

**With AI** (HI): The AI competes with people for the same energy and water (Bostrom's instrumental resources; Crawford's material AI). [1]

**In our game** (Nov 14 rulesets): yes: energy, water and money in Data Center and Normal Technology; money and energy in The Committee [1]

## How to

**Try it in two minutes** (GM's card): Each player gets ten paper clips. Every action costs some; the table sets the prices first. [1]

**Build it** (GM's card): Four meters on `/stage` (for example compute, power, water, and trust) that each action raises or drains. [1]
