# Cooperation

## Definition

All players are on one side, against the game itself, and win or lose together.

On GM's card: "Everyone wins or loses together." [1]

## Description

In games:

- **[Pandemic](../games/pandemic.md)**: The players race the player deck together; each is dealt a different role, and they win or lose as one team. [2]
- **[Hanabi](../games/hanabi.md)**: Players hold their cards facing outward, so each sees everyone's hand but their own, and must build fireworks together from limited hints. [3]
- **[Data Center](../games/data-center.md)**: The factions share the region and lose together if the AI takes half of it, but each also has a private goal, so the cooperation is partial. [4]

**Shows it** (GM's card): *Pandemic* [1]

**With AI** (HI): Humans cooperating against a system, while each faction's private goal pulls them apart. [1]

**In our game** (Nov 14 rulesets): yes: the factions win or lose together against the AI's share of the map [1]

## How to

**Try it in two minutes** (GM's card): The group counts to twenty; anyone may say the next number, but two voices at once means starting over. [1]

**Build it** (GM's card): A shared bar on `/stage` that rises only when every player hits their pad in the same window. [1]

## Why to

**Proposed, for Marlon to choose:**

1. A cooperative game lets a class practice coordination without anyone losing to a classmate.
2. Semi-cooperation (shared loss, private goals) is a model for committees and coalitions.
