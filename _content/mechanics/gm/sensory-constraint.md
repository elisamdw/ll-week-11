# Sensory constraint

## Definition

The game takes away or limits a sense (sight, hearing, speech), and play is built around the gap.

On GM's card: "Take away a sense and the game changes." [1]

## Description

In games:

- **[Marco Polo](../games/marco-polo.md)**: The seeker in a pool keeps their eyes closed and finds the others by calling 'Marco' and hearing 'Polo'. [2]
- **[Pictionary](../games/pictionary.md)**: Players must get their team to guess a word by drawing it, without speaking or writing letters. [3]

**Shows it** (GM's card): Marco Polo [1]

**With AI** (HI): Play as the AI with only its senses: a camera feed, a meter, a log. [1]

**In our game** (Nov 14 rulesets): no [1]

## How to

**Try it in two minutes** (GM's card): Lights out: finish the scene in the dark. [1]

**Build it** (GM's card): A blackout cue on `/stage`; players find each other by sound alone. [1]
