# Story circle

## Definition

Players take turns adding to a shared story, each building on what the last one added.

On GM's card: "Everyone adds to the story in turn." [1]

## Description

In games:

- **[Once Upon a Time](../games/once-upon-a-time.md)**: Players tell a story together using cards of story elements, each trying to steer it toward their own ending card. [2]
- **[Dungeons & Dragons](../games/dungeons-and-dragons.md)**: The players and the dungeon master build the story turn by turn, each adding what their character does. [3]

**Shows it** (GM's card): *Once Upon a Time* (storytelling cards) [1]

**With AI** (HI): Human and AI take turns adding to the story (AI Dungeon). [1]

**In our game** (Nov 14 rulesets): no [1]

## How to

**Try it in two minutes** (GM's card): Each person adds one sentence to a story; the last person must end it. [1]

**Build it** (GM's card): `/voice` transcribes each turn; Claude keeps the story so far on `/stage`. [1]
