# Incremental growth

## Definition

Numbers that rise by themselves over time, usually faster and faster, while the player's choices change how fast they grow rather than the numbers directly.

On GM's card: "Numbers grow by themselves, and the player tunes the rate." [1]

## Description

In games:

- **[Cookie Clicker](../games/cookie-clicker.md)**: Each building bakes cookies every second, and buying more buildings raises the rate rather than the count. [2]
- **[Universal Paperclips](../games/universal-paperclips.md)**: Autoclippers and later factories make clips without the player clicking, and the player's job becomes setting how fast that happens. [3]
- **[Data Center](../games/data-center.md)**: The AI's goal is one number in petaflops with an acceleration of 1.25, so it grows faster each round. [4]

**Shows it** (GM's card): *Cookie Clicker*; *Universal Paperclips* [1]

**With AI** (HI): The AI's growth compounds; the players tune its rate (Paperclips, Fizek, Kurzweil's doubling). [1]

**In our game** (Nov 14 rulesets): yes: the AI's goal number grows each round in every version [1]

## How to

**Try it in two minutes** (GM's card): Every minute, each player's pile of paper clips doubles, unless they spend some to change the rule. [1]

**Build it** (GM's card): Claude Code builds a one-page clicker on any theme: three resources, each one feeding the next. [1]

## Why to

**Proposed, for Marlon to choose:**

1. Show compound growth by feel rather than by formula: students watch a number run away from them.
2. Use it to talk about delegated play (Fizek): when the game plays itself, what is left for the player?

## Home game version

### In a regular classroom

**Proposed, for Marlon to choose:**

1. A jar that gets one bean per minute, then two, then four; students decide when to spend beans to change the rate.
2. A doubling chart on the board updated every round.

### Digitally

**Proposed, for Marlon to choose:**

1. Play Cookie Clicker or Universal Paperclips for ten minutes and graph the number.
2. A one-page counter built with Claude that grows on its own and has one upgrade button.
