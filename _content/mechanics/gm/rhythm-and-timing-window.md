# Rhythm and timing window

## Definition

Each input must land within a short window around a beat or cue, and the closer it lands, the better the result.

On GM's card: "Act on the beat; how close you are is the score." [1]

## Description

In games:

- **[Dance Dance Revolution](../games/dance-dance-revolution.md)**: Players step on arrows on a floor pad as matching arrows reach a line on screen, and each step is rated by how close to the beat it lands. [2]
- **[Guitar Hero](../games/guitar-hero.md)**: Notes scroll toward the player, who presses the matching fret and strums as each one arrives. [3]

**Shows it** (GM's card): *Dance Dance Revolution*; *Guitar Hero* [1]

**With AI** (HI): Can a human keep time with a machine that never drifts? [1]

**In our game** (Nov 14 rulesets): no [1]

## How to

**Try it in two minutes** (GM's card): Clap on the beat of a song; anyone off the beat sits out. [1]

**Build it** (GM's card): MIDI pads scored against a click track, accuracy shown on `/stage`. [1]
