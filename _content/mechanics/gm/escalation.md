# Escalation

## Definition

Pressure that rises as the game goes on: each round asks more, moves faster or hits harder than the last, so the game gets harder toward its end.

On GM's card: "Each round asks a little more than the last." [1]

## Description

In games:

- **[Tetris](../games/tetris.md)**: Pieces fall faster as the player clears lines, so the same placing decision gets less and less time. [2]
- **[Space Invaders](../games/space-invaders.md)**: The invaders speed up as fewer of them remain, so the end of a wave is the hardest part. [3]
- **[Pandemic](../games/pandemic.md)**: Each epidemic card raises the infection rate and puts the cities already drawn back on top of the infection deck. [4]
- **[Data Center](../games/data-center.md)**: The AI's growth multiplies by its acceleration each round, and the turn card multiplies it again. [5]

**Shows it** (GM's card): *Tetris* (the speed rises); *Jenga* [1]

**With AI** (HI): Each round the AI asks a little more; the question is whether the humans notice the slope. [1]

**In our game** (Nov 14 rulesets): yes: the AI's acceleration and its 'grow' cards [1]

## How to

**Try it in two minutes** (GM's card): Each line of a scene must be one word shorter than the last. [1]

**Build it** (GM's card): Each cue fires faster than the last until someone hits stop. [1]

## Why to

**Proposed, for Marlon to choose:**

1. Give a session a rising curve: the first round is easy and the last one asks the most.
2. Use it to compare the AI as a sudden turn with the AI as steady escalation.
