# Branching dialogue

## Definition

The player chooses what their character says from a set of options, and each choice sends the conversation down a different branch.

On GM's card: "The player picks what to say, and the conversation forks." [1]

## Description

In games:

- **[Mass Effect](../games/mass-effect.md)**: A dialogue wheel offers lines to choose from, and choices change how characters respond and what happens later. [2]
- **[Baldur's Gate (series)](../games/baldurs-gate.md)**: In Baldur's Gate 3, dialogue choices change companions' approval and the course of the story. [3]

**Shows it** (GM's card): *Mass Effect*; Choose Your Own Adventure books [1]

**With AI** (HI): Authored branches against an LLM that answers anything (Façade between them). [1]

**In our game** (Nov 14 rulesets): no [1]

## How to

**Try it in two minutes** (GM's card): Write a conversation on colored cards: one opener, three replies, and what each reply leads to. [1]

**Build it** (GM's card): Colored dialogue cards under `/draw-a-box`; the projector plays the chosen branch. [1]
