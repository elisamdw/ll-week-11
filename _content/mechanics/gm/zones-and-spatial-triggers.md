# Zones and spatial triggers

## Definition

Entering a defined area sets something off, so space itself carries rules.

On GM's card: "Stepping into a part of the room makes something happen." [1]

## Description

In games:

- **[Pokémon Go](../games/pokemon-go.md)**: Walking near a real-world PokéStop or gym lets the player use it. [2]
- **[The room is the board (prototype)](../games/proto-the-room-is-the-board.md)**: Each turn the AI spreads into the places in the room where no one is standing. [3]

**Shows it** (GM's card): Red Light, Green Light [1]

**With AI** (HI): Walking into a sensor's view triggers the system: surveillance as a zone. [1]

**In our game** (Nov 14 rulesets): no [1]

## How to

**Try it in two minutes** (GM's card): Tape three zones on the floor; each one makes a different sound when someone stands in it. [1]

**Build it** (GM's card): `/track/zones`: a column lights when occupied; moving on red costs a life. [1]
