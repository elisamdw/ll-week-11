# Rewind and do-over

## Definition

The player can turn time back and play a moment again, changing what they do.

On GM's card: "Go back and play the last moment again, changed." [1]

## Description

In games:

- **[Braid](../games/braid.md)**: The player can rewind time at any moment, and puzzles are built around objects that ignore the rewind. [2]
- **[Prince of Persia: The Sands of Time](../games/prince-of-persia-the-sands-of-time.md)**: The Dagger of Time lets the prince undo the last few seconds, such as a fatal fall. [3]
- **[Every tap is a row (prototype)](../games/proto-every-tap-is-a-row.md)**: The board is only the rows of taps replayed, so it can be replayed to any earlier moment. [4]

**Shows it** (GM's card): *Braid*; *Prince of Persia: The Sands of Time* [1]

**With AI** (HI): Rewind the AI's last move: the off switch as a game mechanic, and why it fails once the AI has turned. [1]
