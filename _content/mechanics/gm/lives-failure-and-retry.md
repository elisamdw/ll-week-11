# Lives, failure, and retry

## Definition

A way to fail that costs the player something (a life, progress, time) and sends them back to try again, without ending play.

On GM's card: "A way to lose that sends you back without ending play." [1]

## Description

In games:

- **[Super Mario Bros.](../games/super-mario-bros.md)**: Touching an enemy shrinks Super Mario or costs small Mario a life and sends him back in the level; losing every life ends the game. [2]
- **[Hades](../games/hades.md)**: Each death sends Zagreus back to the House of Hades, where what he carried home buys upgrades for the next attempt. [3]

**Shows it** (GM's card): *Super Mario Bros.*; *Hades* (each death is progress) [1]

**With AI** (HI): Humans retry; an AI that learns from each loss (self-play) gets stronger every time you reset it. [1]

**In our game** (Nov 14 rulesets): no [1]

## How to

**Try it in two minutes** (GM's card): Three lives each in a round of rock-paper-scissors; the last one standing wins. [1]

**Build it** (GM's card): Three lives on `/stage`; a MediaPipe zone you must not enter costs one. [1]
