# Deck building and the draw

## Definition

What players can do comes from cards drawn from a deck; in deck-building games players change that deck during play, adding, removing or upgrading cards that shape their later turns.

On GM's card: "Players build the deck they will draw from." [1]

## Description

In games:

- **[Dominion](../games/dominion.md)**: Every player starts with the same small deck and buys cards from a shared supply into it, so each deck becomes a different strategy. [2]
- **[Slay the Spire](../games/slay-the-spire.md)**: After each fight the player adds a card to their deck or skips it, and the deck is what they fight with. [3]
- **[Data Center](../games/data-center.md)**: The AI's behavior is a deck of ten cards, each a move tagged with the goal it serves, one of them the hidden turn. [4]

**Shows it** (GM's card): *Dominion* [1]

**With AI** (HI): The AI as a deck of readable cards rather than a black box; students build the deck. [1]

**In our game** (Nov 14 rulesets): yes: the AI's behavior is a deck everyone can read; students change the deck through Claude Code [1]

## How to

**Try it in two minutes** (GM's card): Start with five blank cards; each round, write one new card into your deck and shuffle. [1]

**Build it** (GM's card): A draw-a-card app with a "buy a card" step added. [1]

## Why to

**Proposed, for Marlon to choose:**

1. A deck is an easy way to write an AI's behavior that students can read, shuffle and edit.
2. Have students add one card to the AI's deck and argue for what it says about AI.
