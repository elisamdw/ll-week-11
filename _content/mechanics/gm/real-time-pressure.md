# Real-time pressure

## Definition

Everyone acts at once and the game does not wait, so speed and coordination count as much as the choice itself.

On GM's card: "Everyone acts at once and the world does not wait." [1]

## Description

In games:

- **[Overcooked!](../games/overcooked.md)**: Players prepare, cook and serve orders together in a kitchen under a time limit, and must coordinate closely to keep up. [2]
- **[League of Legends](../games/league-of-legends.md)**: Ten players, in two teams of five, fight in real time on one battlefield. [3]

**Shows it** (GM's card): *Overcooked!* [1]

**With AI** (HI): The AI acts on a clock whether or not the humans have moved (Fizek's idle games). [1]

**In our game** (Nov 14 rulesets): no (async play on a clock is proposed) [1]

## How to

**Try it in two minutes** (GM's card): Everyone sorts a shuffled deck by suit at once, sharing one table. [1]

**Build it** (GM's card): Orders arrive as timed cues on `/stage`; players clear them with keys. [1]
