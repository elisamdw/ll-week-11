# Alliance and betrayal

## Definition

Players make deals and alliances that the rules do not enforce, so any promise can be broken when breaking it pays.

On GM's card: "Deals that can be broken, with nothing to enforce them." [1]

## Description

In games:

- **[Diplomacy](../games/diplomacy.md)**: Players negotiate freely between moves, then write orders in secret, so an ally's promise is tested only when the orders are revealed. [2]
- **[Place to commit (prototype)](../games/proto-place-to-commit.md)**: The table talks, then every phone commits one token in secret, to defend a place or to the player's own project. [3]

**Shows it** (GM's card): *Diplomacy* [1]

**With AI** (HI): A pause or treaty between labs, enforceable only by trust: the arms-race dilemma. [1]

**In our game** (Nov 14 rulesets): no (proposed: a treaty with a rival lab that either side can defect from) [1]

## How to

**Try it in two minutes** (GM's card): Make deals for two minutes, then everyone writes a move in secret and reveals at once. [1]

**Build it** (GM's card): Orders written in secret and revealed all at once on `/stage`. [1]
