# Push your luck

## Definition

Players keep taking a risk for more reward, or stop and keep what they have; one bad result can wipe out everything not yet banked.

On GM's card: "Keep going for more, or stop and keep what you have." [1]

## Description

In games:

- **[Can't Stop](../games/cant-stop.md)**: Players roll four dice and advance markers up columns, rolling again as long as they dare; a roll that cannot be used loses the turn's progress. [2]
- **[Blackjack](../games/blackjack.md)**: A player draws cards to get close to 21 and loses the hand if they go over. [3]

**Shows it** (GM's card): *Can't Stop*; blackjack [1]

**With AI** (HI): One more model scale-up, or stop while it's still controllable? [1]

**In our game** (Nov 14 rulesets): no [1]

## How to

**Try it in two minutes** (GM's card): Roll a die as often as you like and add it up; a 1 wipes your total. Stop whenever you want. [1]

**Build it** (GM's card): Each press adds a point and a chance of losing everything; a second pad banks. [1]
