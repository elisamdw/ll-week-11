# Feedback ("juice")

## Definition

Every action gets an immediate response in sound, light, motion or vibration, which tells the player it registered and makes it satisfying.

On GM's card: "Every action gets a sound, light, or shake that makes it feel good." [1]

## Description

In games:

- **[Peggle](../games/peggle.md)**: Hitting the last orange peg slows time, zooms in, and plays Beethoven's 'Ode to Joy'. [2]
- **[God of War (2018)](../games/god-of-war-2018.md)**: The axe's recall has its own controller rumble for the catch, and its sound grows louder as it flies back. [3]

**Shows it** (GM's card): *Peggle* [1]

**With AI** (HI): Juice makes an AI's loop feel good even when nothing is decided (Bogost's Cow Clicker; Lantz's token test). [1]

**In our game** (Nov 14 rulesets): partly: the play page logs and narrates each event [1]

## How to

**Try it in two minutes** (GM's card): Play rock-paper-scissors where the room cheers every win. Then play it in silence. [1]

**Build it** (GM's card): Every cue also fires a light and a sound on `/stage`; take it away and play again. [1]
