# The spect-actor

## Definition

Spectators can step into the game and change it, so watching becomes a way of playing. The term is Augusto Boal's, from his Forum Theatre.

On GM's card: "Watchers can step in and change the play." [1]

## Description

In games:

- **[Twitch Plays Pokémon](../games/twitch-plays-pokemon.md)**: Viewers of a 2014 stream played Pokémon Red together by typing commands in the chat. [2]
- **[The audience layer (prototype)](../games/proto-the-audience-layer.md)**: The audience's phones show where the AI will spread, and each watcher has three nudges for the game. [3]

**Shows it** (GM's card): *Twitch Plays Pokémon* [1]

**With AI** (HI): The audience can intervene in the AI's run, as Twitch chat did in Claude Plays Pokémon. [1]

**In our game** (Nov 14 rulesets): no [1]

## How to

**Try it in two minutes** (GM's card): Anyone watching can say "stop", step in, and replace a player. [1]

**Build it** (GM's card): Audience phones or voices send cues that the player on stage must take. [1]
