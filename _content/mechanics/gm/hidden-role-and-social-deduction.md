# Hidden role and social deduction

## Definition

Some players are secretly given roles (often a hidden traitor team), and the others must work out who is who from behavior, talk and votes.

On GM's card: "Secret roles; the group must work out who is who." [1]

## Description

In games:

- **[Werewolf (Mafia)](../games/werewolf.md)**: Werewolves secretly kill at night; by day the village argues and votes on whom to eliminate. [2]
- **[Among Us](../games/among-us.md)**: Crewmates do tasks on a ship while impostors among them sabotage and kill; meetings vote to eject a suspect. [3]
- **[Private goals on your phone (prototype)](../games/proto-private-goals.md)**: One player at the table is secretly the AI, and at the end the table tries to name it. [4]

**Shows it** (GM's card): *Werewolf* (Mafia); *Among Us* [1]

**With AI** (HI): One player is secretly the AI's ally, or secretly an AI; the group has to find out (Werewolf for LLMs, the Turing test). [1]

**In our game** (Nov 14 rulesets): no (proposed: a player secretly working for the AI) [1]

## How to

**Try it in two minutes** (GM's card): Deal one secret card to each player; one card says "traitor". Talk for three minutes, then vote. [1]

**Build it** (GM's card): A bot sends each player a role by direct message; the room votes on `/stage`. [1]
