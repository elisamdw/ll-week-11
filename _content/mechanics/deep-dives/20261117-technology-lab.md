# Technology lab, Tuesday Nov 17

Marlon, Nov 17, 10:3x (typed, relayed by EL): "I want to have some LLUFs work with NFC stickers/cards as a 'technology' and discover the various mechanics it allows for, then think about some ways they could deploy this in a game 1) for the LL, like a workshop game/activty, and then 2) for a hybrid tabletop/digital game, potentially but not necessarily the AI 4x game we've been thinking of designing for stafford--they should at least TRY to come up with an idea for that one. Then some other LLUFs can focus on other technologies and mechanics. That will be today's activity." [1]

## The frame

A technology is a thing you do something with; a mechanic is the move it makes possible. Today each group takes one technology and finds its mechanics by trying things with it, not by reading about it. Then it puts what it found into two games.

1. **Try it** (the first half hour). Use your group's sheet: what's in the kit and ten or so things to try. Each time something works, or fails in an interesting way, write it as a move: "You can ___, and that changes ___." Those sentences are the mechanics.
2. **Game 1: an LL workshop game or activity.** Something a class could do in the studio for 15 to 30 minutes, at one station or across the room. Who plays, what they touch, what they learn.
3. **Game 2: a hybrid tabletop and digital game.** Paper, cards or a board on the table; phones or a screen holding what the paper can't. **Try at least one idea for the Stafford game:** the AI strategy game William Stafford's students play, take apart and remix on Monday, Nov 23 [3]. It is a 4X game (explore, expand, exploit, exterminate), the genre of *Civilization*, to be looked at critically as a genre [4]; the week's version on screen is at `/game` [5]. Your idea can be one piece of it: a way to deal the factions' secret goals, a map you tap, a hidden AI card.

## What you hand in

- **A deep dive on the technology**, on the [template](template.md), about what your group found [2]. One per group is fine; a second is welcome.
- **Two game sketches**, half a page each, in the form below. A photo of a paper mock-up counts as half of one.

### Game sketch

- **Name:**
- **For:** an LL workshop / the Stafford game / another hybrid game
- **Players and time:**
- **What is on the table** (paper, cards, board) **and what is on a phone or screen:**
- **The move:** what a player does with the technology, and what changes.
- **One round,** in three to five steps.
- **Mechanics it uses,** by name or id from the group's sheet.
- **What it teaches or argues** (for the Stafford game: what claim about AI or infrastructure the rule makes).
- **What you'd need to build it,** and whether you tried any of it.
