# Deep dives: how the fellows' writing comes in

## Proposed, for Marlon to choose

**A. A HackMD note per fellow or pair (recommended for today).** EF drafts a note from the template; Marlon creates it in the `ll-26-27` team and shares the link in the room. Fellows write and drop photos straight in, from any laptop, with no git. EF pulls each note into the repo when it is done. *Needs:* Marlon's go to create the notes (outward-facing); fellows on HackMD; photos uploaded to HackMD are hosted there.

**B. A Slack thread.** One parent post in a fellows' channel, one reply per deep dive, photos attached. Easiest from a phone, and the photos arrive with the words. *Needs:* Marlon to post the parent (draft below) and to name the channel; EF reads the thread as The Notebook Ensemble, which has to be in that channel; longer entries are awkward to write in Slack.

**C. A file each in this folder, or dictation through ED.** A fellow on an LL computer with the repo copies `template.md` to `as-written/<entry-id>.md` and writes; or talks it through and ED transcribes into `_content/mechanics/_dictation/`. *Needs:* the repo on the fellow's machine (or Marlon's laptop), and someone to commit; dictation needs ED and a quiet corner. Best for a fellow who would rather talk than type.

Photos in every case: until Marlon decides where stills live, EF saves them to the gitignored `_raw/mechanics/deep-dives/<entry-id>/` and the entry lists filenames, so nothing with a face reaches git by accident.

## Draft Slack parent post (for B; Marlon posts, not EF)

> Deep dives on mechanics and technologies: pick one thing you have had your hands on (the list and the questions are in the printout), write it up in about an hour, and reply in this thread with your text and two or three photos. One reply per deep dive. Hands, tables and screens in the photos rather than faces.

## When a deep dive arrives

EF does this, following [format.md](../format.md) ("Deep dives"):

1. **`as-written/<entry-id>.md`:** the fellow's words, unchanged, with when and how they came in. Nothing corrected.
2. **`<entry-id>.md`** (in this folder, `kind: deep-dive`, `subject: <table>/<id>`, `author: "LLUF"`): the same words laid into the template's headings. Words are moved, not rewritten; anything EF adds is marked `[EF: ...]`.
3. **Questions for the fellow** at the foot of that page: what is missing or unclear, at most five.
4. A line in the [pick list](pick-list.md) pointing to it, and three lines to EL.

Photos go to the gitignored `_raw/mechanics/deep-dives/<entry-id>/`.

No names in any of it until Marlon decides how fellows are credited.
