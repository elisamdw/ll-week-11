# Printed cards and index cards

## Definition

Paper cards for a session: blank index cards that people write on, or sheets printed with content and cut into cards.

## Description

From the record: index cards for the Stafford database exercise, Sept 28, 2026 ("Database construction with index cards") [1]; "colored cards" for dialogue trees in ENGL 189VG, Oct 26, 2026 [2]; and this week's printed idea cards, laid out by the reader's print views (`/ideas/print` on Letter with `?per=4|6|8`; `/cards/print`) [3], in a 5.5-inch column on 11×17 [4]. Marlon chose "abbreviated print cards that match fuller digital text" and no QR codes [5].

## How to

Blank: index cards and markers. Printed: open a print view, print, cut [3][4]; on card stock if the cards will be handled a lot (our suggestion).

## Why to

See [Cards](../ll/cards.md): the uses are the mechanic's.

## Home game version

### In a regular classroom

Index cards from any supply cupboard; printed cards on Letter from any printer [3].

### Digitally

The same cards as a web view (`/cards`, `/ideas`) [3].
