# Green paper

## Definition

Plain green paper or card stock laid under a camera and keyed out like a green screen, so that whatever lies on it appears over another picture.

## Description

Marlon names it twice: Nov 17, 07:13, "using green paper as a green screen... and the various things that makes possible" (voice agent's record) [1]; and Aug 8, 2026, in a dictation, the LL's use of the green screen horizontally with green paper as "a misuse of the tool, but it creates all these opportunities for creativity and collaboration" (machine transcript) [2]. Green paper is also something the LL keeps off keyed tables: a 2023 workshop plan sets out the art supplies with "(avoid green paper )" [3]. `Tested` is "No" because the record I read has no dated session this term using it; Marlon's Aug 8 words say the practice exists.

## How to

See [Green paper as a green screen](../ll/green-paper-key.md). In short: a sheet that fills the overhead camera's frame, even light, a chroma key on the switcher or in software (OBS's Chroma Key filter defaults to green [4]), and nothing else green on the table [3].

## Why to

Marlon, Aug 8, 2026: "creativity and collaboration that are not typically the name of the game when you think of digital compositing" [2]. The uses are proposed in the mechanic's entry.

**Proposed, for Marlon to choose:** what to stock.

1. Matte green card in 11×17, cut to the overhead frame, kept with the overhead tables.
2. Green paper rolls for larger tabletop scenes and puppet stages.

## Home game version

### In a regular classroom

Green construction paper from any supply cupboard, a phone above it, and a free keyer on a laptop [4]. (Our suggestion; not tested.)

### Digitally

Not needed digitally: a drawing app's layers do the same thing. (Our observation.)
