# 11×17 paper and the printer

## Definition

Tabloid (ledger) paper, 11 by 17 inches, twice a Letter sheet, and a printer that can print it.

## Description

Marlon, Nov 17, 07:13 (voice agent's record): "11×17 paper is the technology, and the mechanic could be sort of the map, the table" [1]. From the record:

- **The printer:** "the Ricoh (11x17, Tray 3)" [2]. The Notebook's `mk-printing` skill prints Markdown "by default 11x17, 2.5-inch side margins, Inter 10 pt" [3].
- **What the LL prints on it:** card columns "5.5" wide (a column of 11x17 vertically" (Marlon, Nov 15) [4]; "He prints on 11x17 (and Letter), cuts the cards, and uses them on tables and walls" [5]; the 11×17 mechanics posters, Nov 13 [6]; the Halloween "4:00 programme printed on 11x17" [7]; a workshop agenda written by hand on 11×17 [8].

## How to

1. Make a PDF at 11×17 (`node .agents/skills/mk-printing/scripts/print.mjs <file.md> --open` from The Notebook root) [3].
2. Print from Tray 3 of the Ricoh, at 100 percent [2].
3. For cards, cut along the 5.5-inch column [4].

## Why to

Large enough for a map, a poster or a place setting; small enough for one person and the overhead camera's frame. Marlon's words name it as the technology behind the place-setting map [1].

**Proposed, for Marlon to choose:** the LL's default sheet for handouts.

1. 11×17 for everything on tables, Letter only for take-home.
2. Letter by default, 11×17 for maps and posters.

## Home game version

### In a regular classroom

Most office printers take Letter only; two Letter sheets side by side make 17 by 11 inches. (Our note.)

### Digitally

A PDF at 11×17 opens on any screen; printing it is optional.
