# Microphones

## Definition

Microphones of different types (USB, XLR dynamic and condenser, lavalier) that pick up a voice differently, used both to record and to let people hear the differences.

## Description

The mic taste test (FRENCH 30 and 40, GENED 1140, ANTHRO 1826, in the Greatest Hits report, a model's synthesis of the HackMD record): "Students have conversations using different microphones", record with each, and describe them with adjective cards; prep is "3-4 different microphones (USB, XLR, lavalier, etc.)" [1]. This term: a "mic/performance" station in TDM 196SS, Sept 15, 2026 [2]; an interview session with "mics, interface, two cameras, ATEM" planned for TDM 155AI, Oct 28 [3]. TDM 119B, spring 2025, recorded the same text in "five distinct recording zones, including an audiobook booth" [4].

## How to

From the report: "Set up recording stations with microphones and headphones", record a short segment with each mic, listen back, describe [1].

## Why to

The report's reasons (a synthesis): "Demystifies equipment: Students discover gear isn't magic—it's choices" and builds the vocabulary to describe a choice [1].

## Home game version

### In a regular classroom

Phones, earbuds with a mic and a laptop's built-in mic make a three-way taste test. (Our suggestion.)

### Digitally

Students record the same sentence on whatever mics they have and post the files. (Our suggestion.)
