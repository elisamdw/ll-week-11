# Lipstick cams

## Definition

Miniature cameras, roughly the size of a lipstick tube, mounted low (in a table rail, for example) to show a close view that a larger or overhead camera cannot get.

## Description

Marlon, Nov 14, 2026 (voice chat): set up "a Texas Hold'em situation" with small cameras; "let's just go with lipstick cams" [1][2]. The game-state page's account of how television solved hidden cards, from the voice chat's notes and "not checked here": glass-topped tables, then "small 'lipstick' cameras in the table rail aimed at each player's hole cards" (*World Poker Tour*, about 2003), and now RFID in the cards [1]. The LL has not tested them.

## Why to

To show hidden information to an audience while players keep it from each other: the photo of a card table "can't see a hand" [2].
