# 4×8 whiteboards

## Definition

Whiteboard panels four feet by eight, mounted vertically, used as display walls that paper can be taped or pinned to and written around.

## Description

Marlon, Nov 13, 2026: "the large four-by-eight vertical whiteboards that we have in the studio"; "we have three of these boards, so we're gonna rotate through them", with "a large gray wall next to the boards" for stories kept after a board is retired [1]. Week 9's notes name a "dev board", the "main board in the main studio", a "notebook board" and a "database board" [2].

## How to

See [The story board](../ll/story-board.md): collect, print, arrange, record in front of it, rotate [1].

## Why to

The surface for "a core storytelling mechanic" (Marlon) [1]; the uses are the story board's.

## Home game version

### In a regular classroom

The classroom's whiteboard or a bulletin board. (Our note.)

### Digitally

A shared online board. (Our note.)
