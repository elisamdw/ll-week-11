## What changes the families

- The inventory's ten families have no home for how a game **tells a story through its structure and space**: items 5, 7, 8 and 11. They fit "Story, characters, and progress" without a new family, but that family then grows from 11 to 16. Proposed, for EL to choose: keep them there; or split out a family "Structure and world" (`narrative-structure`, `open-world`, `multiple-endings`, `spatial-story`).
- Items 10, 14 and 18 (physics, the input device, adaptive music) are what the course calls the tactile and sensory side. They fit "Space and the body", which has six rows and would have nine with `combat`.
- Nothing in the course material asks for a family on AI; the two Nov 14 families stand.
