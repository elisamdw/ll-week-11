# LL mechanics and technologies: the candidate list

Marlon, Nov 17, 07:14 (the voice agent's record of him, `W11/marlon/20261117-morning-mechanics-entries-nfc-and-the-color-agents.md`): "...having something that's sort of a thing we really frequently do in the Learning Lab... and then ways of achieving that elsewhere." An **LL mechanic** here is a move a person or the room makes in a session; a **technology** is what it runs on ("11×17 paper is the technology, and the mechanic could be sort of the map, the table", 07:13).

How to read the lines: **bold** is a working name, `the-id` is a proposed id, then what it is, then where the record has it. "Ran" means the record says it happened; "planned" means it was written into a plan and the record does not say it ran. Marlon picks, renames and adds; nothing here is final.

## The six Marlon named (Nov 16 and 17)

1. **The overhead view** `overhead-view`: a camera looking straight down at a table, so whatever hands do on paper becomes a live video feed for the room or a recording. Named Nov 17, 07:13 ("how it's valuable, which contexts you might want it in, and ways of achieving it outside the Learning Lab space"). Ran: SCRB 111 explainer segments, "a paper prototype + overhead camera" (HackMD: `Fall 2025 SCRB111 Workshop.md`, fall 2025); a workshop welcome, "use overhead to visually facilitate welcome" (HackMD: `Nonverbal Communication and Visuals workshop plan.md`, undated, a plan); Station 1 of the three-station video essay workshop (HackMD: Greatest Hits report, GENED 1145). Fall 2026: FYSEMR 68D looms, "students build looms, hammering in nails; overhead camera" (`NB/_context/projects/50-in-focus/ll-weeks/fall-2026/04-ending-20261002/HANDOFF.md`, Oct 1; outcome not recorded); draw a box, Nov 11 (line 19).
2. **Four overhead cameras, one per station** `overhead-stations`: the same camera multiplied so several tables work in parallel and each is a feed. Not yet used in a session on the record.
3. **Green paper as a green screen** `green-paper-key`: a sheet of green paper under a camera keyed out in software, so a drawing, a cut-out or a hand sits over any image or feed. ## More LL mechanics found in the record

7. **Station rotation** `station-rotation`: the room split into three (or more) stations, groups rotating every 15 to 30 minutes. The Greatest Hits report's most common format (HackMD: GENED 1145, AFVS 173F, the 2024 open house `20241010-open-house-plan.md`). Marlon, Nov 17, 05:12: the Stafford game "could be one of the stations" in TDM 155AI.
8. **Paper prototype under the camera** `paper-prototype`: make it on paper first, shoot it from above, adjust, shoot again, then go to software. Greatest Hits report ("draw, photograph, adjust = 2 minutes"); SCRB 111, fall 2025.
9. **Unpack the machine** `unpack-the-machine`: take 30 to 60 seconds of a finished work and map every move it makes. "Serial unpacking" (HackMD: Greatest Hits report, podcast workshops); SCRB 111: "we 'unpacked the machine' of the explainer video" (HackMD, fall 2025).
10. **Take apart a game** `take-apart-a-game`: the game version of unpacking: play a familiar game, then name its parts. Planned for the LLUFs and TDM 155AI this week (`W11/marlon/20261116-morning-games-active-learning.md`, Nov 16; `_content/activities/tdm155ai/take-apart-a-board-game.md`).
11. **The taste test** `taste-test`: try several tools side by side and describe the difference in words. The mic taste test (HackMD: Greatest Hits report, FRENCH 30/40, GENED 1140, ANTHRO 1826).
12. **Chalk under the overhead** `chalk-under-overhead`: chalk marker on dark paper as the overhead camera's surface. The LLUFs' note calls it "New Mechanic: chalk marker on dark paper under overhead" (HackMD: `Chalk Work.md`, undated).
13. **Multiplane layering** `multiplane`: flat layers at different distances from a camera (glass, paper, puppets), shot as one picture. The puppetry residency's "multi-plane camera rigs" and the "Godzilla" scene (HackMD: puppetry LLUF prompt).
14. **Paper dolls from the green screen** `paper-dolls`: a person shot on the green screen, cut out in Photoshop, printed on thick paper and used as a puppet. HackMD: `Paper Doll Steps.md` (undated).
15. **The studio screen as a shared readout** `shared-readout`: one big screen everyone reads at once: a reveal, a score, a timer. Planned: "Guess the category", fifteen terms shown one by one "on the screen in the studio" (Nov 16, 05:58, `W11/marlon/20261116-morning-games-active-learning.md`; `_content/cards/guess-the-category.md`).
16. **The secret on your phone** `private-screen`: each person's phone shows what only they may know (a hand, a role, a team). Marlon, Nov 17, 06:01: "a card for each position around the table, people tapping their phones there and then seeing whatever the secret information is on their phone". Proposed as the first NFC proof of concept; not run.
17. **The tap** `tap-to-move`: tapping a phone on a tagged card as the move that changes the game's state. Marlon, Nov 16, 17:31 to 17:34 (the card as the game object, its properties digital; `W11/marlon/20261116-afternoon-rationales-play-and-the-card-as-game-object.md`). Tags written and read Nov 16 evening (18:12 post); no session yet.
18. **Photograph the state** `photograph-the-state`: shoot a board from above before play and mid-game; the difference is the game state. Marlon, Nov 14, voice chat (`_content/cards/photograph-the-neutral-state.md`); planned for TDM 155AI (`_content/activities/tdm155ai/photo-to-game-state.md`).
19. **Draw a box** `draw-a-box`: draw a rectangle in thick dark marker on paper under the overhead camera; the computer finds it and plays a clip inside it. Built for TDM 155AI, Nov 11 (`TDM:weeks/10-20261109-orchestration/20261111-class-workspace/run-of-show.md`, "optional", "only if the overhead station passed the morning test"); whether it ran in class is not in what I read.
20. **The game show from the booth** `booth-game-show`: an operator runs buzzers, a countdown and music from the booth while a speaker performs. EXPOS 40's *Are You Serious?!*, board built Nov 9 (`_content/cards/primer-are-you-serious-the-home-game.md`); its home game is this week's build.
21. **Print it for the room** `print-for-the-room`: what was made on screen goes on 11×17 and onto the walls or tables. Marlon, Nov 13: "inventories of game mechanics that are printable and displayable throughout the space" (`_content/cards/game-mechanics-printed-throughout-the-space.md`); the card print view, Nov 15.
