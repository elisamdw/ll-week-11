# Annotate, then the model reads the room

## Definition

A session move in which participants work by hand on paper (annotating, sorting, writing), the LL captures the paper as images, and a model reads all of it at once to report patterns back to the room during the session.

## Description

From the LL's overview (undated) [1]:

- **COMPLIT 207, fall 2024:** "a full-class studio session where students annotated 3-foot-long physical printouts of the syllabus ... We captured the annotated syllabi and used the OpenAI Vision API to generate class-wide reflections in real time, prompting deeper discussion."
- **AI Tools for Language Instructors, fall 2024:** faculty hand-annotated "printed translations of the Harvard Mission Statement"; "Our team digitized the annotations and used AI to identify themes across participant responses. The workshop showed how AI can process large amounts of analog student work while preserving the pedagogical value of handwriting, translation nuance, and voice."

This term: the TF AI-comfort card sort, "12 table photos → CSV → chart" [2]. The Greatest Hits report (a synthesis) adds "Interactive Polling with Gen AI (live polls → AI finds patterns → discussion questions)" [3].

## How to

From the record: print large (a 3-foot syllabus [1]); collect or photograph the paper (tables shot from above [2]); give the images to a vision model with a request for themes or a table; show the result on the studio screen and discuss [1][2].

## Why to

From the record: "AI can process large amounts of analog student work while preserving the pedagogical value of handwriting, translation nuance, and voice" [1]; the reflections were for "prompting deeper discussion" [1].

**Proposed, for Marlon to choose:** the caution the entry should carry.

1. Students see the raw photos beside the model's summary, so they can check what it missed.
2. Students' handwriting goes to a model only with their knowledge, said at the start.

## Home game version

### In a regular classroom

Phone photos of each group's sheet, uploaded by the instructor to any chat model that reads images, with the summary projected. (Our suggestion.)

### Digitally

A shared document or form instead of paper; the model reads the export. (Our suggestion.)
