# Data Center

## Definition

The week 11 game: an AI spreads across a region's infrastructure while four human factions (the utility, the city council, the company, the neighbors) act in turn, each with a private goal; one hidden card in the AI's deck is the turn. [4]

## Description

**Made by:** The LL (GL, Claude Code), for week 11 · **Year:** 2026 · **Form:** LL-made

Mechanics it shows in this inventory:

- **[Goal and win condition](../gm/goal-and-win-condition.md)**: The AI wins when it holds half the region's nodes; the humans survive if the ten rounds run out first, and then check each faction's private goal. [1]
- **[Core loop](../gm/core-loop.md)**: Each round the AI draws a card, upkeep runs, then each faction acts; ten rounds of the same cycle. [2][1]
- **[Score and points](../gm/score-and-points.md)**: Public trust and present harms are tracks, and each faction's private goal is a threshold on one number (Energy, harms, Money or trust). [1]
- **[Timer and countdown](../gm/timer-and-countdown.md)**: Ten rounds (twelve in The Committee) set the length of play; the humans survive if the AI is still under half the map when they run out. [1][3]
- **[Turn order](../gm/turn-order.md)**: The AI's phase comes first in every round, then upkeep, then each faction acts in turn. [2]
- **[Incremental growth](../gm/incremental-growth.md)**: The AI's goal is one number in petaflops with an acceleration of 1.25, so it grows faster each round. [1]
- **[Escalation](../gm/escalation.md)**: The AI's growth multiplies by its acceleration each round, and the turn card multiplies it again. [1]
- **[Chance](../gm/chance.md)**: The AI's deck is shuffled, so the humans know what is in it but not the order. [1]
- **[Hidden information](../gm/hidden-information.md)**: The turn card is hidden in the AI's deck, and each faction holds a private goal. [1]
- **[Voting and the collective decision](../gm/voting-and-the-collective-decision.md)**: An AI proposal waits for the humans to approve or block it, until the turn card, after which its proposals pass without a vote. [2]
- **[Cooperation](../gm/cooperation.md)**: The factions share the region and lose together if the AI takes half of it, but each also has a private goal, so the cooperation is partial. [1]
- **[Roles and role swap](../gm/roles-and-role-swap.md)**: Four factions (the utility, the city council, the company, the neighbors) each have their own actions and goal. [1]
- **[Trading and negotiation](../gm/trading-and-negotiation.md)**: The company's 'Sign a data deal' gives the AI a node and the company money, a trade between a faction and the AI. [1]
- **[Resource management](../gm/resource-management.md)**: Energy, water and money start at six each and are shared; the AI's nodes draw upkeep in energy and water. [1]
- **[Territory and area control](../gm/territory-and-area-control.md)**: The AI spreads across fourteen linked nodes and wins when it holds half of them; zoning freezes and occupations take nodes back. [1]
- **[Deck building and the draw](../gm/deck-building-and-the-draw.md)**: The AI's behavior is a deck of ten cards, each a move tagged with the goal it serves, one of them the hidden turn. [1]
- **[Interruption and event cards](../gm/interruption-and-event-cards.md)**: The AI's card each round breaks into the humans' plans before they act. [2]
- **[Irreversible choice](../gm/irreversible-choice.md)**: Once the turn card is drawn, the AI's proposals pass without a vote for the rest of the game. [2][1]
- **[The treacherous turn](../new/treacherous-turn.md)**: One hidden card in the AI's deck, 'The turn', ends voting on its proposals and lets it spread and grow at once. [1]
- **[Convergent values on every move](../new/convergent-values.md)**: Each card in the AI's deck carries a value: acquiring land and drawing cooling water serve resources, training a bigger model serves cognition, building a backup site serves self-preservation. [1]
- **[Singleton win](../new/singleton-win.md)**: The game ends when the AI holds half the region: 'No faction can make a decision that changes the map anymore.' [1]
- **[Present-harms track](../new/present-harms-track.md)**: Present harms start at 0 and the game is lost at 10; a shortage adds one; the rule cites Cegłowski's point that you can lose to today's harms while fighting tomorrow's superintelligence. [1]
- **[Profitable harm](../new/profitable-harm.md)**: The company's goal is to end with twelve Money, and its best actions sell power to the AI and sign data deals that give the AI nodes. [1]
- **[Every rule states its claim](../new/rhetoric-field.md)**: Rules in the ruleset carry a rhetoric line; the AI's goal says 'The AI's goal is one number that only goes up.' [1]
- **[The not-modeled list](../new/not-modeled.md)**: Six items, among them the people who work in the data center and the chips, mines and supply chain behind the hardware. [1]
- **[Cascading failure](../new/cascade-failure.md)**: When the shared resources run short, a shortage adds a present harm. [1]
- **[Harden a node](../new/harden-node.md)**: The utility's 'Harden a node' adds backup power and water, so the node stays up through shortages. [1]
- **[Adoption only](../new/adoption-only.md)**: The contrast: here the AI's own deck spreads it, and one card is the turn. [1]
- **[Unplay, reskin, rewrite](../new/unplay-reskin-rewrite.md)**: The week's game page suggests ways into a remix, among them reskin, unplay and rewrite (Nov 14; remixing since moved to Claude Code). [4]
- **[Rules apart from fiction](../new/rules-and-fiction.md)**: Each AI card keeps its label and text apart from its effects. [1]
- **[Private metric and revise your metric](../new/private-metric.md)**: Each faction has a private goal on one number: the utility's Energy, the council's present harms, the company's Money, the neighbors' trust. [1]
- **[Write a faction](../new/faction-card.md)**: Each of the four factions is a goal and a short list of actions, the model for writing a new one. [1]
- **[Contested assumptions as settings](../new/growth-setting.md)**: The AI's acceleration (1.25 each round) is a setting in the ruleset that a remix can change. [1]
- **[Everyone or no one](../new/everyone-wins.md)**: When the rounds run out, each faction checks its own goal: 'did you win together, or did someone win alone?' [1]
- **[Nested loops](../new/nested-loops.md)**: A faction's action sits inside its turn, turns inside a round (AI card, upkeep, factions), rounds inside the game. [2]
- **[Multiple endings](../new/multiple-endings.md)**: The game ends either with the AI holding half the region or with the rounds run out, and then each faction's private goal is checked. [1]
- **[The move updates state](../new/the-move-updates-state.md)**: Each AI card, vote and faction action changes the saved state, which the play page keeps in the browser. [5]
- **[Growth and upkeep](../new/growth-and-upkeep.md)**: Each node the AI holds draws one energy and one water every round from resources the human factions share, while its goal grows by a rate multiplied by 1.25 each round. [1]

**In the LL:** Built Nov 14, 2026, for TDM 155AI on Nov 18 and William Stafford's SOC-STD 98to workshop on Nov 23. [4]
