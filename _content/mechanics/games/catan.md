# Catan

## Definition

A board game of settling an island of hexes, collecting resources by dice roll and trading them to build roads, settlements and cities. [2]

## Description

**Made by:** Klaus Teuber · **Year:** 1995 · **Form:** Board

Mechanics it shows in this inventory:

- **[Turn order](../gm/turn-order.md)**: In setup each player places a settlement in turn, then a second one in reverse order, so the player who placed first places last. [1]
- **[Trading and negotiation](../gm/trading-and-negotiation.md)**: On your turn you may trade resource cards with any player at any rate you agree, or with the bank at a fixed rate. [1]
- **[Resource management](../gm/resource-management.md)**: Five resources come from dice rolls on the hexes you border, and every road, settlement and city costs a set mix of them. [1]
