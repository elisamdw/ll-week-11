# Mass Effect

## Definition

A science-fiction role-playing game in which the player's dialogue choices and decisions carry across a trilogy. [2]

## Description

**Made by:** BioWare · **Year:** 2007 · **Form:** Video

Mechanics it shows in this inventory:

- **[Irreversible choice](../gm/irreversible-choice.md)**: On Virmire the player must leave one of two squad members to die, and that choice carries into the sequels. [1]
- **[Branching dialogue](../gm/branching-dialogue.md)**: A dialogue wheel offers lines to choose from, and choices change how characters respond and what happens later. [2]
- **[Quests and missions](../gm/quests-and-missions.md)**: Missions on the galaxy map give Shepard goals, some required for the story and some optional. [2]
- **[Narrative structure](../new/narrative-structure.md)**: Dialogue and mission choices branch the story and carry into the sequels. [2]
- **[Character creator](../new/character-creator.md)**: The player picks Commander Shepard's face, gender and class before the story starts. [2]
