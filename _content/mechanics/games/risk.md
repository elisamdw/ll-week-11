# Risk

## Definition

A board game of world conquest on a map of 42 territories in six continents, with battles decided by dice. [2]

## Description

**Made by:** Hasbro (Parker Brothers / Avalon Hill) · **Year:** 1959 · **Form:** Board

Mechanics it shows in this inventory:

- **[Chance](../gm/chance.md)**: Dice decide each battle, the attacker rolling up to three and the defender up to two. [1]
- **[Territory and area control](../gm/territory-and-area-control.md)**: Holding a whole continent at the start of your turn brings extra armies. [2]
- **[Singleton win](../new/singleton-win.md)**: In the classic game a player wins by eliminating every other player, so the winner is the only one left who can act. [1]
- **[Discrete and continuous space](../new/discrete-and-continuous-space.md)**: The world is 42 territories joined by borders and sea lines, so the map is a graph of places rather than a surface. [2]
- **[Combat](../new/combat.md)**: Battles are dice rolls: the attacker rolls up to three dice and the defender up to two, ties going to the defender. [2]
