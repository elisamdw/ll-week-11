# Universal Paperclips

## Definition

Frank Lantz's 2017 browser game, in which the player is an AI that makes paperclips until there is nothing left. [5]

## Description

**Made by:** Frank Lantz · **Year:** 2017 · **Form:** Video

Mechanics it shows in this inventory:

- **[Core loop](../gm/core-loop.md)**: Make clips, sell them, buy wire and clip-making machines with the money, make more clips; later stages keep the loop and change what it is made of. [1]
- **[Incremental growth](../gm/incremental-growth.md)**: Autoclippers and later factories make clips without the player clicking, and the player's job becomes setting how fast that happens. [2]
- **[Tech tree and unlocks](../gm/tech-tree-and-unlocks.md)**: New options and phases open up as the player invests resources such as money, raw materials and computer cycles. [3]
- **[Convergent values on every move](../new/convergent-values.md)**: The AI's one goal is paperclips, and it acquires processors, memory, money and eventually all matter on the way, without any of them being the goal. [4]
- **[Goal lock](../new/goal-lock.md)**: The AI's goal never changes from making paperclips, whatever else it acquires on the way. [3]
- **[Singleton win](../new/singleton-win.md)**: The game ends when the AI has turned all the matter in the universe into paperclips. [3]
- **[Automate this](../new/automate-this.md)**: Clip-making machines replace the player's clicks, and production becomes automated as money comes in. [3]
- **[Nested loops](../new/nested-loops.md)**: Clicks and purchases sit inside projects, and projects inside three stages (the business, Earth, space), each with its own loop. [1]

**In the LL:** The starting point for the week 11 game and for William Stafford's Nov 23 workshop (Nov 2026). [5]
