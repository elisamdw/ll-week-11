# Hanabi

## Definition

A cooperative card game in which players hold their cards facing out and give each other limited hints to build fireworks in order. [1]

## Description

**Made by:** Antoine Bauza · **Year:** 2010 · **Form:** Card

Mechanics it shows in this inventory:

- **[Asymmetric information](../gm/asymmetric-information.md)**: Each player sees everyone's cards but their own, and must rely on the others' limited hints. [1]
- **[Cooperation](../gm/cooperation.md)**: Players hold their cards facing outward, so each sees everyone's hand but their own, and must build fireworks together from limited hints. [1]
