# Cascade (prototype)

## Definition

A small grid where an overloaded place fails and pushes its load onto its neighbors: at once across a tight link, next round across a loose one. The AI's data centers draw more each round, a heat wave lands somewhere announced, and each round you harden one place and loosen one link. [2]
