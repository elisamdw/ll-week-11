# Super Mario Bros.

## Definition

A side-scrolling platform game in which Mario runs and jumps through levels to rescue a princess. [2]

## Description

**Made by:** Nintendo (Shigeru Miyamoto) · **Year:** 1985 · **Form:** Video

Mechanics it shows in this inventory:

- **[Lives, failure, and retry](../gm/lives-failure-and-retry.md)**: Touching an enemy shrinks Super Mario or costs small Mario a life and sends him back in the level; losing every life ends the game. [1]
- **[Discrete and continuous space](../new/discrete-and-continuous-space.md)**: Mario's position and speed change smoothly as he runs and jumps, so where he is is a matter of degree. [2]
- **[Tutorial and onboarding](../new/tutorial.md)**: World 1-1 introduces the first enemy, a Goomba, and the first power-up, a mushroom from a ? block, getting the player ready for the rest of the game. [3]
- **[Physics and tuning](../new/physics-and-tuning.md)**: How high Mario jumps depends on how long the jump button is held, and he speeds up smoothly from a walk to a run. [4]
- **[Adaptive music](../new/adaptive-music.md)**: When the timer drops below 100 the music speeds up, warning the player that time is running out. [1]
