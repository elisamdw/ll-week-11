# God of War (2018)

## Definition

An action game in which Kratos and his son cross a Norse world; Kratos's main weapon, the Leviathan Axe, can be thrown and recalled. [2]

## Description

**Made by:** Santa Monica Studio · **Year:** 2018 · **Form:** Video

Mechanics it shows in this inventory:

- **[Feedback ("juice")](../gm/feedback.md)**: The axe's recall has its own controller rumble for the catch, and its sound grows louder as it flies back. [1]
- **[Combat](../new/combat.md)**: Kratos fights at close range with the axe and his fists, and at a distance by throwing the axe. [2]
- **[Throw and recall](../new/throw-and-recall.md)**: Kratos throws the Leviathan Axe and calls it back to his hand, and it can hit enemies on the way back. [3]
