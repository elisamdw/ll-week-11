# Civilization

## Definition

A turn-based strategy series in which the player leads a civilization from its first city through history, exploring, expanding, exploiting and exterminating (4X). [4]

## Description

**Made by:** Sid Meier (MicroProse); later Firaxis Games · **Year:** 1991 · **Form:** Video

Mechanics it shows in this inventory:

- **[Turn order](../gm/turn-order.md)**: Every player and computer civilization moves its units during its turn, and the world changes only when the turn ends. [1]
- **[Fog of war and exploration](../gm/fog-of-war-and-exploration.md)**: The map starts hidden, and units explore it to lift the fog of war and reveal what is nearby. [2]
- **[Resource management](../gm/resource-management.md)**: Each city turns its land into food, production and gold, and the player chooses what each city works on. [3]
- **[Tech tree and unlocks](../gm/tech-tree-and-unlocks.md)**: Researching one technology opens the ones that depend on it, from pottery, the wheel and the alphabet onward. [4]
- **[Delegation card](../new/delegation.md)**: Workers can be set to automate, choosing their own improvements without the player's orders. [5]
- **[Any-map test](../new/any-map-test.md)**: Each game can start on a newly generated map, so the computer players must play maps no one designed for them. [2]
- **[Turn time](../new/turn-time.md)**: The computer players take their turns between the human's, and players expect to wait for them. [6]
- **[Nested loops](../new/nested-loops.md)**: Each unit moves within a player's turn, and the turns carry a civilization from before 1 A.D. toward the modern era. [2]
