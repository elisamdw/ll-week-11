# Monopoly with live rents (prototype)

## Definition

The LL's Monopoly on the table, with the rents moved off the deeds onto a screen and phones: printed, rebalanced by a designer between games, or rising with every landing. [1]

## Description

**Made by:** The LL (PB, Claude Code), Nov 15 · **Year:** 2026 · **Form:** LL-made

Mechanics it shows in this inventory:

- **[House rules saved](../new/house-rules.md)**: Rents on a screen can be rebalanced by a designer between games. [1]
- **[Rebalancing on the fly](../new/rebalancing-on-the-fly.md)**: The rents move off the printed deeds onto a screen, so a designer can rebalance them between games or let them rise with every landing. [1]

**In the LL:** One of the week 11 prototypes of game mechanics (Nov 15, 2026), for TDM 155AI on Nov 18, William Stafford's workshop on Nov 23 and the LLUFs. [2]
