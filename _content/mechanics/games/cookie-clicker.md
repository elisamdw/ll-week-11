# Cookie Clicker

## Definition

An incremental browser game in which clicking a cookie, then buying buildings that bake for you, makes ever larger numbers of cookies. [1]

## Description

**Made by:** Julien Thiennot (Orteil) · **Year:** 2013 · **Form:** Video

Mechanics it shows in this inventory:

- **[Incremental growth](../gm/incremental-growth.md)**: Each building bakes cookies every second, and buying more buildings raises the rate rather than the count. [1]
- **[Engine building](../gm/engine-building.md)**: Each building bought raises the cookies made per second, which buys more buildings. [1]
- **[Achievements and badges](../gm/achievements-and-badges.md)**: Hundreds of achievements mark milestones such as cookies baked or buildings owned. [2]
- **[Automate this](../new/automate-this.md)**: Cursors and buildings bake for the player, so cookies keep coming without clicking. [1]
- **[Come back later](../new/come-back-later.md)**: With the Twin Gates of Transcendence upgrade, buildings keep producing at a reduced rate while the game is closed. [3]
