# The intent (prototype)

## Definition

One player plays the AI, and only their phone says what it is optimizing for. Everyone else reads its intent from the moves it picks and passes over, and locks a guess; if the table reads it, the AI scores nothing. [1]

## Description

**Made by:** The LL (PI, Claude Code), Nov 15 · **Year:** 2026 · **Form:** LL-made

Mechanics it shows in this inventory:

- **[Hidden rule](../gm/hidden-rule.md)**: Only the AI player's phone says what it is optimizing for; everyone else reads its intent from the moves it picks and passes over. [1]
- **[Any goal, same ending](../new/arbitrary-goal.md)**: Only the AI player's phone says what it is optimizing for, and the table must read the goal from its moves. [1]

**In the LL:** One of the week 11 prototypes of game mechanics (Nov 15, 2026), for TDM 155AI on Nov 18, William Stafford's workshop on Nov 23 and the LLUFs. [2]
