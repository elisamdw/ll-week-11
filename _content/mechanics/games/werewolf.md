# Werewolf (Mafia)

## Definition

A party game of hidden roles in which a village votes each day to eliminate a suspect, while werewolves secretly kill at night. [1]

## Description

**Made by:** Dmitry Davidoff (Mafia); Werewolf variant by Andrew Plotkin · **Year:** 1986 · **Form:** Party

Mechanics it shows in this inventory:

- **[Hidden role and social deduction](../gm/hidden-role-and-social-deduction.md)**: Werewolves secretly kill at night; by day the village argues and votes on whom to eliminate. [1]
- **[Voting and the collective decision](../gm/voting-and-the-collective-decision.md)**: Each day the village votes to eliminate one player, without knowing who the werewolves are. [1]
