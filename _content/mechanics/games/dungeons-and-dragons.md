# Dungeons & Dragons

## Definition

A tabletop role-playing game in which players voice characters in a story run by a dungeon master, with dice deciding what their actions achieve. [1]

## Description

**Made by:** Gary Gygax and Dave Arneson · **Year:** 1974 · **Form:** Tabletop role-playing

Mechanics it shows in this inventory:

- **[Story circle](../gm/story-circle.md)**: The players and the dungeon master build the story turn by turn, each adding what their character does. [1]
- **[Levels and progression](../gm/levels-and-progression.md)**: Characters earn experience points and gain levels that bring new abilities. [1]
- **[Open world](../new/open-world.md)**: The dungeon master's world has no fixed route: where the players choose to go decides what they meet. [1]

**In the LL:** Named in the ENGL 189VG brainstorm as an example of loops and of open-world play (fall 2026). [2]
