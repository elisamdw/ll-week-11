# Pandemic

## Definition

A cooperative board game in which players with different roles travel the world treating outbreaks and racing to cure four diseases. [2]

## Description

**Made by:** Matt Leacock (Z-Man Games) · **Year:** 2008 · **Form:** Board

Mechanics it shows in this inventory:

- **[Goal and win condition](../gm/goal-and-win-condition.md)**: The players win together by curing all four diseases, and lose together in any of three ways (too many outbreaks, too few disease cubes, too few player cards left to draw). [1]
- **[Timer and countdown](../gm/timer-and-countdown.md)**: The player deck works as a clock: if a player has to draw two cards and fewer than two are left, everyone loses. [1]
- **[Escalation](../gm/escalation.md)**: Each epidemic card raises the infection rate and puts the cities already drawn back on top of the infection deck. [1]
- **[Cooperation](../gm/cooperation.md)**: The players race the player deck together; each is dealt a different role, and they win or lose as one team. [1]
- **[Roles and role swap](../gm/roles-and-role-swap.md)**: Each player is dealt a role card (such as the Medic or the Scientist) whose special abilities change how some actions work. [1]
- **[Interruption and event cards](../gm/interruption-and-event-cards.md)**: Epidemic cards shuffled into the player deck interrupt a turn and raise the infection rate. [1]
- **[Present-harms track](../new/present-harms-track.md)**: The outbreak marker is a track the players can lose on while they are busy curing diseases elsewhere. [1]
- **[Cascading failure](../new/cascade-failure.md)**: A city with three cubes that gets a fourth has an outbreak into every connected city, and an outbreak can set off another in a chain. [1]
- **[Harden a node](../new/harden-node.md)**: The Quarantine Specialist keeps disease cubes out of her city and the cities connected to it. [1]
