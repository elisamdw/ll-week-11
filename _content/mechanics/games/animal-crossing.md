# Animal Crossing

## Definition

A life-simulation game in which the player lives in a village of animals and pays off a house loan to the shopkeeper Tom Nook. [3]

## Description

**Made by:** Nintendo · **Year:** 2001 · **Form:** Video

Mechanics it shows in this inventory:

- **[Debt and obligation](../gm/debt-and-obligation.md)**: Paying off the house loan earns a bigger house and a bigger loan (Bogost's example, as quoted in the glossary). [1]
- **[Commitments track](../new/commitments-track.md)**: Each paid-off loan brings a bigger house and a bigger loan (Bogost's example, as quoted in the glossary). [1]
- **[Come back later](../new/come-back-later.md)**: The village runs on the real-world clock, so days and seasons pass while the player is not playing. [2]
- **[Mechanics tell the story](../new/mechanics-as-story.md)**: Paying off the house loan earns a bigger house and a bigger loan, so the game argues about debt without saying a word (Bogost, as quoted in the glossary). [1]
