# League of Legends

## Definition

A team game (a multiplayer online battle arena) in which two teams of five champions fight to destroy each other's base. [4]

## Description

**Made by:** Riot Games · **Year:** 2009 · **Form:** Video

Mechanics it shows in this inventory:

- **[Core loop](../gm/core-loop.md)**: Kill minions for gold, go back to base to buy items, return to fight stronger; the ENGL 189VG brainstorm names it as an example of a loop. [1]
- **[Real-time pressure](../gm/real-time-pressure.md)**: Ten players, in two teams of five, fight in real time on one battlefield. [2]
- **[Fog of war and exploration](../gm/fog-of-war-and-exploration.md)**: Players see only what their team's champions, minions, turrets and wards can see. [3]
- **[Rebalancing on the fly](../new/rebalancing-on-the-fly.md)**: Riot Games publishes balance patches every few weeks that change champions' and items' numbers, so the same game plays differently from one patch to the next. [4]
- **[Combat](../new/combat.md)**: Champions fight minions, towers and each other, and gold from kills buys the items that make the next fight easier. [4]

**In the LL:** Named in the ENGL 189VG brainstorm as an example of a loop (fall 2026). [1]
