# Monopoly

## Definition

A board game of buying property around a loop of forty spaces and charging rent, until all but one player is bankrupt. [2]

## Description

**Made by:** Hasbro (Parker Brothers) · **Year:** 1935 · **Form:** Board

Mechanics it shows in this inventory:

- **[Goal and win condition](../gm/goal-and-win-condition.md)**: The last player who is not bankrupt wins, so the game is won by ruining the others rather than by getting rich. [1]
- **[Debt and obligation](../gm/debt-and-obligation.md)**: Landing on another player's property means paying rent, and a player who cannot pay must mortgage or sell, or go bankrupt. [1]
- **[Trading and negotiation](../gm/trading-and-negotiation.md)**: Players can trade properties and cash in any deal they agree on, and trading is a key way to complete a color set. [1]
- **[Interruption and event cards](../gm/interruption-and-event-cards.md)**: Landing on Chance or Community Chest draws a card that pays, charges or moves you. [2]
- **[House rules saved](../new/house-rules.md)**: Putting fines on Free Parking is a common house rule (the glossary's example). [3]
