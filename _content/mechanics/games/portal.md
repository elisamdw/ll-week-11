# Portal

## Definition

A first-person puzzle game in which the player solves test chambers with a gun that makes linked portals. [1]

## Description

**Made by:** Valve · **Year:** 2007 · **Form:** Video

Mechanics it shows in this inventory:

- **[Puzzle and lock-and-key](../gm/puzzle-and-lock-and-key.md)**: Each test chamber is a puzzle the player solves by placing portals to move themselves and objects through it. [1]

- **[Tutorial and onboarding](../new/tutorial.md)**: The test chambers introduce one challenge after another, and the game's support is slowly removed as the player progresses. [1]
- **[Physics and tuning](../new/physics-and-tuning.md)**: Momentum carries through a portal, so the player gains speed by falling into one portal and flies out of the other. [1]
