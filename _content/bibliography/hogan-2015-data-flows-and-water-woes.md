# Data Flows and Water Woes: The Utah Data Center

**Citation:** Hogan, Mél. "Data Flows and Water Woes: The Utah Data Center." *Big Data & Society* 2, no. 2 (2015). https://doi.org/10.1177/2053951715592429.

**What it argues:** Writing after the Snowden revelations, Hogan takes "a new materialist line of questioning" to the NSA's Utah Data Center and argues that "surveillance and privacy can never be disconnected from the material infrastructures" that make mass surveillance seem normal (abstract, seen via the SSRC listing). Her case is water: the facility needs large daily volumes to cool its servers in one of the driest states in the country, so the cloud has a watershed.

**Why it's canonical:** It is one of the founding case studies of data-center materiality in media studies, widely cited and taught in modules on data infrastructure; William Stafford's annotated bibliography for SOC-STD 98to chose it for exactly this game.

**Key terms:** data-center materiality, new materialism, the water footprint of computation, infrastructure and surveillance.
