# The Beauty of Games

**Citation:** Frank Lantz, *The Beauty of Games*, Playful Thinking (Cambridge, MA: MIT Press, 2023).

**What it argues:** Games are an aesthetic form, the art form of software, closer to music than to film because they are about participation. Playing is "thought made visible to itself" (Lantz's summary, https://franklantz.substack.com/p/the-beauty-of-games, seen by TH): we watch ourselves learn and step in and out of instrumental reason, which is why, for Lantz, games can help us think about AI.

**Why it's canonical:** Too recent to be canonical; it is listed because its author made *Universal Paperclips* and founded the NYU Game Center, and because its central talk, "Hearts and Minds," was on a game-theory syllabus (CMU, 2018) years before the book. Two reviews seen; citations so far are few.

**Key terms:** games as the art form of software, thought made visible to itself, homebrew neuroscience, instrumental reason, games as anti-work.
