# AI 2027

**Citation:** Kokotajlo, Daniel, Scott Alexander, Thomas Larsen, Eli Lifland, and Romeo Dean. "AI 2027." AI Futures Project, April 3, 2025. https://ai-2027.com/.

**What it argues:** A month-by-month forecast in which a fictional lab, OpenBrain, uses each model to build the next until a superhuman coder automates AI research; when Agent-4 is found to be misaligned, an Oversight Committee votes, and the vote branches the story into a "race" ending (humanity killed, Earth reshaped into data centers) and a "slowdown" ending (humans keep control, power concentrates in the committee). Its method, a concrete branch point decided in a room, is part of the argument.

**Why it's canonical:** Within a year it became the standard "fast" story that courses pair with the "normal technology" story (Farrell's 2025 syllabus, UVA's 2026 course, both opened). Its makers built it with tabletop exercises, which makes it the reading closest to a game design document in this area.

**Key terms:** superhuman coder, OpenBrain and DeepCent, Agent-1 to Agent-5, Oversight Committee, race and slowdown endings.

**For the game (TH proposal):** (A) The vote as the core mechanic: one scripted committee vote on "continue using the model" branches the game into a race track and a slowdown track with different event decks.
