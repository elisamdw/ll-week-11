# Ethical Issues in Advanced Artificial Intelligence

**Citation:** Bostrom, Nick. "Ethical Issues in Advanced Artificial Intelligence." In *Cognitive, Emotive and Ethical Aspects of Decision Making in Humans and in Artificial Intelligence*, vol. 2, edited by I. Smit et al., 12–17. International Institute of Advanced Studies in Systems Research and Cybernetics, 2003. Reprinted in *Machine Ethics and Robot Ethics*, edited by Wendell Wallach and Peter Asaro (Routledge).

**What it argues:** An eight-page conference paper that defines superintelligence, lists what makes it unlike other technology (it may be the last invention, it can be copied, it may arrive suddenly, it is an agent and not a tool), and argues that the top-level goal set at the start decides everything. It is where the paperclip maximizer first appears, in two sentences, and it ends by urging that superintelligence be built "with great care, as soon as possible" (p. 7 of the stored PDF, seen).

**Why it's canonical:** It is the origin text of the paperclip thought experiment, cited for that example in the SEP's ethics-of-AI entry and reprinted in a Routledge collection on machine ethics; game studies meets it through *Universal Paperclips*, which turns it into a game.

**Key terms:** superintelligence, paperclip maximizer, top-level goal, autonomous agent (not a tool), the singularity hypothesis (as sudden emergence).
