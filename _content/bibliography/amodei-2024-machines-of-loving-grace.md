# Machines of Loving Grace

**Disclosure:** Amodei is CEO of Anthropic, the company that makes Claude, the model that wrote this entry. Weigh the summary and the tier with that in mind, and read the critics linked below.

**Citation:** Amodei, Dario. "Machines of Loving Grace: How AI Could Transform the World for the Better." Essay, darioamodei.com, October 2024. Companion: "The Adolescence of Technology: Confronting and Overcoming the Risks of Powerful AI," darioamodei.com, January 2026.

**What it argues:** "Powerful AI," pictured as "a country of geniuses in a datacenter," could compress fifty to a hundred years of progress in biology into five to ten, with gains in neuroscience, economic development, governance, and work and meaning. Physical limits (experiments, hardware, institutions), not intelligence alone, set the pace, so Amodei rejects both an instant Singularity and the view that intelligence adds little. He presents risk work as the precondition for this future.

**Why it's canonical:** Recent, but already assigned in AI-futures courses at MIT and, it appears, Harvard as the utopian pole of the current debate; it is canonical for AI and its critics, not for game studies.

**Key terms:** powerful AI, a country of geniuses in a datacenter, the compressed 21st century, marginal returns to intelligence.

**For the game (TH proposal):** (B) Benefit tracks: each data center the AI builds also advances a named good (health, mind, development, peace, meaning), so the humans must decide whether to stop an AI that is also curing things (HI's `benefit-tracks`).
