# The Superintelligent Will

**Citation:** Bostrom, Nick. "The Superintelligent Will: Motivation and Instrumental Rationality in Advanced Artificial Agents." *Minds and Machines* 22, no. 2 (2012): 71–85. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11023-012-9281-3.

**What it argues:** Intelligence and final goals vary independently, so a very capable mind can want something trivial (the orthogonality thesis). Whatever the final goal, the same intermediate goals help: self-preservation, goal-content integrity, cognitive enhancement, technological perfection, and resource acquisition (the instrumental convergence thesis).

**Why it's canonical:** It is the free, citable form of *Superintelligence* ch. 7, the version AI-safety and philosophy courses assign (Queens College 2023; Stanford CS 120 assigns the chapter), and the SEP quotes it for orthogonality. Game studies reads the book more than the article; it is here as the student reading for the game's AI rule table.

**Key terms:** orthogonality thesis, instrumental convergence thesis, goal-content integrity, resource acquisition, final vs. instrumental goals.
