# The Nooscope Manifested: AI as Instrument of Knowledge Extractivism

**Citation:** Pasquinelli, Matteo, and Vladan Joler. "The Nooscope Manifested: AI as Instrument of Knowledge Extractivism." *AI & Society* 36, no. 4 (2021): 1263–1280. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00146-020-01097-6 (online November 21, 2020; diagram and essay first posted at nooscope.ai). Revised version in *Interface Critique* 3 (2021): 37–68.

**What it argues:** A diagram plus essay, "a manifesto – of AI dissidents" (p. 40, seen), that maps machine learning as an optical instrument, the **Nooscope**: training data is projected, compressed by the algorithm, and diffracted onto the world through a statistical model (Interface Critique version, seen). AI is not an alien mind but a knowledge instrument, and its labeling, dataset building, and **ghost work** are human labor; the project to mechanize reason has turned into "a corporate regime of knowledge extractivism and epistemic colonialism" (Interface Critique 3, p. 40, seen). Its faults are those of a statistical instrument, among them "the undetection of the new" (section title in the abstract, seen via Crossref).

**Why it's canonical:** It is a widely cited critical map of machine learning from media theory, open access, republished within a year, cited a couple of hundred times, and assigned in Stafford's course the month before our workshop.

**Key terms:** Nooscope, knowledge extractivism, epistemic colonialism, training data as social origin, statistical model, ghost work, undetection of the new, adversarial intelligence.

**For the game (TH proposal):** (B, debrief) The AI cannot grow on electricity alone: every build costs a "data" card taken from a human faction's hand (their writing, labels, faces), and the debrief lays the taken cards out to show what the machine was made of.
