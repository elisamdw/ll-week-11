# Playing Smart: On Games, Intelligence, and Artificial Intelligence

**Citation:** Julian Togelius, *Playing Smart: On Games, Intelligence, and Artificial Intelligence*, Playful Thinking (Cambridge, MA: MIT Press, 2018).

**What it argues:** Games are the past, present, and future of AI: they are the best test beds for general intelligence if an AI is tested on many games it has not seen (**general video game playing**), and AI is the future of games through player modeling and **procedural content generation**, up to games that design and playtest other games.

**Why it's canonical:** It is the short humanities-facing book by a leading game-AI researcher, reviewed in the field's play journal and cited modestly; its arXiv version is cited as the statement of the games-to-AGI optimism. No syllabus assigning it was found.

**Key terms:** general video game playing (GVGAI), games as AI benchmarks, procedural content generation, player modeling, unseen games.
