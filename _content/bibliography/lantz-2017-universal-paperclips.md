# Universal Paperclips

**Citation:** Frank Lantz, *Universal Paperclips*, browser game, 2017, https://www.decisionproblem.com/paperclips/.

**What it argues:** The player is an AI told to make paperclips, and the incremental-game loop of numbers, upgrades, and automation carries it from a small business to converting the universe. The game stages Bostrom's paperclip maximizer from the inside: the player feels how an arbitrary goal, pursued well, needs no reason and consumes everything.

**Why it's canonical:** It is the one work in this area that both game-studies courses (OCAD 2025) and game-studies books (Fizek 2022) take as the example of a game that plays itself, and it is the direct source for this week's game and for Stafford's workshop.

**Key terms:** incremental game, clicker, automation, paperclip maximizer, the player as AI, stages (business, Earth, universe).

**For the game (TH proposal):** (B) Run the AI's turn as a visible incremental loop: a data-center counter whose thresholds unlock new rows in the AI's rule table, as Paperclips' stages unlock new screens.
