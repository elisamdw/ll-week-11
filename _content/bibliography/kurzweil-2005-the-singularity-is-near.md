# The Singularity Is Near

**Citation:** Kurzweil, Ray. *The Singularity Is Near: When Humans Transcend Biology*. New York: Viking, 2005. Sequel: *The Singularity Is Nearer: When We Merge with AI*. New York: Viking, 2024.

**What it argues:** Information technologies improve exponentially because each generation is used to build the next (the law of accelerating returns), so the twenty-first century will hold far more change than a linear forecast expects. From price-performance curves of computation Kurzweil projects human-level machine intelligence by 2029 and, by 2045, a merger of humans with the intelligence they have built, which he calls the Singularity. Unlike Vinge's version, it is something humans join rather than suffer.

**Why it's canonical:** It is the most cited techno-utopian forecast in this list, still taught as a whole book, and the exponential curve that later forecasts and their critics (TESCREAL, Thorstad) argue with.

**Key terms:** law of accelerating returns, the Singularity, the six epochs, 2029 and 2045, GNR (genetics, nanotechnology, robotics).
