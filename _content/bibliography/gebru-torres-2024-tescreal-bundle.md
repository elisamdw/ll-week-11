# The TESCREAL Bundle

**Citation:** Gebru, Timnit, and Émile P. Torres. "The TESCREAL Bundle: Eugenics and the Promise of Utopia through Artificial General Intelligence." *First Monday* 29, no. 4 (2024). https://doi.org/10.5210/fm.v29i4.13636.

**What it argues:** The drive to build AGI is ideological: seven overlapping movements (transhumanism, Extropianism, singularitarianism, cosmism, Rationalism, Effective Altruism, longtermism), traced to twentieth-century eugenics, share an eschatology in which utopia and apocalypse are two faces of one technology. An undefined system like "AGI" cannot be safety-tested the way a scoped tool can, and the race concentrates power while harming people now; the alternative is well-scoped systems with stated operating conditions.

**Why it's canonical:** It is the most cited critique of AI doom-and-utopia talk as one ideology (274 citing works on OpenAlex) and is assigned as the "beyond the hype" counterweight even in an AI-safety course (Stanford CS 120, 2025). Game studies has not taken it up; it is here because the game's premise is the bundle's apocalypse.

**Key terms:** TESCREAL, AGI as unscoped system, eschatology (utopian and apocalyptic), longtermism, effective accelerationism, evading accountability.
