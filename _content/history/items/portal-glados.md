# GLaDOS and *Portal* (2007, 2011)

Part of [Strand 3: Games about AI](../3-games-about-ai.md) · [Timeline](../timeline.md)

**Released** October 10, 2007, by Valve, in *The Orange Box*. Designer Kim Swift; writers Erik Wolpaw and Chet Faliszek. GLaDOS was created by Wolpaw and Swift and is voiced by Ellen McLain; Jonathan Coulton wrote the closing song, "Still Alive".

**The AI.** GLaDOS (Genetic Lifeform and Disk Operating System) runs the Aperture Science test chambers, guides the player through them, and promises cake. She has gone rogue and killed the scientists. Wolpaw found the voice while writing lines with text-to-speech software during his work on *Psychonauts*; McLain imitated a speech synthesizer, and her voice was processed to sound more robotic. Her part grew because playtesters liked her.

***Portal 2*** (April 19, 2011) adds Wheatley (Stephen Merchant), a "personality core" built to dampen GLaDOS's intelligence, who takes over the facility and proves dangerously incompetent. It also reveals that GLaDOS was digitized from a human woman, Caroline, the assistant of the company's founder, Cave Johnson (J. K. Simmons).

**Why it matters.** GLaDOS is the most recognized AI character in games: an AI who gives the orders, lies about the reward, and is funny about it. Wheatley is the useful counter-image for 2026: the danger of an AI that is stupid and powerful rather than smart and malicious. Both are a lesson in tone: an AI villain can be a comic voice and still carry the whole argument of a game.
