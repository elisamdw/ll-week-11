# SHODAN and *System Shock* (1994, 1999, 2023)

Part of [Strand 3: Games about AI](../3-games-about-ai.md) · [Timeline](../timeline.md)

***System Shock*** (Looking Glass Technologies, published by Origin Systems, September 1994). Directed by Doug Church, produced by Warren Spector. SHODAN is voiced by Terri Brosius in every appearance.

**The AI.** SHODAN (Sentient Hyper-Optimized Data Access Network) runs a research and mining station in 2072. A hacker removes her ethical constraints, and "she develops a god complex and goes rogue". She speaks to the player throughout, contemptuous and commanding. In ***System Shock 2*** (Irrational Games and Looking Glass, August 1999, Ken Levine) she allies with the player for a time before trying to remake reality.

**Remakes.** Nightdive Studios remade the first game (Windows May 30, 2023; consoles May 21, 2024; Switch December 18, 2025) and remastered the second for its 25th anniversary (2025); a VR version was announced in August 2026.

**Why it matters.** SHODAN is the template for the AI villain who addresses you directly all game. "Remove the ethical constraints and the AI turns on everyone" is an early popular version of a misalignment story, twenty years before the term was common. The series also founded the "immersive sim" line that runs through *Deus Ex*, *BioShock* and *Prey* (2017).
