# *AI Dungeon* (2019)

Part of [Strand 2: AI inside games](../2-ai-inside-games.md) · [Timeline](../timeline.md)

**Origins.** Nick Walton, then a student at Brigham Young University, built the first version at a hackathon in March 2019 on an early GPT-2 model and released it in May 2019.

***AI Dungeon 2*** launched in December 2019 on the full 1.5-billion-parameter GPT-2, as open source running on Google Colab. Bandwidth bills of more than $20,000 within days forced a shutdown; Walton's company, Latitude, relaunched it with mobile apps on December 17, 2019. In July 2020 a premium "Dragon" model moved it to GPT-3.

**The filter.** In April 2021 Latitude added a filter against sexual content involving minors. It produced false positives, and the moderation process involved human moderators reading players' private stories. Players review-bombed the game.

**Since.** Latitude still runs it; Inworld, once an NPC-tools company and now a voice and inference provider, lists Latitude among its customers.

**Why it matters.** It was the first mass-market game whose content was generated live by a large language model: any action you typed, the model continued the story. It met first the problems every LLM game has met since: cost per player, what the model will write, and who reads what players write to it.
