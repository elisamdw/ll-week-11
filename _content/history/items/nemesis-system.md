# *Shadow of Mordor*'s Nemesis system (2014)

Part of [Strand 2: AI inside games](../2-ai-inside-games.md) · [Timeline](../timeline.md)

***Middle-earth: Shadow of Mordor*** (Monolith Productions, published by Warner Bros. Interactive) came out September 30, 2014 in North America on PS4, Windows and Xbox One.

**The system.** Every Uruk orc is tracked as an individual. An orc that kills you, or survives a fight with you, is promoted to captain, grows stronger, remembers what happened, and may come looking for revenge. A player's death can be shared online as a Vendetta mission for a friend to avenge.

**The patent.** US 10,926,179 B2, "Nemesis characters, nemesis forts, social vendettas and followers in computer games", assigned to Warner Bros. Entertainment: priority March 26, 2015, filed March 25, 2016, granted February 23, 2021, anticipated expiration August 11, 2036 (Google Patents). No game outside the series has used the system.

**Why it matters.** It showed that NPC memory and relationships can produce emergent stories in a big commercial game, and it showed a game-AI design being fenced off by a patent. It is a good discussion point next to LLM characters that "remember" you: memory is what makes an enemy a character.
