> Addition: this is something to think about for our various looping agents. We know of necessity that we need to do some turn-based things with APIs. They can stream back, and there can be the illusion of living in a continuous real-world space, but it's just a bunch of overlapping, quantized, discrete, turn-based things. At some point, with enough enveloping, nested, recursive turn-based things, we could produce the illusion that the agent you're talking to, or the game you're playing, is more human-like or more always-on.
