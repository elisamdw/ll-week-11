**What it is.** A two-player race dilemma, built by PE on November 15 at `/proto/economy/the-race`. Two labs race to a capability of 14, and a shared clock counts toward the ending nobody wants.

**How it plays.** Each round each lab chooses in secret: *Safe* (1 capability, 2 safety work), *Balanced* (2 and 1) or *Sprint* (3 and 0), on separate keys of one keyboard or against the computer. Capability moves a lab toward the finish and moves the clock; safety work moves the clock back. Every round capability is worth 10% more and carries 15% more risk. If the clock reaches 20, everyone loses. Either lab can propose a treaty that caps both at *Safe*; inspectors catch anything more 60% of the time, and a caught lab's gain leaks to its rival. Two to four minutes.

**What it does for learning.** In PE's simulation, *Safe* against *Safe* finishes together in round 10; *Sprint* against *Safe* wins in round 5; two sprinters run out the clock in round 4. Against a careful rival, pushing wins; when both push, nobody does, and neither lab has to want the bad ending. The treaty makes the careful outcome possible, but only as reliable as its inspection. PE's readings: *AI 2027*'s race between labs; Vinge's argument that a ban in one place speeds the work elsewhere (the leak); Yudkowsky and Soares's proposal for a treaty on compute; Chun's *Control and Freedom*, which the mechanics inventory pairs with the compute cap, on turning political problems into technical ones such as an inspection rate. PE names what it leaves out: two labs only, safety work always works, and winning is good for the winner.

**Which of Marlon's ideas it serves.** No words of Marlon's are on record about it. CM's reading: it puts pressure behind the speed setting that separates the AI game's two versions, which is why SW's *Play Data Center* card points to it, and its escalating clock is the "apocalypse" side of the week's road into week 12. PE's proposals for the AI game: a rival lab spreading from the far edge of the *Data Center* map, the clock as a printed track, inspections for *The Committee*.
