> Addition: part of this, in agent and harness engineering, is knowledge and text engineering.
> • MK wants to think of writing as less what he's doing; more what he's doing is knowledge and text engineering, and systems engineering, obviously.
> • If we need a name for the product, it's still going to be text or knowledge, but writing isn't the process that produces it. There's speaking, thinking, mapping, designing.
> • De-emphasize writing as a term, or think of writing as always already involving all those things, but now happening in places that are not literally pen and paper.

Voice Claude's summary of Marlon's words, Nov 15, about 11:08, in the thread "Idea: harness and agent engineering," #mk-work-in-progress.

A description of his own week, as CI reads it: he dictates, agents draft, he steers, and the result is a folder of text that no one sat down and wrote. Calling that "writing" hides most of the work; calling it engineering puts the weight on structure, sources and alignment.

The phrase "always already" leaves a second reading open, which he offers himself: writing was never only pen and paper, and these are its new places.
