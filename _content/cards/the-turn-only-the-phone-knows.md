GL's game idea, Nov 14, from Marlon's request for a bunch of NFC game ideas and his direction that NFC mechanics are worth trying this week. Voice Claude had floated the same thing in the handoff back (section 6, not Marlon's): "the hidden turn card as a tag only the machine reads."

Every AI card on the table carries a tag. Tapping one shows what the AI does this round, and only the phone knows which card is the treacherous turn, so no one at the table can peek or stack the deck. It moves *Data Center*'s hidden turn card (Bostrom's treacherous turn as hidden information) from a shuffled deck into a machine's keeping.

What it says, as CI reads it: the players can hold every piece of the AI in their hands and still not know what it will do. GL's test: tag ten index cards, and a phone page that reads each tag's id and looks it up in the game state. Waits on the NFC kit.
