GL's game idea, Nov 14, from Marlon's request for a bunch of NFC game ideas. Not his words or his idea.

NFC tags can be rewritten. When the AI takes a place, the game writes the AI's mark into that place's tag, and anyone who taps it afterward gets the AI's version of the place. GL draws on Wendy Chun's *Control and Freedom* and Bostrom's infrastructure profusion.

What it shows, as CI reads it: a takeover you can't see by looking. The card on the table is unchanged; only a reader reveals that it now belongs to something else. Put on William's students' index cards ([Index cards that answer](index-cards-that-answer.md)), it would mean their own categories being quietly rewritten by the AI in the game. GL's test: rewrite one NTAG213 sticker from a phone and read it back on another.
