The card format names Luhmann's two kinds of link, `follows` and `links`. His slip box is online at Bielefeld, with its numbering, cross-references and keyword register. What does the archive show about how he linked cards, and how do Obsidian, Roam, Tana and property graphs model the same thing?

---

**Why the week needs it.** KG is proposing the architecture of the knowledge graph the cards live in (`_content/cards/graph.md`) and asked on Nov 16 for a sourced comparison of five models. The format cites Luhmann from secondary knowledge; nobody has read the archive's own account.

**What would answer it.** For each model, what a node is, what an edge is, whether edges are typed, and where the truth lives (files or a database): Luhmann's slip box, Obsidian (links, properties, JSON Canvas), Roam and Logseq (block references, attributes), property graphs against RDF, and Tana's supertags. One line each on what the deck could borrow.

**Where to look.** niklas-luhmann-archiv.de, help.obsidian.md and jsoncanvas.org, the Logseq and Roam documentation, Neo4j's Cypher manual and the W3C RDF primer, Tana's docs.
