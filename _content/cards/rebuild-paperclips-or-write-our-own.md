> I should research universal paperclips the game--maybe even rebuild it---or is it an opensource repo?

Marlon, Nov 14, 10:40, opening the game workspace (`_content/marlon/20261114-game-week-opening.md`).

GL answered the same morning (`_content/paperclips/`): the game's code is readable in the browser and mirrored on GitHub, but no licence has been published, so copying or porting it would need written permission from Lantz or Everybody House Games, and the mirrors have none to give. GL's recommendation: write an original game in its spirit, borrowing mechanics and not assets, and have students play the real one.

That is what happened. PE's [paperclip loop](paperclip-loop.md) (Nov 15) is written from scratch, and GL's list of what can be borrowed reads like a mechanics inventory: one arbitrary goal as the only score, incremental growth, trust as a currency, staged reveals as the scale changes, drift, a prestige reset.

Still open on the Nov 14 board: whether Marlon wants to ask Lantz for permission at all. CI's note: Marlon's Nov 14 voice-chat line about the game ("We're not trying to steal the game, we're just trying to understand how it's structured") points the same way as GL's recommendation.
