> a mix, but B has to be part of it

Marlon, Nov 14, 11:00, choosing among GL's three options for the game (board of the "Nov 14 · W11 game" workspace). The board records the rest of his answer in GL's words: with student agency (remix, tweak, start from scratch).

The options, from `_content/game/design.md`: A, *The Committee* (*Paperclips* played by a group); B, *Data Center* (a map of a region's infrastructure where an AI spreads data centers turn by turn, drawn from a deck, with a hidden treacherous-turn card, and human factions cooperating against it while their private goals pull them apart); C, *Two Clocks* (both at once). GL built B first, with A as its solo mode.

What "B has to be part of it" keeps, as CI reads it: the map. The Nov 3 proposal ("An AI that wants to turn everything into data centers… with humans fighting back") was already a map game, and the Nov 15 move to the 4X genre keeps it. What changed after the voice chat is how students touch it (Claude Code, not an editor), and on Nov 15 whether there is one game at all ([Playable examples, not one game](playable-examples-not-one-game.md)).

*CI's reading, Nov 15. Not Marlon's words unless quoted.*
