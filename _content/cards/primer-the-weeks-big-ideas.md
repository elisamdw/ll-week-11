Marlon asked for this set on Nov 16 at 14:00, to talk the LLUFs through the week's big ideas that afternoon. It has three parts: the key theorists in their own words, a glossary of the big terms (mechanics first), and one card for each of the big projects.

---

> for talking to the llufs this afternoon i'd love some cards for the "big ideas" of the week. So I'd like some verbatime quotes from the key theorists, a glossary of the "big terms" like mechanics, and then a card for each of the big projects = stafford, tdm155ai, are you serious the home game, home games period.

Marlon, Nov 16, 14:00, to MZ, relayed verbatim to KL (recorded on the cards workspace's board).

**How the set runs** (KL's arrangement). The cards print in this order:

1. **The projects** (cards 10 to 40): [the Stafford workshop](primer-stafford-workshop.md) on Nov 23, [TDM 155AI](primer-tdm155ai.md) on Wednesday Nov 18, [Are You Serious: the home game](primer-are-you-serious-the-home-game.md) for Terry's EXPOS 40 class, and [home games](primer-home-games.md) in general. Each says what it is, who it is for, when, and what the LLUFs could do for it this week.
2. **The theorists** (cards 100 and up): one card each, built around one or two quotations copied from a source a pane opened. The week turns on the pull between Nguyen and Bogost ([Nguyen and Bogost pull apart](nguyen-and-bogost-pull-apart.md)).
3. **The glossary** (cards 200 and up): one short card per term, starting with [mechanics](term-mechanics.md). It grows from [A little glossary](a-little-glossary.md).

The set's name is KL's pick from Marlon's "orientation or briefing or something better", and it is with him as a question.
