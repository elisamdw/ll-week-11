> And I should do some research on other games that could be connectable there.

Marlon, Nov 13, dictated, right after "a cross between Universal Paperclips and Civilization."

"Connectable" is the word to hold onto: not games to play instead, but games whose pieces could connect to this one. The research pane RS gathered about 45 on Nov 13 (`W11/research/stafford-strategy-games.md`), with five to start from: *Pandemic* (outbreaks cascading along a network, the clearest tabletop model of Perrow's tight coupling, which is on William's syllabus for Nov 16), *The Treacherous Turn* (players jointly run one misaligned AI acquiring compute), *Intelligence Rising* (states and frontier labs racing, with a facilitator), and *Microscope* (players write a world's history before playing it, which fits the students' index-card database).

Since Nov 15 the research has a sharper brief, as CI reads it: if the game is a 4X with AI firms as players and a critique built in, the connectable games are also the critical ones (Anti-Monopoly, *The Landlord's Game*, games played from the colonized side). Marlon filed deep-research tasks on those that morning.

RS flagged a hazard worth keeping on the card: freecivweb.org is now a gambling site; the Freeciv-web community server is freecivweb.com.
