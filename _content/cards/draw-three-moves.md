Marlon wants a more gamified AIBL Connect this week, and the site is already a deck: each use case is one move with a type, a time and materials. The smallest game it can host is the one Wednesday's class plays with mechanics: deal three moves at random and design a session that uses all three. A page on the site, and the same moves printed as cards, would let faculty and the LL play it.

---

> It's a great time for me to do the AIBL Connect site and level it up with more things that are the gamified things. That whole site is kind of gamified in reality.

Marlon, Nov 13, dictated (`W11/marlon/20261113-games-and-interaction.md`). On Nov 16 at 06:21 the voice agent recorded his direction as "Yes: a more gamified version of AIBL Connect is the direction" (a summary, not a transcript; permalink in `sources`).

**What it is.** A `/draw` page in AIBL Connect's interface that deals three use cases from `_content/use-cases/` (thirteen entries on Nov 16, plus the template and README), face up, with a fifteen-minute timer and a box for the session plan: who, how long, in what order. "Swap one" redraws a single card, as in the class's *Draw three*. A print set of the same entries as cards, one move per card, for tables in the studio. Nothing is scored; the game is the constraint.

**Who it is for.** Faculty in a consultation or workshop, who design with moves they would not have picked; the LL's staff, as a way to show what the library holds; and the LLUFs, who could playtest it this week beside the mechanics deck.

**What it reuses.** The class activity `activities/tdm155ai/draw-three` (the same mechanic, dealing game mechanics where this deals teaching moves); the decks-of-cards proposal in AIBL Connect's `_context/cards-proposal.md` (Oct 23: "one card per use case", the site and the deck from one source); and the interface's `/print` routes. It also tests CI's reading on `level-up-aibl-connect-with-gamified-things`, that a library of activities is a deck and browsing it is drawing.

**What it would take.** About three hours in `ll-26-27-secret/aibl-connect`, which is its own repo, so a workspace or pane there rather than here. Nothing public: the site isn't deployed and its entries aren't cleared, so the page stays on the laptop.

**What Marlon would decide.** Whether this is the first gamified piece or something larger comes first (unlocks, a tech tree of moves); whether thirteen entries are enough to draw from (KR's `which-game-elements-suit-a-teaching-practice-site` asks what the evidence says before badges or levels); and who works in that repo this week.

*KB's proposal, Nov 16. Not Marlon's words unless quoted.*
