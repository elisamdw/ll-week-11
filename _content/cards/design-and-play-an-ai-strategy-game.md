> We've gotta get ready for the William Stafford workshop where we're gonna engage students in the collaborative design and play of some kind of AI strategy game.

Marlon, Nov 13, dictated (`W11/marlon/20261113-games-and-interaction.md`). The workshop is Monday, November 23, in Stafford's SOC-STD 98to; the builds list counts it as build 2 of the week.

The sentence has two verbs, and the order matters: the students design and they play. Marlon did not say "play a game we made." That puts the LL's work on the materials for designing (a game that can be taken apart, a repo Claude can find its way around, examples of remixes) more than on one finished game. His Nov 14 voice chat sharpened this: the students get Claude Code and the game's repo, and "The UI is Claude code itself" (see [Claude Code is the UI](claude-code-is-the-ui.md)).

"Some kind of" leaves the form open, and the next days narrowed it: a cross between *Universal Paperclips* and *Civilization* (Nov 13, the same paragraph), and by Nov 15, the 4X genre itself, with AI firms as the players.

What it would take, as CI reads the week's records: a playable base game by the 23rd (the Nov 14 workspace's `/game` is a first one), a design move the students can make in the room in under an hour, and a way for William to keep running it after the LL leaves.

*CI's reading, Nov 15. Not Marlon's words unless quoted.*
