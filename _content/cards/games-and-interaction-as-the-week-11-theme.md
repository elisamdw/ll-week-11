> The week 11 theme is gonna be games. One big thing will just be getting ready for my class

Marlon, Nov 13, dictated (`W11/marlon/20261113-games-and-interaction.md`).

> Theme: games and interaction

Story of Week 11 canvas, as of Nov 2 (`W11/story.md`), where the theme is marked "locked; swapped with week 10 on Nov 2."

The week sits in Unit 3 of the term, "beyond the single work." Installations was week 10 and games is week 11, swapped on Nov 2. Marlon's two notes on the canvas frame it: games are complex, with many parts; interaction can be part of both games and installations (CL's and CD's cards).

By the weekend before, the theme had drawn in more than games, as CI reads the records: the Stafford 4X game, the mechanics inventory, game state as data, NFC and phones on the table, learning harnesses ("thought of as 'game' for this week"), the EXPOS 40 home game. What holds them together is the week's question about any of them: what does it let people do, and what does it remember?

*CI's reading, Nov 15. Not Marlon's words unless quoted.*
