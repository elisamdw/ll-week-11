The Stafford game is "a cross between Universal Paperclips and Civilization," and the deck already holds the critique: 4X trains a mindset, and an anti-colonial version is wanted. Which games have changed the four X's, and what do critics say they changed?

---

**Why the week needs it.** [An anti-colonial 4X](an-anti-colonial-4x.md) and [4X trains a mindset](4x-trains-a-mindset.md) are proposals without examples. RS's list (`W11/research/stafford-strategy-games.md`) covers the 4X classics and their open-source clones, not the games that push against them.

**What would answer it.** Five to eight games that drop or reverse conquest (restoration, non-violent expansion, playing the colonized side), with what each changes and one critic's or designer's sentence on it; what Mukherjee and Dyer-Witheford and de Peuter say a 4X would have to change.

**Where to look.** Designer pages and reviews for *Terra Nil*, *Before We Leave*, *Old World*, *Humankind*, *Spirit Island*, and indie anti-colonial games on itch.io; Mukherjee 2018; academic articles on postcolonial 4X play.
