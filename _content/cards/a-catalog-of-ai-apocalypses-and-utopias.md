> I'm going to research some of the theory behind the week (bostrom, bogost, other game studies and AI apocalypse visions)

> I want to research famous AI apocalypse/utopia films, books, tv, comics, etc. Create an md file for each with embedded media if we can find it.

Marlon, Nov 14, 10:40, opening the game workspace (`_content/marlon/20261114-game-week-opening.md`).

CX built it that day in `_content/media/`: 36 works, one file each, from *Metropolis* (1927) and *2001* (1968) through *WALL-E* and *Descender*, each labeled apocalypse, utopia or ambivalent (CX's readings), with official trailers, covers or interviews embedded where found.

Why a game week needs it, as CI reads it: the AI game is a story about AI ending or saving the world, and so are most of these. Students designing the game draw on images they already have from films and books; the catalog makes those images explicit and lets them ask which story their game tells. It also leads into week 12, autonomy and apocalypses.
