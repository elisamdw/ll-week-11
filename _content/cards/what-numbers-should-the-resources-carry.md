The AI game's mechanic [Every resource names a place](every-resource-names-a-place.md) asks that power, water, chips and land be tied to real sites. A game about infrastructure for a course on infrastructure should carry numbers students can look up. Which figures are citable, for which year, from which report?

---

**Why the week needs it.** *Data Center* is the game students play on Nov 23 ([Play Data Center](play-data-center.md)), and [See the data centers](see-the-data-centers.md) and [The cloud has a watershed](the-cloud-has-a-watershed.md) point at the same sites. Numbers in the game should come with a source on the card, not a designer's guess.

**What would answer it.** A table of resource, number, unit, place, year and source: global and US data-center electricity (IEA 2025, LBNL 2024), the largest AI campuses and their megawatts (built or announced), water use figures, where advanced chips are made, and two or three local conflicts (Memphis, Northern Virginia).

**Where to look.** The IEA's *Energy and AI* (2025), LBNL's 2024 US data-center energy report, Epoch AI's data-center tracker, company environmental reports, local reporting on Colossus in Memphis.
