Marlon, Nov 15, shortly before 13:39, starting this workspace (`W11/days/20261115/cards/shared.md`).

Three models, three kinds of order, as CI reads them. Niklas Luhmann's slip box: thousands of short notes, each numbered so a note can continue another and refer across to others, so that the box itself becomes a partner in thinking. Nietzsche's aphorisms: a thought complete in a few lines, standing alone, meant to be reread. Walter Benjamin's convolutes for the *Arcades Project*: lettered bundles of quotations and notes on one subject, gathered over years, in no fixed order. In this folder they became fields: `follows` and `links` for Luhmann, `kind: aphorism` for Nietzsche, `convolute` for Benjamin.

The card is also a test of [The truth is in the documents](the-truth-is-in-the-documents.md) with [graphs beside the docs](graphs-and-databases-beside-the-docs.md): each card is a document, and the frontmatter is the graph.
