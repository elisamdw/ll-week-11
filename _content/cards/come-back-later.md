> yes ultimately

Marlon, Nov 14, 12:05, on async play for the game (board of the "Nov 14 · W11 game" workspace).

GL's game idea, from the same afternoon, makes async play physical: the map stays on a table in the LL from Monday to Friday, the phone runs one AI turn a day on a clock, and each morning players read what happened while they were away, then move their pieces. It is Fizek's delegated play (*Playing at a Distance*) on a tabletop.

What it adds, as CI reads it: a game that plays out over a week in a shared room is also a display, the state of the world visible to anyone who walks past. In the LL's games week it could run alongside everything else, and the AI's spread would be something people notice day by day.
