GL's game idea, Nov 14, from Marlon's request for a bunch of AR/XR-with-a-phone game ideas. Not his words or his idea.

Fiducial markers (ArUco or AprilTag squares) are easier for a camera to find than a picture. Four on a board's corners give its plane, so virtual pieces can sit on exact squares. Week 10's research called a QR code or ArUco marker "the most robust option"; whether a given JavaScript marker library runs well on an iPhone was not checked.

What it would also give, as CI reads it: a square, known frame for the overhead photos in [Photo to game state](photo-to-game-state.md), whose pins land on the wrong spaces when the board is tilted. The same four markers could serve the AR and the game-state reading. GL's test: four markers on a Risk board, one marker library on an iPhone.
