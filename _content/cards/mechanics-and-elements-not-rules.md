> the design of the game is much, much more than the rules.

Marlon, Nov 14, in the voice chat, as transcribed by voice Claude (handoff back, section 1). In the same passage, having rules be "the one and only thing people change" is "perplexing" (his words, in voice Claude's summary).

He was answering the Nov 14 workspace, whose game had a rule editor and whose many proposals hung a field on every rule. Voice Claude's summary: he finds "rules" limiting; he has worked a lot with Jesse Schell's *The Art of Game Design* and its lenses, and thinks more in terms of mechanics and game elements.

Schell's elemental tetrad is mechanics, story, aesthetics and technology, each a place a designer can work; the lenses are a hundred-odd questions to look at a design through. A student told "change a rule" will change a number. A student told "change a mechanic, or the story, or how it looks, or what it runs on" has four places to start.

Voice Claude noted that Schell was missing from the bibliography; the handout TH drafted on Nov 14 (`_content/handouts/rules-and-mechanics.md`) now starts from this point.
