Marlon, Nov 13, dictated (week 10's `marlon/20261113-weeks-12-13-autonomy-archives.md`). The bracketed word was unclear in the transcript.

The workshop falls on Monday, Nov 23, the first day of week 12, whose theme he gives as "autonomy and apocalypses, doomerism and accelerationists." So the game is a week 11 object (a game) about a week 12 subject (AI running on its own).

By Nov 15 the same theme had come back inside games, as CI reads it: in a 4X, the world you build outgrows your attention and runs without you ([A hundred cities](a-hundred-cities.md)), and in *Paperclips* your own drones drift ([The drones drift](the-drones-drift.md)). Autonomy is something the genre already makes players feel.
