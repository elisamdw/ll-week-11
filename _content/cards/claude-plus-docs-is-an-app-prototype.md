> *Idea for this week (TDM 155AI):* something cool for students to understand, related to the built-out learning harness vs. just a bunch of documents and Claude as the harness.
> • Claude plus docs equals, essentially, an interactive prototype of an app. That design pattern is something we've been playing around with all term.
> • You could start thinking about Claude as the sort of everything app that lets you do everything. That's one of the ways we've been accenting it.

Voice Claude's summary of Marlon's idea, Sunday, Nov 15, about 11:15, posted as Process Black to #mk-work-in-progress. Seven additions follow in its thread (the game loop, Claude's turn speed, continuous and discrete spaces, agents as turns).

The contrast is between two ways to get the same behavior. A built-out learning harness, like the LearnHarness repo the learning-harness workspace is reading today, is an app: a front end, a database, agents on a schedule. A folder of documents opened in Claude does much of the same thing without being built: the documents hold the state, and Claude reads and changes them when asked. The folder is the prototype; the built app is what you make once you know what the prototype should do.

For the class, as CI reads it, the point is a design habit more than a tool. Before building an app, try it as documents and a conversation. If it works there, you know what to build; often it is enough as it is.
