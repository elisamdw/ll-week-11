Marlon, Nov 14, answering whether the game should be playable asynchronously, as recorded in the builds list's Nov 14 update (`W11/builds.md`).

On Nov 11 this was a fallback, in case the workshop could not run in person. By Nov 14 it was a direction for later. An async game also suits the material: Sonia Fizek's *Playing at a Distance*, one of the two game readings proposed for William's class, is about idle games, where the system keeps playing while you're away. A Stafford game that advances overnight and that students check between classes would be an idle game about infrastructure that runs without anyone watching.

Related cards: GL's [Come back later](come-back-later.md) (the board stays set up all week and the AI acts once a day), and the Stafford workspace's [async session](../activities/stafford/session-async.md) and [A round a day](../activities/stafford/a-round-a-day.md).

Open, CI's question: where would async play live? The builds list records it as open (the Nov 14 workspace asked whether a deploy is wanted). A deployed game is an outward-facing step that waits on Marlon.
