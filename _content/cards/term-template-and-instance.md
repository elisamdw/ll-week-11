A template says what every thing of one kind has; an instance is one particular thing made from it, with its own values. Every horse in Breath of the Wild has the same kinds of properties; your horse, with its name and saddle, is an instance in your save file. It is how games keep their state.

---

**Where the terms come from.** Programming's class and object: a class is "a template for creating objects" (MDN); "Creating a new class creates a new type of object, allowing new instances of that type to be made" (Python tutorial, §9). Sicart borrows the vocabulary on purpose, calling mechanics "methods." Lev Manovich's "Database as Symbolic Form" (1999) is the reading on the data behind media.

**This week.** The horse is Marlon's example, from the Nov 14 voice chat. In Monopoly, the property card is a template (price, rents); the deed in your hand with two houses on it is an instance. In the AI game, a card from the Stafford class's Sept 28 infrastructure database could be the template, and each node on the board an instance.
