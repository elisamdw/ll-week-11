4X is a genre of strategy game: explore, expand, exploit, exterminate. Civilization is the best known. The game for William Stafford's workshop is a 4X, and the plan is to tell students so before they play, so the map, the tech tree and the growth from one city to many can be questioned as conventions.

---

**Where the term comes from.** Game journalism of the early 1990s; the coinage is usually credited to Alan Emrich's 1993 preview of *Master of Orion* in *Computer Gaming World* (KP from memory, not checked). It covers *Civilization*, *Master of Orion*, *Stellaris* and their kin. The critique the week leans on: Dyer-Witheford and de Peuter's *Games of Empire* (2009), on games and empire.

**This week.** On Nov 15, by voice Claude's summary of Marlon's session, the Stafford game became a 4X, named as a genre and looked at critically. The same session went on to an anti-colonial 4X, firms as the players, and the argument the genre's procedures make.
