Data centers used about 415 TWh in 2024, 1.5% of the world's electricity, heading for about 950 TWh by 2030 (IEA). The largest AI campuses now draw from 370 MW to just over 1 GW each, in Indiana, Memphis, Abilene, Atlanta, Ohio and Wisconsin (Epoch AI's estimates). The water at the power plant is the bigger share of a data center's water. Each number below has its year and its source, so a card can print both.

---

**The answer to** [What numbers should the game's resources carry?](what-numbers-should-the-resources-carry.md), from a research pass on Nov 16; the full note, with a table of about fifty rows and every source URL, is `W11/research/data-center-numbers.md`.

**Power.**

- World: about 415 TWh in 2024, about 485 TWh in 2025, about 950 TWh projected for 2030 (IEA, *Energy and AI*, 2025, and its 2026 update). The US had 45% of it in 2024.
- US: 176 TWh in 2023, 4.4% of US electricity; 325 to 580 TWh projected for 2028, 6.7 to 12% (Lawrence Berkeley National Lab, Dec 2024).
- Gas turbine orders rose 70% in 2025, and 15 to 27 GW of on-site gas may power data centers by 2030, "mostly in the United States" (IEA 2026).

**Places** (IT power as Epoch AI estimates it, Nov 2026; estimates built from satellite images, permits and company statements):

- Amazon/Anthropic, New Carlisle, Indiana: 1,037 MW.
- Colossus 2 (xAI/SpaceX), Memphis: 946 MW.
- Stargate, Abilene, Texas (Crusoe, Oracle, OpenAI): 824 MW; Crusoe announced 1.2 GW.
- Microsoft Fairwater, Atlanta: 636 MW; Fairwater, Mount Pleasant, Wisconsin: 369 MW.
- Meta Prometheus, New Albany, Ohio: 471 MW of a 1 GW plan. Meta Hyperion, Louisiana: 5 GW announced, not yet operating.

**Water.** US data centers consumed 66 billion liters on site in 2023, and about 800 billion liters indirectly, through the power plants that supply them (LBNL). Google's data centers and offices consumed about 8.1 billion gallons in 2024; its largest site, Council Bluffs, Iowa, used 1,010 million gallons (Google, 2025 Environmental Report).

**Chips.** In 2022, chips newer than 10 nm were made "almost 100%" in South Korea and Taiwan (SIA and BCG, 2024). TSMC took a record 70.2% of foundry revenue in the second quarter of 2025 (TrendForce).

**Friction.** Memphis: 35 gas turbines photographed at Colossus 1 in April 2025, a permit for 15 in July 2025, 41 permitted at Southaven, Mississippi in March 2026, and a Clean Air Act suit in April 2026 (Southern Environmental Law Center). Northern Virginia and Greater Beijing together hold 17% of the world's hyperscale capacity (Synergy, Aug 2026). In Santiago, Chile, a court sent back Google's Cerrillos project, which planned to use 7.6 million liters of drinking water a day.

**Use with care.** Epoch's megawatts are estimates and some of its rows are projections; Meta gives two figures for Hyperion; xAI's and OpenAI's own pages refused automated access, so their numbers come from Crusoe, Oracle and Epoch. Prices change and campuses grow monthly: date every number on a card.

**For the game, as KR reads it.** A [GPU chip](gpu-chips.md) can carry Taiwan, a power token Memphis's turbines, a water token Council Bluffs; the ratio of plant water to site water is a fact students may not expect.
