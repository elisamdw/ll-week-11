> And I wanna start with ones where we need to have a workshop in a box, where we wanna take the resources that we would typically use for a workshop in our studio on AI, or on video essays, and we want to create an online companion to those.

> I would love it if a lot of them could be standalone, though I think that when we send graduate fellows out to courses to do things, they'd be working with these materials as their presentation tools, essentially.

> I wanna work from things that exist, and that includes all of the Markdown files and Google Docs that we've created for workshops in the past. And then recordings of workshops that have gone well. And then a lot of my dictation, ultimately, that I will use to fill in the gaps around the workshops.
