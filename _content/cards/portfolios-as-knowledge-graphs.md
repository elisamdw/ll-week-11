> also for portfolios--like knowledge graph for learning but also showing--again the collapse of summative back into formative (or just complete replacement of summative)

Marlon, Nov 15, 08:09, typed in #mk-work-in-progress, a few minutes after posting the LearnHarness repo there.

A graph that a tutor keeps of what you've learned is also a record that can be shown. If the record is rich enough, it does the work of a final exam or a final paper: the evidence of learning accumulates as you learn, and nothing separate is needed at the end. "Again" says he has argued this before; the week 10 cards hold a related note from Nov 12, "basic constructivist and all-formative moves," for the HILT talk.

Portfolios were already on his mind that weekend in another sense: on Nov 14 he dictated a plan for the LL's report with "portfolios of work by kind of theme, topic, medium, tool" (`W11/marlon/20261114-report-portfolios-workshop-companions.md`).
