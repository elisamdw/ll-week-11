# The convolutes

A convolute is a bundle. Walter Benjamin kept the notes and quotations for the *Arcades Project* in lettered sheaves ("A: Arcades", "N: On the Theory of Knowledge"), each holding whatever belonged to its subject, in no fixed order. Here every card has one `convolute:` (a slug from this page), and the bundle is what you print together and lay out together.

The letters are for the printed cards: a short mark at the foot, so a cut card can find its way back to its pile. Use the slug in the frontmatter, not the letter.

**To propose a convolute**, send KL (CL until Nov 15) its slug, title and sentence, and the cards that would go in it. A convolute needs at least three cards. This list is CL's first sorting of the week's material (Nov 15); the titles are CL's except where they quote Marlon.

## How to choose

- Pick the bundle the card would be **printed with**. A card about the paperclip loop that is also used in Wednesday's class goes in `paperclip-loop` and gets `audience: [tdm155ai]`. `class-session` is for cards about the session itself.
- A card that seems to belong in two goes in the one it argues with, and links to a card in the other.
- If nothing fits, tell CL. Don't file it under the nearest one and don't invent a slug.
