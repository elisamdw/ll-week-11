> we should have a little glossary now

Marlon, Nov 14, in the voice chat, as transcribed by voice Claude (handoff back, section 7). The terms that had come up in the chat: rules, mechanics, procedures, game elements, game state, template, instance.

TH drafted it the same day at `_content/glossary/README.md`: a plain definition of each term, who uses it and how (with sources marked seen or unverified), and an example from a game most students know.

The word "little" is the instruction, as CI reads it: a page, not a reference work. The first seven terms come from one conversation and each does work in the week. *Template* and *instance* carry the game-state thread ([Games persist state as data](games-persist-state-as-data.md)); *mechanics* and *rules* carry the handout; *procedures* is Bogost's.

The same morning, in #mk-work-in-progress, Marlon posted another set of glossary terms, for agent week: "unverified code, comprehension debt, intent debt, cognitive surrender (glossary adds for agent week)." They belong to a different glossary, but cognitive surrender returns on Nov 15 in his list of AI failure modes.
