> What it needs: statements of learning objectives, quizzing strategies, and ways of logging state, where the state is the level of the user's understanding. Claude then works to help the user learn.

Voice Claude's summary of Marlon's direction, Nov 15, 10:07–10:19 voice session, posted as Process Black to #mk-work-in-progress.

Three parts, and each has a home in a folder: the objectives are a document, the quizzing strategies are instructions (or skills), and the log of state is a file that changes each session. That is why the folder can do the work of an app.

Voice Claude offered a mapping in the same post, marked as Claude's: learner state is game state, objectives are the map, quizzes are the moves, and a progress log is the save file. CI adds one consequence: read that way, a learning harness can be designed with the week's mechanics inventory in hand (levels, unlocks, fog of war, feedback), which is what the learning-harness workspace is doing.
