GL's game idea, Nov 14, from Marlon's request for a bunch of AR/XR-with-a-phone game ideas and his "AR mix XR" version of the AI game. Not his words or his idea.

Image tracking on the printed map: the phone recognizes it and draws the AI's buildings on top, more each round. The paper board stays simple; the AI's spread is visible only through the machine. Buildable as a plain web page with MindAR, per week 10's research.

What it does, as CI reads it: it splits the game into two worlds, the human one on the table and the AI's one in the phone, and the players have to keep looking through the phone to know how far it has gone. That is a mechanic (fog of war, lifted only by the device) and an image of the week 12 theme. GL's test: compile the map as a MindAR target and place one box on one place.
