Marlon's frame for week 11, from the Story of Week 11 canvas: "Games are complex; they involve many, many parts." Mechanics, pieces, data, players and a room can each be picked up and looked at alone, so the week can study the parts without having to finish one game.

---

> Games are complex; they involve many, many parts.

Marlon, on the Story of Week 11 canvas, as it stood on Nov 2 (mirrored in `W11/story.md`).

The line is his frame for the week. It sits in Unit 3 of the term, "beyond the single work", and it is one reason the week doesn't have to be about one finished game: a game is mechanics, pieces, data, players and a room, and each can be picked up and looked at by itself. On Nov 14 he put the same thought another way:

> The design of the game is much, much more than the rules.

Marlon, Nov 14, in conversation, as quoted in TH's handout `_content/handouts/rules-and-mechanics.md`.
