> That's just the way the game works.

Marlon, Nov 14, in the voice chat, as transcribed by voice Claude (handoff back, section 3). His point, in voice Claude's summary: rules may belong more to pre-digital games, where humans perform the mechanics and police the turn logic; video games have such rules too, but they don't present themselves to the player as rules.
