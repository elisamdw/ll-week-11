William Stafford's course is SOC-STD 98to, *Technologies, Operations, and Infrastructures*. His students come to the Bok Center on Monday, November 23, 12:45 to 14:45, to play an AI strategy game the LL is building, take it apart and remix it. Everything the fellows test this week should help get it ready.

---

> something that's a cross between Universal Paperclips and Civilization

Marlon, Nov 13, dictated.

> We're providing him with a cool game he can take with him.

Marlon, Nov 11, recorded in `W11/builds.md`.

**Who it is for.** About eight students in a course on infrastructure and logistics. William opens the workshop, leads the debrief and keeps the game afterwards.

**What exists.** The game runs at `/game` in four versions. The proposed run of show goes from *Knock over the network* to *Play Data Center* to *What the mechanic argues*. The students' own September 28 index cards become the map. None of this is agreed with William yet.

**What the fellows could do this week.** From the voice agent's record of Marlon's direction this morning, a summary rather than a transcript: "Key thing: the fellows help MK get ready for Stafford." That means:

- playing the game and *Play Data Center* as dry runs;
- labelling each station's readiness;
- saying what a player needs to see of the game's state.
