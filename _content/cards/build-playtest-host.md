**What it is.** LU's plan for the LLUFs, the LL's undergraduate fellows, this week: a ninety-minute studio block, to place inside whatever shifts staff confirm. No LLUF hours appear in the week 11 timeline, and no fellow is assigned; the block is not booked. This card starts a `follows` chain.

**How it runs (LU's proposal).**

- 0 to 5 min: confirm the time, the kit, the current build and which prototype.
- 5 to 30: [Playtest one change, report the evidence](playtest-one-change.md).
- 30 to 60: [The board and the log](the-board-and-the-log.md), or the paper market with prices on a phone, or the gesture-versus-key comparison (which needs 35 minutes).
- 60 to 85: [Host it, reset it, hand it over](host-reset-handover.md).
- 85 to 90: mark what was tested, what is still broken, and the next proposed action, and give AL a three-line handoff: the route, the evidence, what needs a decision.

The outputs: a setup and reset sheet, one observed playtest, a chosen input with a fallback, and a readiness label. A shorter shift can run the playtest card alone.

**What it does for learning.** The fellows move between player, designer and host. They compare a mechanic's intended effect with what a player did (MDA) and attend to story, aesthetics and technology as well as mechanics (Schell). LU's caution is the point of the block: calling a station ready because code exists, or trying all three build options at once, is the failure to avoid.
