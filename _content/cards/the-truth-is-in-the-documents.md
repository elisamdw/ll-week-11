> *Idea: harness and agent engineering* (activity window ~10:50–11:05, Sun Nov 15)
> • We need to get back to thinking about harness and agent engineering.
> • And where the truth, or the persistent elements, lie.
> • The metaphor of the truth being in the documents is something it's easy to drift away from, but essential that we maintain.

Voice Claude's summary of Marlon's words, Sunday, Nov 15, posted as Process Black to #mk-work-in-progress. Ten additions follow in its thread.

The principle has been his for a while: in The Notebook's own doctrine, Markdown is true only in repos, and canvases and chat are intake. On Sunday he restated it as an engineering question for harnesses and agents. An agent's context is temporary; what lasts is what was written down where the next agent will read it. A learning harness that remembers you does so because some file or table holds what it knows.

"Easy to drift away from," as CI reads it, names the pull of a working session: decisions made in a chat, state held in a running process, a summary that replaces its source. The cards in this folder are one attempt to stay on the right side of it.
