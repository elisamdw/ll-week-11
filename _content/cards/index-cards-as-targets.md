GL's game idea, Nov 14, from Marlon's request for a bunch of AR/XR-with-a-phone game ideas. Not his words or his idea.

The AR twin of [Index cards that answer](index-cards-that-answer.md): instead of an NFC sticker, the card's own face is the target, and the phone shows that place's stats and owner over it. MindAR compiles targets in the browser; plain line-art cards track badly, so each card would need a printed pattern added.

Marlon set the limit and the opening on Nov 14: tracking one image at a time "isn't a huge limitation" (voice Claude's summary, [A phone reveals a card's secret](a-phone-reveals-a-cards-secret.md)). One card at a time suits an index card, as CI reads it: a student holds up the card they wrote in September and sees what the game has done to it. GL's test: compile three cards and try multi-tracking.
