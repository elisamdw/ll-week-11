GL's game idea, Nov 14, from Marlon's request for a bunch of AR/XR-with-a-phone game ideas. Not his words or his idea.

Susan Leigh Star's point that infrastructure is what nobody notices until it breaks, made visible: through the phone, power lines light up, the river drops as the AI cools its servers, heat gathers around the data centers. GL cites Star's "The Ethnography of Infrastructure" and Mél Hogan on data centers and water.

Why it fits William's course, as CI reads it: *Technologies, Operations, and Infrastructures* is about exactly these hidden flows, and the Stafford workspace drafted [The cloud has a watershed](../activities/stafford/the-cloud-has-a-watershed.md) on the same ground. GL's test is small: one animated line between two places on the tracked map.
