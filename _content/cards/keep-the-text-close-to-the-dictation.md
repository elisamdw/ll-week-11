Voice Claude's summary of Marlon's words, Sunday, Nov 15, about 11:22, posted as Process Black to #mk-work-in-progress.

"Two sides of the game thing": as CI reads it, "this week and next week" are games (week 11) and autonomy (week 12), and he decided they are one subject seen from two sides. On one side, a person's agency, heightened. On the other, things running on their own and drifting. This card is the first side in his own work: his ideas, dictated, then multiplied by agents into far more text than he spoke, until his voice is a small share of what Claude reads and the work drifts toward Claude's.

These cards are an instance of the puzzle, as CI sees it. Each one leads with his words and labels the rest, so that a reader, Claude included, can tell how much of the card is his. The `voice:` field is a way of keeping the proportion visible.

Possible measures, CI's proposal: the share of a context or a folder that is his words; how far a summary has moved from its dictation; a check that every claim in a card traces to a quote.
