> Then definitely definitely big big history of AI and games. Games about AI, AI use in game dev and game play, etc.

Marlon, Nov 14, 10:40, opening the game workspace (`_content/marlon/20261114-game-week-opening.md`).

HI wrote it that day in `_content/history/`, five strands from 1912 to 2026: games as AI's proving ground (from Turing's hand-played chess to AlphaGo, *Diplomacy* and language models playing Pokémon), AI inside games (the ghosts of *Pac-Man* to LLM characters), games about AI (*WarGames*, GLaDOS, *Universal Paperclips*), AI in game development, and AI in learning games.

The doubled "definitely" and "big big" mark it as one of his priorities that morning. What it gives the week, as CI reads it: the first strand shows that games have always been how AI is tested, which makes the Stafford game a small instance of an old practice, and the third shows how much of the culture's picture of AI comes from games.
