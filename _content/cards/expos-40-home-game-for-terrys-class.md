The Terry app is `ll-26-27/expos40`, built Nov 9: a keyboard sound board and a 1920x1080 stage that one operator runs from the booth for Terry Gipson's game show "Are You Serious?!" in EXPOS 40 (Public Speaking Practicum). Countdown, title, buzzer, theme music, a strike X, quote cards (HG's account, `W11/home-game/README.md`). Terry does the teaching; the LL provides the room and the operator.

The home game turns the booth into a box, as CI reads it: something Terry and his students can run without the LL's operator or the studio. That changes the design. The booth version assumes an operator who knows the keys and a switcher; a home version needs a screen anyone can run, perhaps on a laptop at the front of a classroom.

What waits on Marlon (HG's questions): a `/home` route in `expos40` or a new repo; where students would open it (a deploy); whether to ask Terry for the game's rules, which HG found written down nowhere.
