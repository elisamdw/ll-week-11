A print view for cards that will be cut apart has to plan for the scissors. If the gap between two cards equals the page margin, each card ends up with half a margin after the cut. CL's fix (13:58): every card gets the same 0.45-inch margin on all four sides, so the gap between two cards is twice that, and the sheet adds no margin of its own. CV added a letter-size option (`/cards/print?paper=letter`, two 4.25-inch columns) under the same rule.

The note keeps a printed card a physical object: whatever reads well on screen also has to survive the scissors.
