> then in the other workspace we want to work on prototypes of game mechanics---I don't think I want one single game for right now. Instead I want some playable examples of things we MIGHT want to include in the game.

Marlon, Nov 15, 07:52, starting the day's workspaces (`W11/days/20261115/README.md`).

A change of approach from the day before. On Nov 14 the workspace built one game at `/game` with three versions and an editor; after the voice chat the editor was out. On Sunday morning he asked for the opposite of one finished game: many small playable pieces, each showing one thing the game might do. By 08:39 the prototypes workspace had seventeen at `/proto`, in three groups (economy and the AI as a system; information and players; the body, the room and hybrid play).

What it does, as CI reads it: it keeps the design open for the students. A finished game invites play; a set of parts invites choosing and combining, which is what Marlon wants them to do with Claude Code. "MIGHT" in capitals is the instruction to keep it that way.

*CI's reading, Nov 15. Not Marlon's words unless quoted.*
