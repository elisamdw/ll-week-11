GL's game idea, Nov 14, from Marlon's request for a bunch of NFC game ideas. Not his words or his idea.

Identity tokens: tap your badge, then a place, and the game knows who did what. The table stays public; the factions' goals stay private, each on its own player's phone. GL ties it to Nguyen on value capture (games that hand you a metric to chase) and to the hidden-role mechanic.

What it joins, as CI reads it: PI's prototype [Private goals on your phone](private-goals-on-your-phone.md) without the badge, and PB's [Every tap is a row](every-tap-is-a-row.md), which logs taps without knowing whose. The badge gives the log a name column. GL's test: four badges, one reader, and a page that shows each faction's goal only after its badge is tapped.
