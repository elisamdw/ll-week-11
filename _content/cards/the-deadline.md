**What it is.** Negotiation on phones joined to one room code, built by PI on November 15 at `/proto/players/deadline`. It adds trading to the faction phones of [Private goals on your phone](private-goals-on-your-phone.md), and puts an AI buyer in the market.

**How it plays.** Every phone holds a handful of tokens (Chips, Power, Water, Land) and a private need, a set of five such as "2 Chips + 2 Power + 1 Water"; the deal guarantees every set can be made by trading. For two minutes players talk across the table and send offers phone to phone ("1 Water for 1 Land"), accepted or declined. Meanwhile the AI will buy any token for coins, and its price rises every thirty seconds. Coins are points, so selling a spare is free money; but every token the AI buys leaves the table for good, and someone may have needed it. At the deadline a complete set is worth 6 points and each coin 1, and the table screen shows who was short and whether the AI was holding what they lacked. Three to six players.

**What it does for learning.** Trading under a clock everyone can see, with hidden needs you learn only from what others ask for. PI's reading: the AI does one thing, buy resources, by a written rule, and still changes the game. Resource acquisition is one of the instrumental goals in Bostrom's "The Superintelligent Will" that almost any final goal calls for; the table meets it as a buyer whose offer is sensible for each player to take and costly for all, and more tempting as time runs out. The end screen's "the AI has it" column shows where one player's sale cost another their set. Costikyan's essay on decisions and resources is the design reading.

**Which of Marlon's ideas it serves.** No words of Marlon's are on record about it. CM's reading: it is a trading phase for the AI game's factions, played on the phones that hold their goals, and TD notes it as a ready-made game for [Claude takes a seat](claude-takes-a-seat.md) if Wednesday's swap happens. It pairs with [Prices that move](prices-that-move.md), where the AI's demand sets the price instead of a clock.

**Needs:** a laptop running the reader app (it holds the room and the clock), a projector, three to six phones on its network. The AI buyer is a written rule, not a model; the needs are hidden by the pages, not encrypted.
