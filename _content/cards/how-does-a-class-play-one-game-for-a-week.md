Marlon's contingency for Nov 23: "It could be that we just create it all as an online async experience for the students." Async multiplayer has a long record: play-by-mail Diplomacy, Freeciv's LongTurn, phone games with turn timers. What setups have kept a group playing one game over a week, and what made players drop?

---

**Why the week needs it.** [The Stafford game as an async online experience](the-stafford-game-as-an-async-online-experience.md) and [A round a day](a-round-a-day.md) are the async plans; RS left Polytopia's async timers and Unciv's multiplayer unconfirmed. A class game that loses half its players by Wednesday fails William.

**What would answer it.** Three or four async formats with their turn length, how missed turns are handled (timeouts, AI takeover, skipping), group sizes, and what sources say about drop-off; anything on async games in courses.

**Where to look.** Freeciv LongTurn's pages, Polytopia's and Unciv's multiplayer docs, play-by-email Diplomacy hosts (webDiplomacy, Backstabbr), studies of async games in teaching.
