> On Sept 28 the students sketched databases of the course on index cards, and some groups ended up with a database of infrastructure itself. I photographed the cards. Before Nov 23 I'll build them out into a working database.

Marlon's proposal for William Stafford, Nov 3.

The build under [Design a game on the class's own database](design-a-game-on-the-classs-own-database.md), and Marlon's own task. It is the same move as the week's photo-to-game-state work run on different material, as CI reads it: photographs of paper become structured data. The decisions are the same too: what is a record, what is a field, what links to what. The students' categories should survive the move; the database is theirs, made usable.

*CI's reading, Nov 15. Not Marlon's words unless quoted.*
