GL's game idea, Nov 14, from Marlon's request for a bunch of AR/XR-with-a-phone game ideas. Not his words or his idea.

Hand tracking in the browser (MediaPipe, which the LL already uses in its web prototypes and which week 10's LLUFs used for instruments and launchers) reads gestures from a phone or laptop camera over the table. The body does the mechanic.

What it raises, as CI reads it: a gesture is a public move. Everyone at the table sees you reach for a place, unlike a tap on your own phone, so it suits actions that should be seen (protest, regulation) and not secret ones. The LLUFs' [Gesture versus key](../activities/llufs/gesture-versus-key.md) compares the two. GL's test: a laptop camera over the board and one gesture mapped to one action.
