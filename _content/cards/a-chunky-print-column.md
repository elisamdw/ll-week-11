> for now it can be cards. the print size will be 5.5" wide (a column of 11x17 vertically, with varying heights depending on how much text--headlines can be 16 or so and then body 10pt for a start).

Marlon, Nov 15, shortly before 13:39 and at 13:39 (`W11/days/20261115/cards/shared.md`). "900 pt" reads as weight 900, the heaviest Inter, given the 16-point headline in the second message.

Two lives for each card, as CI reads it: on screen, where it can be moved, linked and graphed, and on paper, where it can be cut, pinned and shuffled by hand. A 5.5-inch column is half the short side of an 11x17 sheet, so two cards stand side by side and each takes the height its text needs. CV is building the print view at `/cards/print`.
