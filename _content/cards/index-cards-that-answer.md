On Nov 14, in the voice chat, Marlon brought William's students' cards into the game. In voice Claude's summary (handoff back, section 4), he mentioned involving

> all of the cards that they wrote or their database

meaning the index cards from the September 28 workshop.

Voice Claude suggested NFC stickers on those cards in the same handoff (section 6, marked as not Marlon's), and GL made it a game idea: each card is a template, the place as the students defined it; a tap shows the instance, who holds it now, what it produces, whether it's down. The physical card stays the same while its game state changes.

It brings three of the week's threads onto one card, as CI reads it: the students' own database ([Design a game on the class's own database](design-a-game-on-the-classs-own-database.md)), template and instance ([Games persist state as data](games-persist-state-as-data.md)), and NFC on a simple tabletop ([NFC for simple tabletop games](nfc-for-simple-tabletop-games.md)). GL's companion idea uses the same cards as AR image targets instead of tags.

What it would take, as GL sized it: five copies of the cards with stickers, and a phone page that maps tag ids to places in the game. Web NFC doesn't run on iPhone, so the reader is an Android phone or the USB reader.
