> Next week we should also do things related to active learning and the building out of tooling and supports and props for active learning and design of active learning.

Marlon, Nov 13, dictated (`W11/marlon/20261113-games-and-interaction.md`). The builds list counts it as build 3, with AIBL Connect, and records it as "not yet defined" with no pane.

Three nouns, three kinds of thing, as CI reads them. Tooling: software a teacher or student uses during an activity (a timer, a randomizer, a capture station). Supports: the scaffolds around it (instructions, examples, a handout). Props: physical objects in the room (cards, tokens, a board, a printed poster). And two objects of the work: active learning itself, and the design of it, so tools for the people planning a session as well as for the people in it.

The week's games supply a lot of it already: a deck of mechanic cards is a prop, a tap station is a tool, a remix example is a support. What would make it its own build is naming a few of these as general-purpose, for faculty outside games week.
