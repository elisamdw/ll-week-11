> Something that's a cross between Universal Paperclips and Civilization or something like that.

Marlon, Nov 13, dictated, right after naming the AI strategy game for the Stafford workshop.

> An AI that wants to turn everything into data centers and use up the world's energy, with humans fighting back (something between *Paperclips* and *Civilization*), would be a lot of fun.

Marlon's proposal for William Stafford, Nov 3.

The two games pull in different directions, which is why the cross is interesting. *Universal Paperclips* (Frank Lantz, 2017) is one player and one number: you are the AI, and the game is the experience of a goal swallowing the world. *Civilization* is many players on a map, each expanding across territory and resources. Crossing them puts the paperclip logic on a map and gives it opponents: the AI expands like a civilization, and the humans are the other civilizations.

It connects to the Nov 14 game at `/game`, whose *Data Center* version already has the AI spreading across a map of places, and to [The paperclip loop](paperclip-loop.md), PE's prototype of the single runaway goal. By Nov 15 Marlon had moved from "something like that" to the genre itself ([The 4X genre for Stafford](the-4x-genre-for-stafford.md)).
