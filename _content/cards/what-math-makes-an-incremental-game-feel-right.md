The LL's own [paperclip loop](paperclip-loop.md) is written from scratch, so its numbers are ours to choose. Idle-game designers have written about the math: costs that grow by a fixed multiplier, production that grows more slowly, resets that multiply. What do they recommend, and how does *Universal Paperclips* pace its stages?

---

**Why the week needs it.** [Incremental growth](incremental-growth.md) and [Automate this](automate-this.md) are mechanics in the week's games; students who remix the loop on Wednesday will change its numbers. HI's save-file reading (`_content/game-state/paperclips.md`) lists Paperclips' values but not the curves.

**What would answer it.** The standard cost and production formulas with one worked example; what the sources say makes the pacing feel right or wrong; how long Paperclips' three stages take and what triggers each, from a source.

**Where to look.** Anthony Pecorella's "The Math of Idle Games" on Kongregate's developer blog (2016), the Universal Paperclips wiki, interviews with Frank Lantz (Wired, 2017), Fizek's work on idle games.
