> Zeroing in on the *4X genre* (Civilization's genre) as the game for William Stafford's workshop. Be upfront about genre and think critically about it.

Voice Claude's summary of Marlon's direction in his voice session of Sunday, Nov 15, 10:07–10:19, posted as Process Black to #mk-work-in-progress. Not his verbatim words.

Two days earlier the game was "something that's a cross between Universal Paperclips and Civilization or something like that." On Sunday morning the Civilization half became the frame: the genre itself, named as a genre. The 4X label (explore, expand, exploit, exterminate) comes from game journalism of the early 1990s and covers *Civilization*, *Master of Orion*, *Stellaris* and their kin.

"Be upfront about genre" is a teaching move as much as a design choice. The students are told the game is a 4X before they play it, so the conventions (the map, the tech tree, the growth from one city to many) are visible as conventions and open to question. The same session goes on to the critique ([An anti-colonial 4X](an-anti-colonial-4x.md)), the players ([The firms are the players](the-firms-are-the-players.md)) and the argument the genre makes ([4X trains a mindset](4x-trains-a-mindset.md)).

What it would take: a short, plain introduction to the genre for students who may never have played one (two or three screenshots, the four Xs), and a base game that has all four Xs in small, so that each can be changed.

*CI's reading, Nov 15. Not Marlon's words unless quoted.*
