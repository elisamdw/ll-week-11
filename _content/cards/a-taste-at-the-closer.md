**What it is.** The only piece of the learning harness inside Wednesday's class: an eight-minute demonstration at the closer, [A mechanic for your Final Project](a-mechanic-for-your-final-project.md), where TD suggested it. Its content is HB's proposal, not yet agreed.

**How it runs.** Three minutes on the folder: on the projector, a harness folder in Claude Desktop's Code tab; ask Claude one practice question, answer it, and open `graph/attempts.json` to show the answer in the file. Three minutes on the game: [The web learning game](the-web-learning-game.md) on the same folder, a node unlocking, the progress bar: "Everything on this map came from that conversation." Two minutes on the take-home: over Thanksgiving, set up your own folder, learn one thing your Final Project needs, play it as a game, try one remix, and show your map on December 2.

**What it does for learning.** The closer asks students to take one mechanic into their Final Project. HB's reasoning for placing it there: unlocking by explaining is a mechanic, and the harness is a tool students can use for their project's research. It also shows, in one file, that an AI tutor's memory can be something the student owns and can read.

**Which of Marlon's ideas it serves.** His answer to HL's question about which session the student setup was for, given before 13:09 on November 15:

> Nov 18 taste + take-home

Marlon, Nov 15, on HL's board (`_context/orchestration/20261115-learning/board.md`). TD records it as items 12 and 13 of the class's `plan-changes`.

**Open for Marlon:** the Code tab needs a paid Claude plan (Anthropic's desktop quickstart, read by HD on November 15) and the syllabus promises free tiers, so how students get access is his call; so is whether the take-home is required.
