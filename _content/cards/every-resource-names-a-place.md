SW's change to Data Center, after Crawford's Atlas of AI: the pooled Energy, Water and Money become stacks of tokens on named water sources, power sources and labor pools. Each data center draws from the places it is connected to, and when one runs dry the people there feel it first. Where Prices that move asks what changing prices do to play, this asks who pays.

---

**What it is.** A change to the AI game proposed by SW, after TH's reading of Crawford. The *Data Center* ruleset has three pooled resources, Energy, Water and Money. SW's point: pooled resources are Bostrom's view, matter is matter. This mechanic replaces the pool with draws from places.

**How it plays.** Each data center the AI places must connect to a named water source, a named power source and a named labor pool on the map, and running it drains those places. When one runs dry, the people there feel it first, on the present-harms track. On paper: give each place a stack of five tokens; the AI takes from specific places, never from a bank, and empty places flip face down. Keep it to three or four kinds of place, and let a screen do the counting if bookkeeping slows play.

**What it does for learning.** Crawford's point in *Atlas of AI*, played: resources are always somewhere and someone's. SW adds Mukherjee's question for strategy games, whose world is being converted into resources and on whose terms, as a question the map answers. In AL's chain across audiences, this is where William's room picks up [Prices that move](prices-that-move.md): Wednesday's class asks what moving prices do to play, and William's asks who pays them. Prices are one way to make a cost land on someone; named places are the other, and a table could try both. [Leaks](leaks.md) is the same argument from the company's side.

**Which of Marlon's ideas it serves.** No words of Marlon's are on record about it. CM's reading: it answers his "GPU prices or land prices" (November 14) with places instead of prices, and it is built for a course on infrastructure whose map can be the students' own cards ([Cards become the map](cards-become-the-map.md)). It is one of the items on the menu in [Remix one mechanic](remix-one-mechanic.md).
