TDM 155AI, *Making Stuff in the Age of AI*, is Marlon's course. Its class on Wednesday, November 18, runs from 12:00 to 14:45. Students play a board game and take it apart, see its state as data, play the LL's AI game, remix a mechanic with Claude Code, and bring one mechanic back to their Final Projects, due as works in progress on December 2.

---

> In my class, at least, and maybe in Stafford's class, we should actually just go from a board game to how you would represent the data of that board game as a game state

> The UI is Claude code itself.

Marlon, Nov 14, in a voice chat with Claude, as transcribed in the handoff back to Claude Code.

**How it runs.** This is *Session A*, which Marlon chose on Nov 15. It goes from *Change one card* to *Take apart a board game*, then a photo of the board turned into game state, then *Play two theories of AI*, *Remix a mechanic with Claude Code*, and *A mechanic for your Final Project*. If the board games don't arrive, *Claude takes a seat* replaces the first half.

**What the fellows could do this week.** From the voice agent's record of Marlon's direction, a summary rather than a transcript: "At least test out all the little mechanics MK might want to try in his class." That means:

- running *Your five most-played games*;
- trying *Guess the category* as a warm-up;
- labelling each station's readiness.
