At 06:24 the record pairs the chart with "cmux commands for multiagent orchestration."

**What the run needs, from his words.** A large body of work that needs doing; complexity that a loop can't handle; Claude managing agents; something the chart can show leveling up. His candidates: the old HackMDs, the old transcripts, video editing, game design.

**KB's candidate: the unplayed mechanics.** On Nov 16 the seventeen prototypes and the AI game together play 63 of the inventory's 105 mechanics; 42 have no playable form (`where-to-play-each-mechanic`). A run could give each mechanic family to one pane: read the mechanic's row and readings, design a one-mechanic prototype in the house style of `/proto`, build it, play it headless, write its card, and hand it to a reviewer pane. That is design and judgment at every step, the opposite of a loop; it fills a gap the week has; and its output is playable by the LLUFs and the class. It would be large: on the order of ten panes for several hours.

**What it would take.** A plan first, by Marlon's rules (a board, one brief per pane, a JSON plan, nothing launched until he says so); the token chart running beside it; and his go on the cost, which `ll-tokens` can estimate from a small pilot of one family.

**What Marlon would decide.** Whether to run it this week at all; which body of work (his four candidates or KB's); and how large.
