> *AI to build your own learning environment / teach yourself:* MK has posted examples from the last month; many are harness-like tools built for learning. He doesn't want to advocate signing up for or locking into a third-party tool by an indie developer: too much risk. Prefers helping people think about how they'd construct something themselves: a full-stack app or harness, or just Claude Code in the Claude Desktop app, with no new front end and no DevOps (always-on databases, always-on agents). "There's probably a lot that people can do just with documents and Claude Code."

Voice Claude's summary of Marlon's direction, Nov 15, 10:07–10:19 voice session, posted as Process Black to #mk-work-in-progress. The last sentence, in quotes, is his.

> I'd like for fellows/staff/students to make their own thing or tweak this instead of just using vanilla app

Marlon, Nov 15, 13:51, in HL's pane, on LearnHarness (recorded on the learning-harness board).

The stance is about who owns the tool. An indie learning app may vanish, change its terms or charge; a folder of documents and a model you already use belongs to you. So the LL's job is to teach construction, not adoption: what the pieces are, and how few of them you need. LearnHarness itself is AGPL-3.0, so forking and changing it is allowed, with the condition that changed versions served to others share their source (HL's reading of the licence).

*CI's reading, Nov 15. Not Marlon's words unless quoted.*
