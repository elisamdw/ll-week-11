"Another moment" and "go back" say the LL has done this before: TDM 98's game-mechanics page and 40 challenge cards (Nov 5) and the ENGL 189VG station work are on record (builds list, build 1). The new inventory is larger: 105 mechanics in `_content/mechanics/mechanics.csv`, each linked to readings, as of Nov 14, with an 11x17 poster print of the first 50 test-rendered in the class repo.

"Throughout the space" makes the studio the display, as CI reads it: a mechanic on the wall by the station where it can be tried (a tap mechanic by the NFC reader, hidden information by the card table). Printed and placed, the inventory becomes a map of the room.

These cards are a second instance of the same wish: Marlon asked for them in a print view, a 5.5-inch column on 11x17.
