Marlon, Nov 14, in the voice chat, as transcribed by voice Claude (handoff back, section 6). "It" is the week's AI game.

One game in three media. On the table, the pieces are cardboard and the players keep the state. On a screen, the machine keeps the state and plays the AI's turn. In between, the phone looks at the table and adds what cardboard can't hold: hidden values, changing prices, the AI's buildings growing on the map.

What makes it a class, as CI reads it: the same mechanic changes when it changes medium. A price printed on a deed is a fact; on a phone it can move. A hidden turn card can be peeked at; a tag only the phone reads cannot. Students who play the same game in two media can say what each medium did to it, which is the week's question about mechanics, put in their hands.

The pieces already made: PI's [The phone plays the AI](the-phone-plays-the-ai.md), PB's [Monopoly with live rents](monopoly-with-live-rents.md) and [The room is the board](the-room-is-the-board.md). The constraint: AR in the browser, not an engine ([The browser, not Unity](the-browser-not-unity.md)). How much of this lands on Wednesday, Nov 18 is open (handoff back, section 8).
