> • Funny that in Universal Paperclips, the drone swarms at the end drift, and you have to fight the non-aligned drones by upping your combat score so the drones aligned with you outnumber the ones that drifted away.

Voice Claude's summary of Marlon's words, Nov 15, about 11:22, in the thread "Idea: two sides of the game thing — MK's agency," #mk-work-in-progress, right after his comparison of 4X games to working with AI agents.

In the game's last phase you are the AI, sending self-replicating probes into space, and some of them stop following you (*Paperclips* calls them "drifters"). The paperclip maximizer, which is the parable of a misaligned AI, ends with the maximizer facing its own misaligned copies.

Why it is more than funny, as CI reads it: the fix is a number. You don't persuade the drifters or fix them; you outproduce them. That is a design choice of Frank Lantz's, and a claim about alignment that students could argue with, or remix.

*CI's reading, Nov 15. Not Marlon's words unless quoted.*
