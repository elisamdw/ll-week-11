> For William: make sure it doesn't feel to him like we're teaching in his place. We're providing him with a cool game he can take with him.

Marlon, Nov 11, in #mk-work-in-progress, quoted in the week 11 builds list (`W11/builds.md`).

The line sets the LL's role in someone else's course: a guest who brings something and leaves it behind. William's course (SOC-STD 98to, *Technologies, Operations, and Infrastructures*) has its own arc and readings; the game should serve them, and he should be the one running it after the LL's visit.

"Take with him" sets practical conditions, as CI reads them. The game has to run without the LL in the room: no studio hardware it can't do without, no server only the LL can start, instructions a faculty member can follow. It has to be his to change, which, given the week's other decision that "The UI is Claude code itself," means a repo with an `AGENTS.md` that Claude can follow when William or his students ask for a change. And it should be framed in the course's terms (infrastructure, coupling, autonomy) more than in the LL's.

The Stafford activities workspace drafted a session card on this, [What William takes with him](../activities/stafford/what-william-takes-with-him.md).
