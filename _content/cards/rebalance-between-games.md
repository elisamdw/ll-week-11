> try playing it with a new set of constraints

Marlon, Nov 14, in the voice chat, as transcribed by voice Claude (handoff back, section 6). He was saying that phone or AR components in place of printed values allow "balancing the game on the fly": in Monopoly the prices are printed on the cards, but on a phone they could be changed either as a one-time rebalance (this card) or dynamically, "as in the real world, because prices change in response to demand" (CM's [Prices that move](prices-that-move.md) and [Monopoly with live rents](monopoly-with-live-rents.md)).

Voice Claude separated the two in the handoff, and CI keeps the separation: a one-time rebalance is the designer's move, playtesting; prices that respond to demand are the game's own behavior, a small economy. This card is the designer's move.

It is the cheapest design exercise the week has, as CI sees it. The board stays the same, one number changes, and the class plays again and says what changed in how people played. It needs no code beyond a page that shows the values, and it teaches that a game's balance is a choice. The LLUFs' [Playtest one change](../activities/llufs/playtest-one-change.md) is this, as an activity.
