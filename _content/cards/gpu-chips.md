> For our AI game, these could be things like GPU prices or land prices or whatever.

Marlon, Nov 14, in the voice chat, on the values a phone could hold in place of printed ones, as transcribed by voice Claude (handoff back, section 6).

The GPU had come up in his notes earlier in the week. In the Nov 11 HILT dictation, voice Claude's summary: "The joke: right now it seems like everything is scarce because of AI. There definitely aren't enough GPUs out there, as anyone who's tried to buy a PC recently knows."

GL's game idea from Nov 14 makes the GPU a thing on the table: tagged tokens, a reader labeled "the AI," and a price on screen that rises with each chip sold, like the toys-to-life figures (amiibo, Skylanders) that a game reads when you set them down. Voice Claude's note in the handoff names the mechanic underneath: when the AI buys GPUs and power, prices rise for the human factions, a mechanic that makes the argument by being played.

With the Nov 15 decision that the players are firms (NVIDIA among them), the chip becomes the contested resource of the 4X, as CI reads it: the thing one firm sells and the others need.
