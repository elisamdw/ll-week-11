Read first: `_context/orchestration/20261114/voice-handoff-back.md` (Marlon's hour in voice mode, saved verbatim), sections 1, 3, and 7.

**Marlon, Nov 14 (voice chat):** the rules-versus-mechanics point "seems like an important thing to say, and maybe we have that in a handout or something like that," and "we should have a little glossary now." He thinks in **mechanics and game elements**, from Schell's *The Art of Game Design*, not rules: "the design of the game is much, much more than the rules." His reading of Bogost: procedural rhetoric is about "the sort of mechanics and actions people can take rather than the rules," because "it's through the play that the argument is made." Rules may belong more to pre-digital games, where humans perform the mechanics; in video games "That's just the way the game works."
