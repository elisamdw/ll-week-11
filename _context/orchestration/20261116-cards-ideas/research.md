## Could not confirm

The items that matter most for this week, one line each; the full lists are at the foot of each note.

- **1, NFC:** whether an iPhone gives a second notification when the same sticker is tapped twice, and whether a LAN address (`http://<ip>:3111/...`) opens from a sticker. Both need one test on a real iPhone before stickers are written. Also unconfirmed: what Android 15 and earlier do with a URL tag; whether Harvard's Wi-Fi lets phones reach a laptop.
- **2, language models:** no study found of people tricking a model player at a board game; CICERO's *Science* paper would not open (figures from Meta's pages); Kaggle's own pages would not render.
- **3, photo:** no benchmark of current models on real photos of Monopoly, Risk, Life or a card table; nothing on face-down cards.
- **5, role-plays:** the AI 2027 exercise's mechanics come from event listings and press, not from the project; no account of one of these games inside a single class period; the 2024 paper's "left to the roll of dice" was not re-opened by KR.
- **6, numbers:** Epoch AI's megawatts are estimates and some rows are projections; Meta gives two figures for Hyperion; xAI's and OpenAI's own pages refused automated access.
- **4, debrief:** Lederman's phase names and Thiagarajan's six questions seen only second-hand; the Army's own after-action review text did not open; Cheng et al. 2014 compares simulation-plus-debrief against nothing, so it doesn't isolate the debrief (Savoldelli 2006 does).
- **7, persuasion:** Sicart's article quoted second-hand (gamestudies.org refused connections); sample sizes for Peng 2010 and Peña 2018 not in the abstracts opened; no meta-analysis of persuasive or social-issue games alone.
- **8, designing games:** Kafai's 1995 numbers come from Kafai and Burke's summary; no meta-analysis gives designing games its own effect size; no study on remixing with an AI coding agent or designing from a class's own card database.
- **10, async:** longturn.net blocks bots (its rules read from its GitHub templates); Polytopia's timer rules come from developer posts of about 2022 and may be out of date; no peer-reviewed study of drop-out in async strategy games.
- **11, 4X:** Ford's full article and Mukherjee's "Playing Subaltern" not read in full (abstract and book contents only); Old World, Humankind, Endless Legend and others left out for lack of opened sources; press on *Spirit Island* (The Atlantic, Ars Technica, NYT) blocked.
- **12, incremental math:** the original Kongregate posts are gone (Game Developer reprints read); the Paperclips fan wiki, Wired and The Verge refused fetches, so stage triggers come from the game's own JavaScript; playtime from one player review.
- **13, game languages:** Stanford's GGP pages, the Digital Ludeme Project site, Schaul's VGDL paper and the *Antiquity* article did not open; no 2025-26 paper found of a model writing Stanford GDL.
- **14, accessibility:** Harvard's own policy and accessibility pages refused automated fetches, so the policy wording comes from two *Harvard Gazette* articles (2019, 2023); no page says Harvard has formally adopted WCAG 2.2; whether the Me First kit's USB-C port types keys is not stated.
- **15, badges:** no controlled study of a faculty practice-sharing community; no direct measure of badges crowding out intrinsic motivation; only Sailer and Homner's abstract read.
- **16, AI tutors:** the Harvard tutor's system prompt is in a supplement not opened; the World Bank repository refused access (Nigeria sample size and prompts missing; abstract and blog disagree on the tool and the English effect, 0.23 or 0.24 SD); OpenAI's study-mode evaluation refused access.
- **17, learner models:** Khan Academy's help center refused access (point values and demotion rules unconfirmed); "outer fringe" from Wikipedia, not ALEKS's pages; Birdbrain's model type not named.
- **18, constraints:** the jam study's 30% vs 3% from *Psychology Today*, not the paper (a scan); Kalyuga's definition read through a summary; no direct test that a limited kit hinders experts in creative work; no controlled comparison of constrained and unconstrained game-jam teams.
- **9, computer use:** no Anthropic or OpenAI page on a user resizing mid-task; OpenAI's own pages returned 403 (system card PDF and API guide used instead).
